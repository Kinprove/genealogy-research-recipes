# Find a Civil War patient in the published Medical and Surgical History

Use this recipe when you know a Civil War patient's name, unit, hospital or injury and want to find the episode in the Surgeon General's *Medical and Surgical History of the War of the Rebellion*. Search named injury-table rows even when a patient has no case narrative.

## Choose the volume and search by patient

Find digitised volumes by the work's full title. Search the NLM copy's searchable text or PDF for the surname, and browse the relevant part by the injury or disease when a name search fails. Use Civil War Washington's case index as another entry point and take a found case back to its printed page. [S2 @2026-11-22] [S3 @2026-11-22] [S4 @2026-11-22]

## Check the person and the type of entry

According to the presenter in the available excerpt, read adjacent numbered cases separately and check each patient's initials, company, regiment and wounding event [S1†]. Compare these details with the service record before identifying the patient. Read the patient's service designation in the entry itself. A wound grouping can include patients from different armies. [S5] [S6]

According to the presenter in the available excerpt, use the injury tables as a separate search [S1†]. Read the column headings before extracting the patient’s wound, treating physician or disposition. Keep empty fields open and keep aggregate totals separate from named entries. [S2] [S6]

## Return the treatment lead

Give volume, part, printed page, case or table number, patient identifiers, and the reported treatment and disposition. If the entry names a hospital or unit, use [Civil War service record episodes](us-civil-war-service-record-episodes.md) or [Confederate hospital records](confederate-hospital-record-recovery.md) to look for records of the same patient and episode.
