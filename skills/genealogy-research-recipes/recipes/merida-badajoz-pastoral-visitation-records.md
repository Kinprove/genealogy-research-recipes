# Find a pastoral visitation and parish return in Mérida-Badajoz

Use this recipe to identify a visitor’s or priest’s dated role in Mérida-Badajoz, or to establish parish conditions at a particular date. Choose the fonds by the institution visited and the authority that made the visit.

## Choose the fonds by the institution visited

Identify the parish and its arciprestazgo for the visit year. In the diocesan fonds, start in Gobierno under Visitas pastorales and check the locality and year [S2 @2026-11-22]. For the cathedral chapter search its separate inventory at series 1.2.3.1 [S3 @2026-11-22]. For a place under the Order of Alcántara, check its own Gobierno inventory under Expedientes de visitas pastorales [S4 @2026-11-22]. Keep the fonds and the numbered box or expediente in the locator.

## Extract the named roles and parish return

According to the available article excerpt, check the lists of visitadores when tracing an official’s career [S1†]. In the dossier, distinguish the named visitor from the priest or institution assessed. Record the role, parish and date separately for each mention [S2].

Check the diocesan listing for estadísticas-parroquias as a separate route when a visitation file is silent [S5]. According to the available article excerpt, the forms describe the parish at a particular date [S1†]. Read the date, reporting parish and categories actually filled in. Distinguish a questionnaire prepared before the visit from the record of the visit itself. Record any named clergy separately from parish-level accounts, worship or sacrament counts. Use a named act or register entry for an individual marriage or sacrament [S2].
