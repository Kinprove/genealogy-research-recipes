# Trace a U.S. federal civilian employee from a register to a personnel file

Use this recipe for a U.S. federal civilian employee whose employing office or dates are uncertain.
Use [postmaster appointments](us-postmaster-appointments-and-office-sites.md) for the appointment and
office of a postmaster, and [military service
reconstruction](us-20th-century-military-service-reconstruction.md) for a military personnel file.

## Find the employer in the register

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: third-party-tool --> The employee’s name and any other search fields you enter go to MyHeritage. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) to data about anyone who may be living.

Search the MyHeritage Official Register index when you need a candidate edition and entry
[S1 @2026-11-22]. Check the original edition’s scope using the National Archives guide
[S3 @2026-11-22].

A dated Official Register entry gives a snapshot of employment. Copy the listed position, agency,
bureau, duty station, compensation, birthplace and appointment district where stated. Preserve
residence as its own field. Check adjoining editions to bound changes in employment and compare each
edition’s inclusion rules before interpreting a miss [S1], [S3].

## Route the personnel file or service card

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: request --> The employee’s identifying and employment details and any required eligibility proof go to the personnel records office. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) before requesting a record about anyone who may be living.

According to the presenter, follow a civilian employment lead to an Official Personnel Folder
[S2† @2026-11-22]. For service ending before 1952, look up the agency in the National Archives at St.
Louis archival holdings and verify access for that employment. Use the Archives’ later-record routing
if service continued into 1952 or after. Check for a service record card when a folder is unavailable
[S4 @2026-11-22], [S5 @2026-11-22], [S6 @2026-11-22].

Send a request to the appropriate personnel records office after confirming eligibility and the access
rule. Identify the agency and employment interval and give only the identifying details the office
needs.

Extract dated appointments, promotions, transfers and separation [S4]. According to the presenter, a
folder can supply personal details [S2†]. Record any birth or family detail with the source document
and informant when shown. Return the edition and page for the register entry, the personnel series and
file locator, and the specific gap or access outcome for any missing product.
