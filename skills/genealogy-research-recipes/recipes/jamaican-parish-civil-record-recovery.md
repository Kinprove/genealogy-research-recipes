# Recover Jamaican civil, Anglican and Dissenter records

Use this recipe with the event date, parish and any evidence of the congregation. Keep civil registration, Anglican transcripts and Dissenter marriage books as separate searches. For slavery-era returns and the transition into records of free people, use [British colonial slave registers](british-colonial-slave-registers.md).

## Choose the register by event and date

Use 1878 as the start of the regular civil birth and death series and 1880 for marriages. For a mid-century birth or death, also check whether the short-lived civil registers from 1844–1851 survive for the parish [S4].

Search FamilySearch’s Jamaica, Church of England Parish Register Transcripts, 1664–1880, by event, parish and year. For an earlier vital event, or one missing in the civil search, check the parish volume and any local handwritten index. Use the parish’s actual volume span to judge a miss [S1 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22].

For a possible non-Anglican marriage, search the separately described Dissenter marriage transcripts via FamilySearch catalog locator 766992. Use the Jamaican Parish Registers microfilm list to identify the right volume and its date range. The microfilm list also points to volume 6 among the parish collection’s images. Check the officiant and congregation in the entry [S1] [S3].

## Route a civil certificate request

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: request --> The certificate application sends the subject’s identifying and event details, and any required proof, to Jamaica’s civil registrar. Apply the privacy gate for anyone who may be living.

Direct a Jamaican civil certificate request to the National Identification and Registration Authority (NIRA), formerly the Registrar General’s Department. Identify the district and event first and check the applicable ordering requirements [S1 @2026-11-22] [S4 @2026-11-22] [S5 @2026-11-22].

## Search local collections for context

Use the National Library of Jamaica for newspaper, manuscript and map leads. Also check the University of the West Indies’ Mona Library Special Collections for West Indian newspapers and maps. Record the publication or collection and the item-level locator [S1 @2026-11-22] [S6 @2026-11-22] [S7 @2026-11-22].

Return the event, parish and congregation if known, each series and range searched, and the full register or image locator. Follow a collection-index miss through the [FamilySearch image workflow](familysearch-practical-record-workflows.md) or use [historical newspaper search](historical-newspaper-section-search.md) for notice leads.
