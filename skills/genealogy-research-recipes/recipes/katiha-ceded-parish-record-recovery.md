# Recover ceded Finnish parish records through Katiha

Use this recipe when a family belonged to a parish in territory ceded by Finland and a communion-book page or event entry is needed. One indexed person does not establish who else was on the page.

## Choose the access route

Use the National Archives’ public Katiha form for eligible records from the ceded parishes. Look for the parish and available birth, death, moving and marriage series. Include Petsamo when the parish or event belongs there [S4 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22] [S6 @2026-11-22] [S7 @2026-11-22] [S8 @2026-11-22].

Use the National Archives’ web interface for personal data more than 100 years old. Check the separately available death data, for which the archive gives a 50-year threshold. For restricted material, use Katiha Windows at a National Archives reading room under its access-permission procedure [S5 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22].

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: request --> Send the archive only the identifiers and proof it requires for access to records naming the person who may be living. Apply the privacy gate before making the application.

Apply for restricted Katiha access only for the records needed to answer the family-history question.

## Recover the page and its indexed entries

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: third-party-tool --> A name search sends the subject’s name and any parish, dates or relationship terms entered to the archive’s Katiha service. Apply the privacy gate for anyone who may be living.

Search Katiha by surname, parish and record type. Compare patronymics and titles across burial and communion-book hits. To see who else is indexed on a communion-book page, run Tarkennettu haku with the Sidos (volume) and Sivu (page) fields from the hit. Leave the personal name fields empty and check the matching parish and record type [S1 @2026-11-22] [S3 @2026-11-22].

If the hit omits the volume’s years, find the parish’s communion books in Digihakemisto or the National Archives catalogue. Match the volume identifier before using its year range. Keep the page reference, transcription and original-image access as separate observations [S1] [S7] [S2].

A sparse result leaves another person’s presence and any explicit earlier-page reference unresolved. Check the archive’s omitted-materials list and follow the reference in the original series. The National Archives says it makes no regular corrections to the database. Keep an index miss open while the register page remains unexamined [S1 @2026-11-22] [S8 @2026-11-22] [S3 @2026-11-22] [S4 @2026-11-22].

If the burial and communion-book entries together supply a father’s given name and trade, search historical newspapers for the name and occupation together. Use an appointment or transfer notice to check whether a candidate worked in the parish at the relevant time [S1].

## Return the record chain

Return the parish, series, volume and page, the fields actually transcribed, the scope of the volume-and-page search and the status of the image or reading-room route.
