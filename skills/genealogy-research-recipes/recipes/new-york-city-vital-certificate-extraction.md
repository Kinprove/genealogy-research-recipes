# Read New York City Clerk marriage packets and historical birth certificates

Use this recipe when a New York City marriage search returns one certificate, or an early city birth index leaves the family’s origin or earlier children unresolved.

## Find digitized vital records

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: third-party-tool --> Before submitting a name or record locator that identifies someone who may be living, clear the gate for the fields sent to NYC Historical Vital Records or to the outside index you use.

Open **Historical Vital Records of NYC** from the [DORIS Genealogy page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/records/historical-records/genealogy.page). [S5 @2026-11-21] If you lack a certificate number, try the portal’s name search for the image, according to the video presenter’s advice. [S4† @2026-11-21], [S6 @2026-11-21] If the name search misses, look up the number in an outside index and search the city portal by record type, number, borough and filing year. For a City Clerk marriage license, start with the separate license index and use its license number. [S6 @2026-11-21], [S7 @2026-11-21]

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: third-party-tool --> Before querying MyHeritage, clear the gate for any names, dates or places you would send to MyHeritage that identify people who may be living.

If the city search stalls, use the collection links in the [MyHeritage guide](https://education.myheritage.com/article/new-york-city-vital-records-an-essential-resource-for-researching-american-heritage/) to search Births (1866–1909), Marriages (1866–1949) or Deaths (1866–1948) and open the associated image if available. [S8 @2026-11-21], [S9 @2026-11-21] For a 1908–1937 marriage, use Marriages to look for the Health Department certificate and seek the City Clerk license in its own series. [S9 @2026-11-21]

## Find the two marriage filings

According to the podcast adviser, seek the City Clerk license as well as the New York City marriage certificate when searching for the spouses’ and parents’ birthplaces. [S3†]

Search both filing series separately for a New York City marriage dated 1908–1937. The Health Department registered the officiant’s certificate and the City Clerk kept a separate license series. Look up each result under its own index and keep its own number. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S2 @2026-11-18]

In the Clerk’s packet, record the couple as the suppliers of the affidavit, the Clerk as the issuer of the license and the officiant as the reporter on the completed certificate. Use the officiant’s certificate for the ceremony and check the application and license pages for occupations, residences and parental birthplaces. Copy each detail from the page that gives it. [S1], [S2]

## Read the historical birth certificate fields

Read each parent’s birthplace field on the image. Preserve the smallest place actually written, even if only one parent’s entry names a county or another subdivision. [S1]

Copy the form’s wording and both counts for children the mother has had and children living. Use the two figures to plan sibling searches and check whether the form or the individual return counts the new child before interpreting the difference. [S1]
