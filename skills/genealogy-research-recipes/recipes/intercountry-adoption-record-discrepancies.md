# Reconcile names, dates and consent in intercountry adoption records

Compare birth and placement records, adoption papers and attributed family accounts when an intercountry adoption may have been irregular.

## Compare the versions before assigning identity or consent

Make one row per source. Record the pre-adoption name, assigned name, stated birth date, issuing authority, record date and stated basis for placement or consent. Keep the birth parent’s account as a separate attributed source and compare its chronology with each document before calculating age. When a Chilean civil birth search misses, keep a home birth with no surviving registration among the possibilities. A consent statement in the file records what the paperwork says. Establish what the signer understood and agreed to from independent evidence. [S1] [S2] [S3]

## Ask for an account through an established route

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: contact --> The requester’s identity and an invitation about the person’s own account go to the birth parent through an existing authorized route. Clear the contact gate before sending.

Through an existing authorized route, ask the birth parent whether they want to give their own account of the placement. With consent, ask what they recall signing, what they understood the document to authorize, and how the child entered care. Record their wording and distinguish what they witnessed from what they learned later. [S1] [S2]

## Route an origin-file request

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: request --> The adoptee’s identity and required proof go to the origin-search authority. Adoption papers may identify living relatives and sensitive parentage. Clear the gate for the exact fields and every living person before sending.

For a Chilean origin-file request, start with the official Búsqueda de Orígenes route for applicants in Chile or abroad. Use consular guidance where an overseas applicant needs help identifying the route. Submit only the fields and proof the office requires after clearing the gate. [S4 @2026-11-21] [S5 @2026-11-21]
