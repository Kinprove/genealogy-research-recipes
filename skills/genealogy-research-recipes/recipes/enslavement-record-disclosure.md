# Make privately held records of enslaved people discoverable

Use this recipe after finding named enslaved people in privately held papers. Prepare an extract that preserves the names as written and gives a locator for the original. Keep an association in the record distinct from a family relationship inferred from it.

## Publish the names with a way back to the record

**Scope.** The community author suggests sharing names and details with genealogy groups or societies tied to the document’s place. The Beyond Kin directory takes the researched enslaver or institution as its entry point. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S2 @2026-11-18]

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: publish --> Names and details from the historical record and any living people named in the draft would go to the society, group and its audience. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) and redact unrelated living people’s data.

Publish the source-cited historical extract through a genealogy society or descendant research group that covers the document’s locality. Put the enslaved people’s names in the searchable text, along with the record type, date, place and locator. Read the receiving project’s collection scope before preparing a submission. [S1]

## Preserve access to the source

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: upload --> The original or its copy and any owner, donor or living-person details would go to the receiving repository. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) and agree on access before transfer.

Arrange deposit of the original or a permitted copy with an archive, library or museum collecting records for the locality. For a privately shown document, obtain the holder’s permission to copy and deposit it. Keep the recipient and the accession or collection reference with the extract. [S1]

The author suggests routing records about the construction of the University of Virginia to the research on enslaved laborers there. [S1]

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: contact --> The record citation and the sender’s details would go to the university’s descendant outreach contact. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) before sending.

Send a concise record lead to the Gibbons Project’s descendant outreach contact when the document connects people enslaved by the family to labor at the University of Virginia. Identify the record and its locator in the first message. [S3 @2026-11-18]

## Describe a documented parent-child relationship

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: publish --> The tree entry and kinship evidence could be visible to service users or the public and could identify living descendants. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) before publishing.

Publish a documented parent-child link in the online tree with the evidence for parentage and the recorded condition of enslavement. Describe the documented relationship between the enslaver and the enslaved parent without implying a consensual union. Keep tentative links in private research notes. In one published example, a tree note described the father as the enslaver of the child’s mother. Cite the records for each relationship and distinguish what each record actually says. [S1]
