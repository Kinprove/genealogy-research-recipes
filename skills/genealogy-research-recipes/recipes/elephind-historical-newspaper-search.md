# Search historical newspapers with Elephind

Use Elephind to discover articles across historical newspaper collections. If the title or issue is already known, use [newspaper finding by title and date](newspaper-finder-title-coverage.md) to verify the issue coverage.

## Check the title and pick the search mode

Check Elephind’s Newspaper Collections list for the title or the participating collection. If the title is outside the list, seek it through a different host. A search miss is bounded by the titles, issues, filters and queries you checked [S1 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22].

Choose **Keyword Search** for a known term or distinctive name. Choose **Smart Search** for an event or subject that may appear under different period vocabulary. Phrase the topic as a question or in modern words, rephrase if needed, and try literal period terms from promising articles. Filter by publication, collection or date to inspect one bounded set at a time [S1 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22].

## Retrieve the item from the newspaper host

Follow a result to the contributing library or archive. Check the issue and the printed article before drawing a conclusion, and cite the host's issue, date and page [S1] [S3].
