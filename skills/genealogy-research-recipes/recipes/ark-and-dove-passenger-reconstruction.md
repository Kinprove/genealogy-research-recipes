# Test an Ark or Dove passenger claim in early Maryland records

Use this recipe when a family tradition or compilation names a passenger on the ships that brought Maryland’s first colonial expedition. Treat a compiled passenger name as a claim with a source trail.

## Choose the part that tests the claim

According to the presenter’s advice, use the Russells’ compilation to identify the relevant part and follow the record references behind a passenger or descent claim. [S1†]

Use Part One of *The Ark and the Dove Adventurers* for Lord Baltimore’s family. Use Part Two for passengers with families and documented descendant treatments. Check Part Three before accepting a tradition, especially one about a later settler or a passenger with no known descendants. The publisher identifies the roster as reconstructed from other records. [S2]

## Test the reconstructed passenger against original records

Read the cited original colonial court and assembly records, then check the original county’s land, probate and church material for the identity and link in question. Use the Society’s adventurer citations and the Maryland State Archives’ *Early Settlers* entries as locators. Record exactly what the underlying document says about the voyage or arrival. A later land or probate record can establish early presence and still leave the vessel unresolved. [S2 @2026-11-18], [S3 @2026-11-18], [S4 @2026-11-18]
