# Trace a Samoan family through matai titles, villages and gafa

Open this recipe when a Samoan ancestor appears under different names or records abroad give a title or village. Track each title, holder, village and date separately.

## Find the title and village

Search for the matai title as well as the birth surname in land and public records. Track the title as a role associated with a person at a particular date. Use a title’s village association to choose the locality to research and retain separate candidates when several people held the title. [S1], [S3], [S4]

For a customary-land or chiefly-title dispute in the Independent State of Samoa, consult the Land and Titles Court as the jurisdiction to investigate. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S2 @2026-11-18]

Look for a title in wills and public documents as well as custom and land records. Keep the form in each document with its source and date. [S1]

## Reconstruct the gafa with its local sources

Record the gafa from its oral or written account with the reciter or compiler, the village and the line of transmission. Include the extended aiga and label the stated connections between families. [S1]

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: contact --> The sender’s identity and the minimum family names, dates and locality would go to the selected record holder. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) and keep any recent record request unsent until its own access rules and permissions are established.

If the centralized search misses, ask the village, congregation or family custodian about the specific register or gafa and the period it covers. Search the local baptismal entries with the birth and title forms you already have. [S1]

Record the sponsor’s name and role as written in a baptism. If a grandparent is named as sponsor, use the entry to guide the search for kinship records. Check the evidence for the family relationship separately from the sponsor role. [S1]

## Carry overseas records back to Samoa

Start with historical arrival and vital records in the place of settlement if the family’s Samoan locality is unknown. Extract a recorded village or title verbatim from those records and use it to select the Samoan locality and the title forms to check. [S1]
