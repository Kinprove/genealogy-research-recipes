# Recover a County Durham Home Guard enrolment record

Use this recipe for possible County Durham Home Guard service during the Second World War. Use Durham as the place of Home Guard service. Check where the person served during the war even if they were born elsewhere.

## Use Durham as a service-place filter

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: third-party-tool --> A name or date query sends the volunteer’s details to MyHeritage or Discovery. Apply the privacy gate in the skill before using details that identify anyone who may be living.

Search MyHeritage’s England, Durham Home Guard index for service in the county in 1940–1945. Compare the birth date, birthplace and battalion if indexed [S1 @2026-11-22]. Search the identified candidate in The National Archives’ Discovery catalogue within WO 409 and retain the full item reference [S2 @2026-11-22] [S3 @2026-11-22].

For a unit outside Durham, follow the general Home Guard route linked by the Ministry of Defence. The Durham index covers service in that county, so a miss there leaves other Home Guard service open [S1 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22] [S3 @2026-11-22].

## Read the enrolment form

Check the enrolment form’s birth information, service address, enlistment, battalion and earlier military service where given. Record each service episode under its own unit. The enrolment material can identify a posting but leaves day-to-day duties unresolved [S1] [S2] [S3].
