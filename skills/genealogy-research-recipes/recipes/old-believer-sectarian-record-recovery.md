# Recover Old Believer and Edinoverie families from raskol series in consistory, revision, district and police fonds

Use this recipe when a Russian-Empire family suspected of Old Belief (раскол, старообрядчество) or Edinoverie (единоверие) is missing from the ordinary Orthodox metrical books, revision lists or confession rolls. Do not conclude that the family went unrecorded. The state counted, taxed and registered raskolniki in their own series. In the worked example, those series were filed inside the same consistory, revision and district-office fonds as the ordinary books, and after 1874 the police kept raskol metrical registers. This recipe builds a checklist of those series, chains the household across them, and keeps each confession label as a dated administrative classification.

For revision lists as registration ledgers and for chaining a household before you judge a name, use [Russian-Empire identity attribution](https://github.com/Kinprove/genealogy-research-recipes/blob/8b235c47acf62b78cd5a1ccf85f4eb974b2f966a/skills/genealogy-research-recipes/recipes/russian-empire-jewish-identity-attribution.md). Its subject is Jewish families, but its household-chaining method applies here too. To check which pages and sections of a multi-church delo you actually opened, use [archival image coverage verification](https://github.com/Kinprove/genealogy-research-recipes/blob/8b235c47acf62b78cd5a1ccf85f4eb974b2f966a/skills/genealogy-research-recipes/recipes/archival-image-coverage-verification.md). For identity rulings, conflicting ages and treating a declared label as a claim, use [evidence/proof judgment](https://github.com/Kinprove/genealogy-research-recipes/blob/8b235c47acf62b78cd5a1ccf85f4eb974b2f966a/skills/genealogy-research-recipes/recipes/evidence-proof-judgment.md).

## 1. Fix period, locality and the Orthodox parish the family was counted under

- Before you search, write down three things: the years, the settlement, and the **Orthodox parish the settlement was attached to**. In the Ekaterinburg example, the Old Believer births, marriages and deaths of Shartash were kept for Old Believers "числящихся в приходе" of the Ekaterina cathedral (Екатерининский собор), not under a church of their own. [S1]
- Do the same for the Edinoverie parish, and give it its own row even when the village also belongs to an Orthodox parish. In the Shadrinsk example, a village belonged to the Nikolaevskaya church's parish (GASO f.6 op.1 d.421 for 1812) and also to the Preobrazhensky cathedral's, while its Edinoverie households belonged to the Troitskaya Edinoverie church. Use the Edinoverie church's clergy registers (клировые ведомости; GASO f.37 op.4 d.89 for 1870–1871) to see which villages and how many households it served. [S4]
- Find out when the Edinoverie parish was founded. The 1902 diocesan parish history *Приходы и церкви Екатеринбургской епархии* says Shadrinsk Edinoverie believers first went to the Ekaterinburg Edinoverie church, then to Sosnovskoe village, and formed their own parish only in 1839. Add those earlier churches' books for the earlier years to the checklist. [S4]

Inventory-reading rules from GASO f.6 op.1 [S2]:

- In this fond, Old Believer village books sit inside dela whose headings name ordinary churches. d.8 is headed as the 1805 metrical book of the Предтеченская church of Щелкунская слобода, and the second line of its description adds the Екатерининская church's "записные книги" for the Old Believer villages of Shartash, Stanovaya and Sarapulka for 1805. In d.45 the 1809 "записные книги" for the Old Believer villages of Sarapulka and Stanovaya come first (l.1), ahead of the town churches' 1809 books. Read the whole delo description, not just the heading. In other regions, check whether the same arrangement holds.
- In this fond, Edinoverie books are gathered into multi-year, multi-church dela outside the year-by-year volumes (d.197 covers sixteen Edinoverie churches for 1862–1866). Search the inventory for "единоверческ" and do not stop at the volume for your year.
- A place appears under different church forms over time. The 1840 volume d.93 has a "Часовни Шарташского селения" (l.779), and later volumes have the Свято-Троицкая church of Shartash (d.192 for 1862, d.258 for 1873). Give each one a separate row.
- Record old and new delo numbers together where the transcription gives both. The 1807 book is cited as f.6 op.3 d.147ст=d.287нов. [S1]
- Look at what is attached to revision skazki. The 1857 skazka (GASO f.8 op.1 d.1777, ll.530–531) has certificates from the Spasskaya and Troitskaya Edinoverie churches filed with it, giving births and baptisms, including baptisms one or two years after the birth. [S1]

## 3. From 1874, add the police raskol metrical registers

- The law of 19 April 1874 required the police to keep metrical books of the births, marriages and deaths of raskolniki. For any event from 1874 up to the 17 October 1906 ukaz on Old Believer and sectarian communities, give its own row to the police raskol metrical register of the town or uezd where the family lived. Look for it in the police fonds of the regional archive, not only in the consistory fond. A family that stayed outside the Orthodox and Edinoverie churches after 1874 will not appear in the consistory books for those events. For events before 1906, the police register is the series to check first.
- For an event after the 17 October 1906 ukaz, do not assume the police still kept the register. Keep the police row, add rows for the community registers and for the town-board and volost-board registers named in the first lead of step 8, and read the ukaz's registration clauses before you rank them.
- A participant in the Subbotniks thread [S5] cited cases on keeping metrical books for raskolniki in TsGIA SPb: f.960 op.1 d.128 (described as a district police fond) and f.254 op.1 d.7822. These are participant-reported examples. Check them in the TsGIA SPb inventories before you cite them.

## 4. Register each series with its status

For every row, record: series, archive and fond/opis/delo with leaf, the registrar (Orthodox clergy, Edinoverie clergy, district office, commission, revision, police), the years covered, and the status: **searched, with result**, **searched, nothing found** or **not searched / not located**. A series you did not open, or that you could not find in the inventory, is a gap. It is not a negative result. [S1], [S2]

## 5. Chain the household across the series

- Link entries by the head's name and patronymic, ages, the wife's name and patronymic, children, and in-laws named in the household. Ages drift between series. A mismatch ranks a candidate lower but never excludes it. [S1], [S3]
- Keep later annotations separate. In the example, the 1834 skazka (GASO f.8 op.1 d.712) carries pencil additions made in 1850. Date them as 1850 statements, not 1834 ones. [S1]
- Follow the family from the raskol series into town and estate series as its status changes. In the example, the same households appear in a separate 1793 return of Shartash posad people (GASO f.24 op.23 d.6987 l.86), as meshchane in the 1811 revision (GASO f.8 op.1 d.451) and in town inhabitant lists (d.493, 1816–1818). A merchant's move into the meshchane is recorded in the 1834 merchants' skazka (GAPK f.111 op.1 d.2463). [S1]
- When the ordinary confession roll misses a family, use the non-confessant registers to fill the gaps between revisions. [S3]

## 6. Quote every confession label as a dated classification

- Quote, for each person and date, the confession wording, the толк and any "time of joining" exactly as written. Never turn them into one fixed identity for the person.
- Clerks applied labels to everyone at once. In the 1838 опись every family gets the same two entries: "Из давна состоят в расколе" and "Беглопоповщинской секты". That is the inventory's formula, not each family's own testimony. [S1]
- Expect a household to be split. One 1857 skazka says one son with his wife and son were Edinoverie and "а прочие все старообрядцы". Another names two sons with their wives and children as Edinoverie and the rest as Old Believers. Record confession **per member**, not per household. [S1]
- One person can carry different labels over time. The 1742 census says a man fell into raskol as a child under his parents' teaching. An 1849 Edinoverie marriage calls the groom "вновь присоединившийся из раскола". An 1886 Orthodox baptism-section entry describes a woman as a "раскольница беглопоповщинской секты" received by chrismation, and the marriage entry for the same day calls her "из раскола". Keep each label with its date and registrar. [S1]

## 7. Treat a non-confessant listing as a lead

Not confessing does not show Old Belief. The district non-confessant registers list households that did not come to confession, and they give no толк [S3]. A roll titled "не исповедался" does not name a confession either. In the 1812 roll the Old Believers sit under their own "старообрядцы" heading, and that heading is what identifies them [S1]. Record the listing as "listed as non-confessant, year N". Before you call the family Old Believer, find a dated entry that classifies it explicitly as Old Believer or raskolnik, such as a "старообрядцы" heading, a raskol levy or a raskol census. Record the толк only where the record states one.

## 8. Routes kept out of this recipe (unverified forum leads)

A long discussion of the vital registration of Subbotniks and other sectarians [S5] reaches no settled answer, and participants themselves said the question stayed open. Keep these only as **unverified forum leads**. Check each against the legislation for the event year and against the inventories that actually survive:

- A participant quoted a wiki summary saying that from 1905–06 the communities' own clergy, or town boards and volost boards, took over the metrical registration of raskolniki and sectarians. Another participant called the summary unclear.
- A participant suggested that deaths of sectarians may have been certified by different officials from births and marriages, so they may sit in another fond. The participant said they were not sure.
- A participant suggested RGIA fonds 796, 797, 821 and 1284, plus governor's chancery and police fonds, for case files on sects. The same participant warned that these bodies did not necessarily keep name lists.
- Participants reported entries marked "из сектантов субботников" in synagogue books, a certificate issued by a local "spiritual board" for Judaizing sectarians, and an 1839 consistory case on "совращение" that gathered papers from earlier places of residence.

## Output

Return:

1. **A dated series register.** One row per series, as in step 4.
2. **A household chain.** For each person, give each appearance with its locator, age and household position, and quote the confession, толк and conversion wording with its date.
3. **A gap list.** Name the series that were not located or not searched (including any post-1874 police register), the Edinoverie or predecessor parishes not yet checked, and any forum lead from step 3 or step 8 still waiting for verification.
