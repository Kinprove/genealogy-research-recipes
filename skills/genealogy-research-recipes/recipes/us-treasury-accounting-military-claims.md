# Trace a U.S. military payment through Treasury accounting records

Find Treasury audit, paymaster or pension-account records for a U.S. military payment or claim, using the office and transaction date.

## Identify which office handled the transaction

Use the transaction and the accounting office to choose the series. Keep a claim, its settlement and its payment distinct. According to the presenter, route the transaction by the Treasury office that handled it [S1† @2026-11-21]. In RG 217, start with Inventory 14 and the Guide to Federal Records. Use the Register of the Treasury for central receipts and expenditure ledgers and journals. Check the First and Second Comptrollers for the audit route appropriate to the date, and the Accountant for the War Department and Second Auditor for War Department accounting across the 1817 reorganization. Test the Third Auditor for military subsistence or Quartermaster accounts. For postal accounting, check the Fifth Auditor descriptions and the related Sixth Auditor records in RG 28. [S2 @2026-11-21] [S3 @2026-11-21]

## Follow a paymaster or pension payment

Use the paymaster’s name and date to locate a settled Army account. Search the available register or inventory for the relevant account number or box. According to the presenter, record which paymasters each inspected box covers [S1† @2026-11-21]. For a pension payment, check the agency payment abstracts and final-payment vouchers as separate series from the claimant’s application. Compare payee, agency, amount and transaction date before connecting entries. [S2 @2026-11-21] [S3 @2026-11-21]
