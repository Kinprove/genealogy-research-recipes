# Recover a Jersey household from German Occupation registration cards

Trace a Jersey resident or child through German Occupation registration cards and household entries.

## Choose the registration series

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: third-party-tool --> The index search sends the name and any date or place filters you enter to MyHeritage. Answer the privacy gate in `SKILL.md` before searching. Treat the person as living if their status is uncertain.

Look in *Channel Islands, Jersey, Occupation Registration Cards* for an index lead, then use Jersey Archive’s guide for the registration cards, series D/S/A. The title refers to German occupation. Read the resident’s occupation as a separate entry. Record birth date and place, residence and occupation as given, keeping any updates distinct from the initial entry. [S1 @2026-11-21] [S2 @2026-11-21] [S3 @2026-11-21]

## Follow the household if a child has no card

Search for a child under fourteen on the father’s card before treating a missing card as a residence gap. Children below the registration age could appear on the back of the father’s card. Check for a separate card after the child turned fourteen. [S1] [S2]
