# Review a MyHeritage profile Hint before copying facts or relatives

Start from the person’s MyHeritage tree profile on the desktop website. Decide which facts the source supports before selecting any arrows.

## Read beyond the highlighted hint

Open the person’s **Profile**, open the **Hint** and choose **Review** on the relevant source card. Scroll through the side-by-side comparison and open the underlying source where available. Review fields beyond the yellow highlighting. Check whether the source concerns the same person. [S1 @2026-11-18]

A Record Match can point to a namesake even when the name and birthplace or place of death agree, so compare another identifier such as a dated event or named relative before confirming it. [S11]

For a Smart Match, compare the person’s name and both parents’ names with your own evidence. Compare the known sibling names and count as well, checking for siblings omitted from either tree. [S16], [S17] Allow for spelling differences. Treat newly supplied birth dates or places as leads and keep the match pending if a conflicting event still needs checking. [S17], [S18]

If someone in the matched branch who knew the person identifies them in a photograph, record the identification in your notes as that person’s claim, along with how they knew the subject. Use the claim to guide a record search and base the match decision on the records. [S16], [S19]

Check full-record access in the user’s account. A Record Match can be visible even when a subscription is needed to open the record. [S12 @2026-11-22], [S13 @2026-11-22] If it is locked, record the collection and any visible names, dates and locator. Keep the match pending while you [look for the underlying record](myheritage-historical-record-search.md).

For a restricted lead to a 1950 U.S. census entry, check the standalone **1950 United States Federal Census** collection, which MyHeritage lists as free. [S14 @2026-11-22], [S15 @2026-11-22]

## Find additional matches

If no Hint covers the detail you need, check for the profile’s **Matches** tab. Choose **Review** beside a pending Smart Match or Record Match to compare it side by side with the profile. [S6 @2026-11-21]

From the desktop tree, click the brown Record Match icon on the person’s card to open their list of Record Matches. [S11 @2026-11-22], [S4 @2026-11-22]

To change which Hints are displayed, look for **Settings** in the desktop profile’s three-dot menu. If Hint display options are available in your account, select the types you want and choose **Apply**. Check the family tree view as well, since the display settings can affect both views. [S6 @2026-11-21]

On the website, open **Discoveries**. Choose **Matches by people** to review leads for an ancestor or **Matches by source** to focus on a record collection or matching tree. Use **Record Matches** for historical records and **Smart Matches** for other trees. Set the status filter to **Pending**, **New**, **Confirmed** or **Rejected** to reach the review stage you need. [S4 @2026-11-21], [S7 @2026-11-21]

In the mobile app, open **Discoveries** and use the filter icon in the upper right to show only Smart Matches or only Record Matches. Tap **View** for the person, then **Review match** for the lead. [S8 @2026-11-21], [S9 @2026-11-21]

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: publish --> The target’s and relatives’ names and relationships go to MyHeritage and can be visible to other users through the tree or matching according to the site settings. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) before adding the profiles.

To look for a separated branch, add missing profiles for the deceased target and their known deceased parents or siblings. [S16 @2026-11-22], [S17 @2026-11-22] Recheck for Smart Matches and Record Matches on the target and those relatives. Use tree matches to find candidate branches and records naming collateral relatives to trace them. [S20 @2026-11-22], [S21 @2026-11-22]

## Follow relationship leads

When tracing an emigrant branch from a Smart Match, open the matching tree if you have access. Find a relative you can already place in your own tree and follow the relationships from that person toward the emigrant branch. [S3 @2026-11-20] Check the tree even when the match adds no facts about the matched person. If you cannot open the tree, inspect the Smart Match’s close-family comparison for leads. [S4 @2026-11-20] Verify each relationship before copying it.

On the website, choose **Review match**, hover over the matched name and choose **View in tree** if available. Open the matched person’s card and click the relationship in the left panel, when displayed, to see the path recorded in that tree between the person and its site manager. [S22 @2026-11-22], [S23 @2026-11-22]

If branch colors or the blood-relative indicator are absent, check whether the site manager appears in the matched tree. These cues depend on the manager having a profile there. When either cue is missing, trace the relationships from the tree entries before drawing a conclusion about kinship. [S22 @2026-11-22], [S23 @2026-11-22]

When a Smart Match points to an ancestor you have already identified but follows a different line of descent, check whether that line runs through a child of an earlier marriage already in your tree. [S10]

If a Record Match names a deceased sibling, use that sibling’s name as a starting point to trace their deceased descendants in historical records. [S10]

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: contact --> The message shares the sender’s identity and the names or relationships needed for the question with the matching site manager through MyHeritage. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) and the [Living people contact rules](../SKILL.md#living-people) before sending.

If a relationship or event on the branch needs clarification, open the person’s Smart Matches list. Click or hover on the matching site manager’s name and choose **Contact** if available in your account. [S5 @2026-11-22] Ask which source supports the relationship or conflicting event in the matched tree. [S17 @2026-11-22]

## Select values and relationships individually

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: link --> The association between the online profile and the source or matching tree would be recorded on MyHeritage. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) before linking if either side identifies a person who may be living.

Confirm the profile match only once you have established the correspondence. Then open the extraction panel for the fact review. [S1 @2026-11-18]

In the mobile app, tap **Confirm** on a verified Smart Match or Record Match if the control is available in your account. [S24 @2026-11-22], [S9 @2026-11-22]

In **Extract information**, choose the arrows for the supported fields. Compare each selected value with the previous tree value shown beneath it. Use the revert control for an unwanted replacement. Use the pencil on a selected row to edit the value inline when the source supports the edited wording. [S1 @2026-11-18]

In **Immediate family**, open **More details** and inspect the available facts for the relative. Select the supported facts and relationship before adding the relative to the tree. [S1 @2026-11-18]

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: publish --> The source facts and selected family relationships become available in the MyHeritage tree according to its visibility settings. Apply the [privacy gate](../SKILL.md#privacy-gate) before saving any data about people who may be living.

Use **Save to tree** for the selected facts and relatives after reviewing the scope of the change. Reopen the profile to check the new values and their linked citations, keeping a reference to the underlying source for anything the generated citation omits. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S2 @2026-11-18]

After confirming a Smart Match in the mobile app, save only supported information on the next screen. If the app does not offer a way to select only those details, finish the extraction on the website. [S24 @2026-11-22], [S9 @2026-11-22]
