# Recover a U.S. Army Civil War court-martial case file

Start with a general court-martial mentioned in a Union Civil War compiled military service record. Treat the service-card notation as a locator for a separate proceeding.

## Take the service clue to an Army case number

According to the presenter’s advice, follow a general court-martial entry in the service record to the separate proceedings held by the National Archives. [S1†]

Copy the name, rank, regiment, dates and any general order number from the service record. Look up the proceeding by name or, for the Civil War, by regiment in the Archives 1 finding aids. Read the case key and number into your log and use them to locate the early series in RG 153, Entry PC-29 15, National Archives Identifier 301659. [S2 @2026-11-18]

## Read the proceeding in order

Extract the charge, plea, testimony, finding and sentence from the surviving documents. Record any later reviewing order separately. An allegation and the court’s finding are different observations. [S2]
