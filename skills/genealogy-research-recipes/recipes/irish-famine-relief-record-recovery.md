# Find an Irish household in Famine relief and workhouse records

## Choose the office by locality and record type

Start with a townland or town, an approximate Famine-era date and a household clue. Identify the Poor Law union for the workhouse search and keep the named local relief committee or district as a separate field. Use [Irish civil jurisdiction](irish-civil-registration-jurisdiction-recovery.md) for the townland-to-union chain and [Irish emigrant origins](irish-emigrant-origin-locality.md) if you have only an unplaced name.

## Search Famine relief correspondence

Search the National Archives of Ireland catalogue under RLFC/2 for the Relief Commission papers. Work by locality, committee and date as well as surname, allowing for uncertain name readings. Use the separate Distress Papers indexes under CSO CR 70 onward where the correspondence concerns local distress, forming a committee or applications for relief funds. Follow each index entry to its letter, report or petition. [S1 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22]

Record the role each person has in the relief document before using its place as their residence. Distinguish the applicant, signatory or committee member, and the people discussed in the letter. Capture any explicit residence or place of relief and its date. An appeal written for a district leaves individual receipt of aid unproven. [S1] [S2]

## Follow a workhouse or union lead

Look for indoor relief or admission and discharge registers under the appropriate union. Search the volumes covering the suspected stay for the individual or household. Use the guardians’ minute books for the board’s decisions and for named cases where minutes give them. The National Archives of Ireland lists separate holdings for North Dublin (BG/78), South Dublin (BG/79) and Rathdown (BG/137). For another union, establish the holder and surviving years before logging the result. [S1 @2026-11-22] [S3 @2026-11-22]

Return the locality and union or relief committee, the name and role as recorded, the date and place stated in each document, the full reference, and the volume or span actually checked. Record any unsearched or missing series. A district report alone leaves the household’s whereabouts open.
