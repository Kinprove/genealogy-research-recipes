# Recover peasant land and settlement files for Radom Governorate

Use this recipe when a parish entry puts a household in a village in Russian-partition Radom Governorate and you need a dated land or settlement record. Use [historical place-name resolution](historical-place-name-resolution.md) to fix the village, gmina and district for the date. Keep competing village divisions open until the record identifies one.

## Route by the historical office

If the parish route led you to Kielce, check Radom as well for the governorate's peasant-affairs files. Open the [Radom State Archive catalogue for 58/117/0](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/zespol/-/zespol/28668), `Urząd Gubernialny Radomski do Spraw Włościańskich`. Use the village, gmina and district to identify unit titles in the fond. Look for settlement files under `Urządzenia włościan` and for `tabela likwidacyjna` entries. The office's predecessors checked and corrected the tables and received complaints about measurements [S1 @2026-11-22] [S2 @2026-11-22] [S3 @2026-11-22].

## Check each village division

A village name alone does not identify the division covered by a peasant-affairs file. For Bardo, use the reported Górne, Dolne and `cz. poduchowna` titles as separate catalogue leads. Check each matching unit for resident correspondence and the `cz. poduchowna` unit for a liquidation table [S1].

For Szumsko, try the reported 1865–1902 general file and 1868–1901 `cz. poduchowna` file separately. Check the latter for the reported 1896 document. Read the closing witness list and tie each `sołtys` to the settlement named with that office before assigning any residence [S1].

## Follow a table into the mortgage papers

If the peasant-affairs list has no matching table, look in the supporting papers of the village mortgage book. For Radostów, search the [Kielce mortgage fonds](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/zespol/-/zespol/112524), `Hipoteka powiatu kieleckiego` 21/1024/0, for the volume II lead. Inspect the table for named families. Record land, residence and relationship only to the extent that the individual entry states them [S1 @2026-11-22] [S4 @2026-11-22].

Return the archive, full unit reference, village division, document date and page, with a separate observation for a resident, estate owner or official witness. Use [Polish parish and civil register routing](polish-parish-and-civil-register-routing.md) to test the identity of a person the land record names.
