# Recover Lord Dunmore’s War payroll and public service claims

Use this recipe to test a soldier’s service or a household’s material support for the 1774 expedition. A payment can record goods or services supplied instead of militia duty.

## Search the commissioners’ account ledger

From the available excerpt, the presenter points to the claims commissioners’ ledger in Virginia Colonial Government Records, Record Group 1 [S1 @2026-11-22]. Start with the Library of Virginia’s Dunmore’s War Payroll and Service Claims guide. Its partial name index links to digitized ledger pages for Augusta, Bedford, Botetourt, Culpeper and Fincastle [S2 @2026-11-22].

From the available excerpt, the presenter advises browsing the ledger after an unsuccessful name search [S1]. Browse the relevant county pages because the index omits some pages. Transcribe whether the entry pays for a length of service, goods or other support and copy the amount and locator. Keep the exact basis of payment in the result [S2].

## Check the separate West Augusta and voucher records

From the available excerpt, the presenter points to Miscellaneous Microfilm Reel 78 [S1 @2026-11-22]. Use that reel for West Augusta Public Service Claims, the Pittsburgh, Romney and Winchester vouchers and the Pittsburgh payroll. Verify access with the library before relying on a loan. Keep a miss in the online ledger index separate from an unsearched reel [S2 @2026-11-22].

Return each record’s county or district, ledger page or reel and entry, name, basis of payment and amount. Use [military service routing](us-military-service-type-record-routing.md) for other service types and [county record guides](state-archives-county-records-guides.md) to verify the historical jurisdiction.
