# Find a historical Olympic participant or trial entrant

Use this recipe to test a family story about an ancestor and a historical Olympic Games. Keep entry in a qualifying trial distinct from selection for the Games.

## Distinguish trials from Games results

According to the author’s search advice, check Olympic trials coverage if the person is missing from the team list. [S1†]

Search historical newspapers around the trials with the name, sport and the term *Olympic trials*. Follow earlier qualifying events if the coverage identifies one. Record the competition level and result that the article actually names. [S2]

According to the author, use Olympedia for athlete profiles and the editions and events in which they competed. [S1†]

Use Olympedia to identify the athlete, edition, country and event. Compare its event result with the Olympic organizing committee report where the claim needs confirmation. [S3 @2026-11-18], [S4 @2026-11-18]

## Separate published reports from held archival files

According to the author, search the Olympic World Library and the IOC historical archive when studying a competitor or someone involved with the IOC. [S1†]

Look up the edition in the Olympic World Library for Olympic Studies Centre and organizing committee publications. When the lead is an internal record, identify the fonds, dates and file reference in the IOC Historical Archives finding aid. [S4 @2026-11-18], [S5 @2026-11-18], [S6 @2026-11-18]
