# Trace a colonial tavern keeper to licenses and rates

Use this recipe when a colonial record names a tavern, ordinary or keeper in Maryland or Delaware, or a suspected tavern lies along the Old Buckingham Road in Virginia. Treat a named tavern keeper in a compilation as a lead to a dated license.

## Search the compilation that fits the place

For Maryland or Delaware, search by keeper in Henry C. Peden Jr.'s *Colonial Tavern Keepers of Maryland and Delaware, 1634–1776*. Check for the spouse and residence. According to the presenter in the available excerpt, use the separate index to other names if the keeper entries give no hit [S1†]. Record whether the name occurs as a keeper or in another person's entry. Start with the record citation in the vignette. [S2]

For a site on Virginia's Old Buckingham Road, check Agnes E. Gish's *Virginia Taverns, Ordinaries and Coffee Houses* by locality and road segment. Use the book's geographic scope before expanding to other counties. [S3]

## Follow the license and the rate schedule

According to the presenter in the available excerpt, carry a Baltimore County ordinary licence citation to the county court proceedings for the stated grant year [S1†]. Preserve the description of the premises and the keeper's role as written. Use [state archives county-record guides](state-archives-county-records-guides.md) to locate the court volume and [court case-file recovery](court-case-file-recovery.md) for associated papers.

According to the presenter in the available excerpt, Peden's 1776 rates appendix supplies a separate lookup for listed goods and accommodations [S1†]. Check the jurisdiction, date, unit and amount before applying a rate to an establishment. In Maryland, use the applicable county-court schedule where surviving. The act printed in volume 36 required county justices to set rates for liquor and accommodations. Treat a prescribed rate as a schedule entry and require a transaction record to establish what a customer paid. [S4]

## Return the evidence

Give the keeper and place as written, grant date, court and record locator, establishment label, and any corroborating deed or estate locator. For equipment in an estate inventory, use [probate case document inventory](probate-case-document-inventory.md).
