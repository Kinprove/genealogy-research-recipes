# Recover Lithuanian-Jewish household and residence register evidence

Use this recipe when a Lithuanian-Jewish household’s residence or parentage needs evidence from household registration. Check the locality and register coverage before interpreting a name miss.

## Search the index using the historical locality

**Privacy gate:** <!-- privacy-gate: third-party-tool --> Submitting a query sends the research subject’s or relatives’ names, birth details and residence details entered in the form to MyHeritage.

Use MyHeritage’s Lithuanian-Jewish Household Registers from LitvakSIG (1835–1946) as a starting index. Record the birth date, birthplace and parents’ names as shown. Log residence with its own place and date. Check the locality under its period jurisdiction, including relevant places that now fall outside Lithuania. [S1 @2026-11-22], [S5 @2026-11-22], [S6 @2026-11-22]

From the MyHeritage collection, go to the provider’s All Lithuania Database and the Vilnius Household Registers project. For interwar Vilnius, check the list of released batches separately from the name query. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S2 @2026-11-18], [S3 @2026-11-18]

Search MyHeritage’s Lithuanian-Jewish Revision Lists from LitvakSIG (1795–1942) as a companion index for the historical locality. Record the birth date or year, the father’s name and the place and date indexed as residence when shown. [S5 @2026-11-22], [S7 @2026-11-22]

The last imperial revision was compiled in 1858. For later hits, identify the underlying list type before citing it. A family could be registered in one town and live in another. Corroborate the stated residence with a household register or another record that places the person there. [S8]

## Explain the gap before judging residence

Distinguish destruction of the original from a surviving but unindexed register. The Lithuanian Central State Archives holds the interwar Vilnius registers, and LitvakSIG indexes them in batches. When the locality, street or year is outside what you inspected, leave residence in that interval unresolved. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S3 @2026-11-18], [S4 @2026-11-18]
