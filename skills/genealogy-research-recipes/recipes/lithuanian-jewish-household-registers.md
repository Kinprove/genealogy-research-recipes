# Recover Lithuanian-Jewish household and residence register evidence

Use this recipe when a Lithuanian-Jewish household’s residence or parentage needs evidence from household registration. Check the locality and register coverage before interpreting a name miss.

## Search the index using the historical locality

Use MyHeritage’s Lithuanian-Jewish Household Registers from LitvakSIG as a starting index. Extract birthplace and parents as recorded and log residence with its own place and date. Check the locality under its period jurisdiction, including relevant places that now fall outside Lithuania. [S1 @2026-11-18]

From the MyHeritage collection, go to the provider’s All Lithuania Database and the Vilnius Household Registers project. For interwar Vilnius, check the list of released batches separately from the name query. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S2 @2026-11-18], [S3 @2026-11-18]

## Explain the gap before judging residence

Distinguish destruction of the original from a surviving but unindexed register. The Lithuanian Central State Archives holds the interwar Vilnius registers, and LitvakSIG indexes them in batches. When the locality, street or year is outside what you inspected, leave residence in that interval unresolved. [S1 @2026-11-18], [S3 @2026-11-18], [S4 @2026-11-18]
