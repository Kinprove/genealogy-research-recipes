# Trace a wartime civilian inquiry in the Vatican Information Office records

Use this route to trace a deceased civilian who was interned or went missing during the Second World War, or to identify the inquiry that prompted a family-held Vatican reply.

## Search the inquiry index

Use *Ufficio Informazioni Vaticano (Prigionieri di Guerra, 1939–1947)*. Despite the prisoner-of-war title, include civilian inquiries. Start with the person sought. On a matching card, read the identifying details for the person sought and the requester, the reported outcome and any box reference [S2], [S3].

## Follow correspondence and separate the dates

Follow the card’s box reference into the correspondence or lists. Look for the original request, any internee list or third-party communication it cites, and the reply. In the community case a Vatican reply notified a civilian internee’s family months after the event. Treat the inquiry, the reply and the person’s actual fate as separate findings. [S1], [S2].

Use *Inter arma caritas*, volume I, for the fonds inventory. Use volume II’s *Ricerche di civili* for examples of civilian inquiries. Record a miss in the printed selection as a miss in that selection and keep the card search open [S2].

## Return packet

Give the deceased person’s identifying details, the inquiry and reply dates, the card and box locators, and the exact statement that supports each event. If wartime persecution or survivor registration is also at issue, follow [Holocaust registers and survivor lists](holocaust-register-and-survivor-list-recovery.md).
