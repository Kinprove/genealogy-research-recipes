# Recover a British Army service record from the First World War

A missing or fire-damaged service file leaves the service question open. Start with the person's rank or corps, service number and regiment if known.

## Choose the service-specific series

For a Household Cavalry other rank or non-commissioned officer, search WO 400, Household Cavalry soldiers' service records, 1799–1920. Check the enrolment form, next-of-kin statement and any conduct record [S1 @2026-11-20] [S2 @2026-11-20] [S3 @2026-11-20].

For a First World War nurse, search WO 399 for Queen Alexandra’s Imperial Military Nursing Service, its Reserve or the Territorial Force Nursing Service. Check the named service before interpreting a miss [S1 @2026-11-20] [S4 @2026-11-20].

For a woman reported to have served in the Women’s Army Auxiliary Corps, search the service records for 1917–1920. Confirm the corps and check the surviving papers for age, birthplace and address where recorded [S1 @2026-11-20] [S4 @2026-11-20].

## Recover service clues from parallel series

For other ranks, check surviving service papers in WO 363 and pension-derived papers in WO 364. When the service packet is absent or thin, use the medal index card and its referenced medal roll for the service number, regiment and rank. Compare identifiers across these series before attaching a namesake to the service timeline [S2].
