# Follow Turku’s Dahlström cards to municipal records

Use this recipe for a Turku municipal office, court proceeding, city lot or estate when the date,
person, subject or property is known in part. If a card points to additional papers, record the court, session date and
section number, then search for the corresponding minutes as described below.

## Choose an index division

Open *Dahlströmin kortisto* in Astia from the Finnish National Archives guide. Start with the name
index for a person, the subject index for an occupation or matter, the topographic index for a street
or lot, and the probate index for an estate. Switch divisions when the clue changes from person to
property or proceeding [S3 @2026-11-22], [S4 @2026-11-22].

Use municipal appointment and resignation cards to bound a person’s time in office. Check the name and
topographic cards for the quarter and lot number where the property is part of the question. Keep the
office, estate or parcel claim tied to the particular card and source it names [S2], [S3], [S4].

Follow cross-reference cards between divisions and keep the other divisions open when the name index returns no hit [S3 @2026-11-22] [S5 @2026-11-22] [S4 @2026-11-22].

## Read the card for the research question

For a minor, search the child's name and inspect cards for any guardians named there. Record each appointment or change separately, with its date and the relationship as stated. For an apprenticeship, record the trade, the year and the place the apprentice came from. The community case leaves an apprenticeship attributed to one of two same-named men, so match the apprentice through other dated records before assigning it. [S6] [S4]

If a card names a parent and an occupation, use the combination to choose candidate cards and compare the resulting family against parish entries. For a disputed lot, carry the lot label and parties into the property cards and copy the reference to the proceeding. [S7] [S3]

## Carry the card’s reference to the minutes

Use the card as a locator and keep the underlying record’s wording and outcome separate. Copy the
authority, series, session date and numbered section. For a reopened proceeding, follow each dated
reference into the corresponding town-court minutes and keep the original inquiry and the reopened one
separate. Kyläkoski’s Turku example leads from the cards to two separately dated sections of a
renovated court book [S1], [S3].

Route the cards through the National Archives of Finland’s Astia. For the underlying magistrate or
town-court minutes, start with the Turku City Archives holdings identified in the National Archives
guide. If the card points to a renovated copy, search separately by the court and dated section
[S1 @2026-11-22], [S3 @2026-11-22].

For a lot that changed hands or was divided, keep a dated row for each transaction, quarter, lot
number and named part. Carry the identity of that part into [land index
recovery](land-index-book-series-recovery.md) or the [probate
inventory](probate-case-document-inventory.md). Return the card locator, the underlying record locator
and any unverified index-only leads.
