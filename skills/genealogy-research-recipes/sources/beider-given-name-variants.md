   and Ze'ev with Volf, Yissakhar and Dov with Ber, Efraim with Fishel), the calques Asher–Zelig
   and Uri–Shraga, and the "other historical associations" Yehoshua–Falk, Mordechai–Gimpel,
   Meshulam–Zalman, Eliezer–Lipman, Chanokh–Zundel and Menachem–Mendel. The article keys
   beside each pair were looked up in the bundled indexes; the talk does not use Beider's keys.
   JewishGen's [Given Names Data Bases](https://www.jewishgen.org/databases/givennames/)
   are named as a further source of double names, not used for any table row.
