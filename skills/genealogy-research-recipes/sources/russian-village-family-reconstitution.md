**What is authored and what the sources supply.**

The order of the work, the identity key of given name, patronymic, spouse, village and ages, reading a second surname as explanations to test, testing a church-servitor origin, looking for a cleric's son in any taxpaying estate, dating surname forms from the village's common ancestor, searching service records before the first revision for an odnodvortsy family, looking for a founder under the church form of a name and for an ancestress behind a woman's-name surname, reading forms per record series, reading a missing surname as a fact about the entry and its three searches, the three countable checks and their bounds, counting a next-of-kin record as a check only when it names the father, listing children against the civil ordinal, identifying held files by cover, title page and checksum, reading a cited frame's header, the whole-village index in every role, listing every owner the economic notes name, re-running negatives on the identity key, the whole-parish option and the unread status of a one-village pass, reading the VGD map behind a privacy gate, searching the wife's patronymic in existing indexes, recording each godparent or surety link with its strength and rivals, tracing a bridging kin term to the earliest list, charting both spouses' ancestries, the faithful year sheets and the merged sheet carried by entry locator, aligning the last list's men with later couples, reading each household census's own columns, reading a blank death column in a call-up extract as no death known to the priest, the dated snapshot diffed by record URL, normalising name pairs only inside an agreement measure, the per-turn image cap, measuring steps per frame, and the output tables are authored synthesis.
