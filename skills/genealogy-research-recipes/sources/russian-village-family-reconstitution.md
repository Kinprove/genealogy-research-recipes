**What is authored and what the sources supply.**

The order of the work, the identity key of given name, patronymic, spouse, village and ages, reading a second surname as explanations to test, the three countable checks and their bounds, listing children against the civil ordinal, identifying held files by cover, title page and checksum, reading a cited frame's header, the whole-village index in every role, re-running negatives on the identity key, the whole-parish option and the unread status of a one-village pass, searching the wife's patronymic in existing indexes, recording each godparent or surety link with its strength and rivals, tracing a bridging kin term to the earliest list, the faithful year sheets and the merged sheet carried by entry locator, aligning the last list's men with later couples, the dated snapshot diffed by record URL, normalising name pairs only inside an agreement measure, the per-turn image cap, measuring steps per frame, and the output tables are authored synthesis. The sources supply the surname and naming practice, the proof standard, the metrical-book totals and civil-registration forms, the archive and citation guidance, the reconstitution method, the godparent and surety studies, the kin-term definitions, the spreadsheet and database-versioning guidance, and the indexing, recognition and vision-model findings.
