## Sources

- **S1:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [a thread on a retired Prussian captain (1870 to c. 1919)](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/2950717/), thread started 2025-09-07. Used for: a participant's description of the Militär-Wochenblatt and its "Biographisches" index, with a span that turned out to be the Beihefte; the regimental chronicle and Ehrentafel; the Reichsanzeiger notice; the death window and the address-book sighting; the newspaper death notice; a participant's inference from the Verlustlisten miss; the officers' death lists of uncertain coverage; where deaths are registered; and the widow's remarriage file.
- **S2:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [a thread on an 18th-century Berlin family and a cuirassier NCO](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/3011009/), thread started 2026-04-06. Used for: the destruction of the Potsdam Heeresarchiv and the reported loss of the Stammrollen; surviving military church books on Ancestry; the civil parish marriage on Archion; the canton of Kürassier-Regiment Nr. 10 from Lyncker I p. 169; the canton-district baptism as a lead; the transfer of discharged soldiers into civil service; and the Geheimes Staatsarchiv file as a possible link.
- **S3:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [OFB Vaskút (Waschkut) und OFB Ridjica (Regöce, Legin)](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/54510/), thread started 2012-10-28. Used for: the namesake check in family books and register indexes, the Kurrent s/h misreading, the memorial in Hungarian name form, the inference wording, and family books disagreeing with registers.
- **S4:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [a thread on a search for a Wehrmacht-era soldier's birthplace (Josefsdorf/Józefin)](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/3015319/), thread started 2026-06-12. Used for: the WASt request for birthplace, division and regiment; NARA unit records and daily orders by regiment; the unanswered Volksbund source query; and the weak postwar birthplace with candidate places tested.
- **S5:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Zufallsfunde](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/12130/), thread started 2007-10-14. Used for: the regimental marriage registers of the Regiment Prinz Heinrich von Preußen (1756–1806) as published in *Familiengeschichtliche Blätter* 1934–36, entries labelled banns or marriage consent, and origin and confession as recorded.
- **S6:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Zufallsfunde Berlin](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/1550/), thread started 2006-12-26. Used for: the church book of the Regiment des Königs Nr. 18 (Wehrkreis-Pfarramt Spandau) kept apart from civil parishes, and a published Stammrolle extract (Regiment Prinz Heinrich, 1777/78) cited to the Geheimes Staatsarchiv, Rep. 92 B V.1.
- **S7:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Ich suche das Buch ... Wer hat es? Diskussion/Anfragen](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/2550/), thread started 2006-08-17. Used for: the title of Eger's directory of military church books (a book-list mention), and searching the Ancestry fallen-soldier cards (1939–48) by regiment, company and date, with place wording, grave location, cause of death and a cross-border place name.

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The following are authored synthesis:

- the classification by period, army and rank class
- the scope limits
- the routing table and status labels
- the treatment of the canton as a ranked search area
- the rule that an absence counts only as far as each list's coverage reaches

The sources supply forum participants' reports and worked examples. Several key reports come from a single participant, including the loss of the Stammrollen, the Lyncker canton and the Militär-Wochenblatt description. S1 and S2 share participants, so they are not independent of each other. Thread links for S1, S2 and S4 are shortened to the thread number so that personal names stay out of the recipe.
