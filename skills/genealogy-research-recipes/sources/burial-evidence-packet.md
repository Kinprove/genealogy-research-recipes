## Sources

Original procedural synthesis. The sources below are attributed and paraphrased; their prose,
photographs, database rows and real family examples are not reproduced.

- **P1:** MyHeritage, *Discover Family History Through Gravestones*, published 2014-03-31 —
  [webinar](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xsDpYFkS_LI). Office/plot preparation, bilingual
  inscriptions and preserving date discrepancies.
- **P2:** James Tanner, *Where Was Your Ancestor Buried? Using Online Cemetery Programs*,
  presentation 2023-03-23, uploaded 2023-03-28 —
  [class](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hx6hQ1fucnA). Burial/death retrieval distinctions,
  photograph maps and further cemetery evidence.
- **P3:** *More free cemetery databases are helping to find graves in Eastern European*,
  published 2025-03-29 —
  [article](https://lostrussianfamily.wordpress.com/2025/03/29/more-free-cemetery-databases-are-helping-to-find-graves-in-eastern-european/).
  Native-script searches and the demonstrated Pomnim/Pogost field order.
- **O1:** JewishGen, *JOWBR FAQ*, checked 2026-10-07 —
  [documentation](https://www.jewishgen.org/databases/Cemetery/JOWBR_FAQ.htm).
  Cemetery inventory, source types, nullable fields, patronymic searches, photographs and
  full-record/cemetery links.

- **P4:** Genealogy TV, [Cemetery Registry in the United States](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_H9cfhjSTvE),
  9 January 2026. Current-device coordinate capture at the site versus off-site entry. The
  narrated verification label concerns cemetery GPS, not a verified burial or person. No
  actual coordinate, present form, accuracy or legal access claim was independently verified.
