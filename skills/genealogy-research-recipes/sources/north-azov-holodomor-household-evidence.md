## Sources

- **S1:** [Anna Hedo and Pavlos Xenophontos, The Holodomor of 1932–1933 in the Greek Villages of the North Azov Region: Oral Testimonies of Victims](https://elibrary.kubg.edu.ua/id/eprint/50433/). Published 2024. Opened 2026-11-22. Read the repository record and followed the link to the published version.

- **S2:** [Hedo and Xenophontos, published article, Kyiv Historical Studies 18(1), pp. 96–105](https://elibrary.kubg.edu.ua/id/eprint/50433/1/A_Hedo_P_Xenophontos_HGVNAROTV_1_KHS_FSHN.pdf). Published 2024. Opened 2026-11-22. Read the discussions and footnotes on Greek village testimony, the household inventory and movements, grain-gathering punishments and suggested blacklisting. Witness claims stay attributed. The article quotes a first-person recollection in which a violin missing from an inventory was out on loan and later exchanged for food.

- **S3:** [National Book of Memory of Victims of the Holodomor, Donetsk Region, Part 1](https://history.org.ua/LiberUA/NKPZHGDO_2008/NKPZHGDO_2008.pdf). Published 2008. Opened 2026-11-22. Read the title page and introduction identifying the first part and its source material.

- **S4:** [National Book of Memory of Victims of the Holodomor, Donetsk Region, Part 2](https://holodomormuseum.org.ua/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/donecka_obl_t2.pdf). Published 2009. Opened 2026-11-22. Read the title page and introduction. The editors describe the martyrology and explicitly report missing civil-registration entries.

- **S5:** [National Museum of the Holodomor, 33rd: Hunger. People’s Book-Memorial](https://holodomormuseum.org.ua/en/zibrannya/33rd-hunger-people-s-book-memorial/). Opened 2026-11-22. Read the compilation’s scope and place-based organization. The page citation for the Kostyantinopil account is from Hedo and Xenophontos.

- **S6:** [Heorhii Papakin, Chorna doshka: Antyselianski represii (1932–1933)](https://history.org.ua/LiberUA/978-966-02-7023-7/978-966-02-7023-7.pdf). Published 2013. Opened 2026-11-22. Read the printed pages 160–162 on the preliminary proposal and the later decision. The book cites the underlying record. Its original archival image was not inspected.

- **S7:** [Holodomor Digital Archives, Holodomor 1932–1933 rokiv v Ukraini: dokumenty i materialy](https://holodomor-era.history.org.ua/item/0001150). Published 2007. Opened 2026-11-22. Read the document descriptions indexed to Ruslan Pyrih’s 2007 collection, including records on blacklisting and its implementation.
