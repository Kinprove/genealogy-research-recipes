# Sources for katiha-ceded-parish-record-recovery

- **S1:** [Kurkistus Katihaan](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2022/06/kurkistus-katihaan.html), web-page, read. Post read 2026-11-22. Kaisa Kyläkoski compared burial and communion-book results from Sortavala. She used Digihakemisto to place the book in time and ran an advanced volume-and-page search after the person view returned only the selected entry.

- **S2:** [National Archives of Finland, Karjala-tietokanta ja Katiha](https://katiha.kansallisarkisto.fi/tietoa.php), help-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Explains database derivation, the public and reading-room access split and the death-data exception.

- **S3:** [National Archives of Finland, Hakuohjeet](https://katiha.kansallisarkisto.fi/ohjeet.php), help-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Documents parish and church-book filters, volume and page fields, transcription and irregular corrections.

- **S4:** [Lisää "uudistuksia" Kansallisarkiston verkkopalveluissa](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2022/03/lisaa-uudistuksia-kansallisarkiston.html), web-page, read. Post read 2026-11-22. The 2022 post reproduces a release announcement naming Petsamo and reports the winding down of data entry in the earlier Katiha.xamk service.

- **S5:** [Astia herättää tunteita](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2022/04/astia-herattaa-tunteita.html), web-page, read. Post read 2026-11-22. The author reproduces the 2022 notice about reading-room access and a provisional web arrangement. The archive's own current instructions supersede that web address.

- **S6:** [National Archives of Finland, Tarkennettu haku](https://katiha.kansallisarkisto.fi/haku.php), web-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. The public advanced-search form identifies available parish and record-type filters.

- **S7:** [Genealogical Society of Finland, Kirkonkirjat](https://www.genealogia.fi/kirkonkirjat/), help-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Describes Katiha as a church-book search aid for ceded areas and Digihakemisto as an index to National Archives material.

- **S8:** [National Archives of Finland, Karjala-tietokannasta puuttuvat aineistot](https://katiha.kansallisarkisto.fi/puuttuvat.php), catalogue, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Identifies church-book materials omitted from the index by parish and series.
