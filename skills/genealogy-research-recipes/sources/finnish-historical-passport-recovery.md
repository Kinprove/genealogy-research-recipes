# Sources for Find a historical Finnish travel or sea passport

- **S1:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Passikooste](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2019/06/hajatietoja-matkapasseista.html). Passport roundup read 2026-11-22. The author reports inspecting the opening private journeys and later maritime entries of the Helsinki magistrate’s Päiväkirjat, passit 1756–1875. The post also names the Kymenkartano files and cites lost-property notices. The archive’s Helsinki volume was not inspected in this review.

- **S2:** Finnish National Archives, [Merenkulkuun liittyvät asiakirjat](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/merenkulkuun-liittyvat-asiakirjat). Meripassit and ship-record sections read 2026-11-22. Used for the issuer, possible entries, return of the document, related drafts and sporadic survival.

- **S3:** Digihakemisto, [Kymenkartanon lääninhallituksen lääninkanslian arkisto – Ee Passiasiakirjat 1751–1831](https://digihakemisto.net/aineisto/1704347823). Series listing read 2026-11-22. Used for the Ee locator and listed units. The contents of the individual files were not inspected.

- **S4:** Finnish National Archives, [Hollikyytiasiakirjat](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/hollikyytiasiakirjat). Transport-record guide read 2026-11-22. Used for the posting-inn and relay records as a separate search.
