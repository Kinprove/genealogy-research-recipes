**What is authored and what is not verified.** The step order, interval calculation, sweep log,
candidate-spouse table and output packet are authored synthesis. The sources supply the field
meanings, search behaviours and case examples. Registration deadlines, the 1874 Act's start
date and late-registration provisions, the 1912 spouse-surname start, FreeBMD's start quarter
and the GRO's search limits were checked against the authorities listed below. The GRO online
index's year coverage, the local-index route and the Isle of Man and Channel Islands routing
were checked against the official and archive pages cited as S24–S32. The marriage-index end
years and the route for ordering a marriage without a reference were checked against S34–S37.
The required search fields, the meaning of a dash, the quarters from which the printed index
added fields and the display of the maiden surname for modern births were checked against
S38–S40. Not checked: ordering prices, current aggregator displays, and the year from which
the GRO online index first gives ages at death (S41). No coding for under-one ages at death or
FreeBMD wildcard syntax is adopted.

### Sources added on 2026-11-14

The English Ancestors posts below (S47–S51) prompted the rules added on 2026-11-14; each rule is
checked against the official or archive source cited beside it. Ordering prices and the PDF and
image services were checked on 2026-11-14 against S24 and S42, which supersedes "Not checked:
ordering prices" above.

- **S42:** GOV.UK, General Register Office, [How to order and pay for civil registration records (accessible)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/how-to-order-and-pay-for-certificates/how-to-order-and-pay-for-civil-registration-records-accessible), guidance updated 2025-09-13, read 2026-11-14. Used for: the prices (£12.50 for a full certificate ordered online with an index reference, £8.00 for a PDF, £3.00 for an Online View digital image); the PDF and Online View services for births from 1837 up to 100 years ago (the PDF section says over 100 years old) and deaths from 1837 to 1957, each needing a GRO index reference and ordered only online; an Online View image removed 3 months after the order without a reminder; and a PDF having "no evidential value", so that a paper certificate is needed for official purposes such as a passport.
- **S43:** Paternoster family history, [Birth certificates](https://paternoster.orpheusweb.co.uk/famhistory/bmd/birthcerts.htm), undated, read 2026-11-14. Used for: parents slightly adjusting a birth date to avoid the late-registration fine, exposed only by a baptism recording the true date or held before the stated birth date; and inaccurate information sometimes supplied for the father. A personal family-history site.
- **S44:** Rosemary Lockie, Wishful Thinking, [A History of Local Registration Offices in England and Wales](https://places.wishful-thinking.org.uk/LocalRegistration.html), undated, read 2026-11-14. Used for: the GRO's records as quarterly copies of the local registers; the original registers kept by the local register offices, whose certificates are made from them; and ministers acting as proxy registrars for church marriages while keeping the church's own register. A local-history site.
- **S45:** Gloucestershire Archives, [Civil certificates](https://www.gloucestershire.gov.uk/archives/researching/search-by-theme/courts-and-criminals/civil-certificates), undated, read 2026-11-14. Used for: Church of England marriage registers including the same information as the marriage certificate.
- **S46:** Who Do You Think You Are? Magazine, [Marriage certificates: Everything you need to know](https://www.whodoyouthinkyouaremagazine.com/feature/marriage-certificates/), 2026-11-06, read 2026-11-14. Used for: the GRO certificate as a copy of the register that shows no actual signatures, only whether each person could sign or marked an X.
- **S47:** English Ancestors blog, [Hierarchies of evidence and thinking outside the box](https://englishancestors.blog/2024/08/09/hierarchies-of-evidence-and-thinking-outside-the-box/), 2024-08-09, read 2026-11-14. Used for: birth certificates that are not entirely true, with a later birth date given to avoid the late-entry fee and named fathers who are not the biological father. A community blog post. Its suggestion that grandmothers registered a young daughter's child as their own was not confirmed and is not used.
- **S48:** English Ancestors blog, [The Genealogical Proof Standard](https://englishancestors.blog/2026/07/11/the-genealogical-proof-standard/), 2026-07-11, read 2026-11-14. Used for: candidate marriages with no digital image or download, so that each would have needed a £12.50 certificate. A community blog post. Its "£3 digital download" is the Online View image, a view rather than a PDF (S42).
- **S49:** English Ancestors blog, [Instant-access Birth and Death certificate images](https://englishancestors.blog/2023/07/15/instant-access-birth-and-death-certificate-images/), 2023-07-15, read 2026-11-14. Used for: the digital image as only the extract from the GRO register, its use for the causes of death of siblings who died in infancy, and the GRO's statement that the images have no evidential value. A community blog post. Its July 2023 price (£2.50) and death range (to 1887) are out of date (S24, S42), and its calling a GRO certified copy a secondary source is replaced here by derivative copy.
- **S50:** English Ancestors blog, [Do you need to buy the Civil BMDs?](https://englishancestors.blog/2023/02/16/do-you-need-to-buy-the-civil-bmds/), 2023-02-16, read 2026-11-14. Used for: the Church of England register image as a substitute for the marriage certificate, its signatures or marks for later comparison, and civil certificates bought for Catholic and nonconformist marriages, whose registers are rarely online. A community blog post.
- **S51:** English Ancestors blog, [Creating a template for GRO Birth and Death digital download images](https://englishancestors.blog/2023/08/01/creating-a-template-for-gro-birth-and-death-digital-download-images/), 2023-08-01, read 2026-11-14. Used for: images available for three months after ordering, and a citation template that records the GRO reference line (year to page) from the order details with the downloaded image. A community blog post; its menu path on the GRO site is not repeated.
