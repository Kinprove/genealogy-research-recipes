## Sources

- **S1:** [Your Guide to Finding Loyalist Ancestors in the American Revolution](https://familytreemagazine.com/us/find-loyalist-ancestors-american-revolution/), published 2026-04-22. Community guide to wartime, compensation, evacuation and resettlement sources. Opened 2026-11-22 UTC.
- **S2:** [Archives of Ontario, United Empire Loyalists research guide 227](https://www.archives.gov.on.ca/ao-content/media/uploads/2025/08/research_guide_227_united_empire_loyalists-1-1.pdf). AO 12 and AO 13, their contents and their alternate indexes and copies. Opened 2026-11-22 UTC.
- **S3:** [Library and Archives Canada, Loyalists](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/loyalists.html). Federal archival guide to Loyalist regiments, migration and resettlement records. Opened 2026-11-22 UTC.
- **S4:** [Library and Archives Canada, Land records for Upper and Lower Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/land-records/petitions-boards-upper-lower-canada.html). Petition indexes, related records and image and film routes. Opened 2026-11-22 UTC.
- **S5:** [Timothy M. Barnes, Loyalist Newspapers of the American Revolution 1763–1783](https://americanantiquarian.org/proceedings/44517562.pdf). Author’s bibliography organized by colony and city, with issue dates and title histories. Opened 2026-11-22 UTC.
- **S6:** [Nova Scotia Archives, Book of Negroes](https://archives.novascotia.ca/africanns/book-of-negroes/). British list images and history, dates, route and locator for the parallel American list. Opened 2026-11-22 UTC.
- **S7:** [University of New Brunswick, Researching a Loyalist](https://loyalist.lib.unb.ca/sites/default/files/2026-03/ResearchALoyalist_1.pdf). Finding aids and nominal indexes used to locate claim and resettlement records. Opened 2026-11-22 UTC.
