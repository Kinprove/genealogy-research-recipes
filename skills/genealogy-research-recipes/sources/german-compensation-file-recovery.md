**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The authored synthesis is the hit-register layout, the separation of roles, the namesake ranking rule and the routing of residences. The forum threads supply the search routes for Lastenausgleich and WGA files, the field and series names as they appear on screen, the reports of file contents and the experience of making requests. All of these are participant reports. Several statements in S1 come from the same few participants, and S3 quotes S1 for the LAA notice, so the threads are not fully independent. The dated official references S4–S10 identify the statute, portal and archive pages used for the legal, custody and procedure facts. The pilot-announcement date and unbound catalogue date range are not retained as verified facts. The BEG routes and the Berlin custody facts come from the official pages S5–S7, not from the threads. Processing times and costs are individual experiences, not current terms.

- **S11:** Lastenausgleichsgesetz (LAG), [§ 234](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/lag/__234.html), gesetze-im-internet.de, read 2026-11-12. Used for: subsection 4, under which applications for Ausgleichsleistungen could be made only until 31 December 1995, and no later than three years after the right to apply arose, subject to exceptions in §§ 264 and 265 that the subsection names. This entry corrects two earlier statements: the S4 note, which described § 234 only by its subsection 2 rule (relatives' applications for a prisoner of war, internee or missing person until 31 December 1970), and the recipe sentence that called the 1995 date unconfirmed.
- **S12:** Lastenausgleichsgesetz (LAG), [§ 244](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/lag/__244.html), gesetze-im-internet.de, checked 2026-11-07 as part of S4, read again 2026-11-12. Used for: the claim to Hauptentschädigung being inheritable and transferable.
- **S13:** Landesarchiv Berlin, [Entschädigung](https://landesarchiv-berlin.de/archiv-nutzen/personen-familienforschung/entschaedigung/), read 2026-11-12. It appears to replace the first page of S7, whose address answered 404 on that day. Used for: up to ten files a calendar year digitised free of charge for relatives of people persecuted by the Nazis, or people they authorise, for research into the persecution; the same quota covering files from other holdings, such as Wiedergutmachung or prison files, connected with the persecution; and fees under the archive's fee schedule, with a quote in advance, for other scans and digital copies.
