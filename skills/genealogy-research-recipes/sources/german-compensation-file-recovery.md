## Sources

- **S1:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Frage zu Akten Lastenausgleich (Bundesarchiv Bayreuth)](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/156210/), thread started 2019-12-22. Used for: the invenio person search versus the general search; the ZLA 1–3 limit; opening the result tree; entering through bundesarchiv.de; the Feststellungsakten Bestand and its identifying fields; the same-region namesake; place-level items such as a village plan; people inside a file not being indexed; ordering by exact signature; the application and access-review forms; participants' experience of the pilot questionnaire and suggested wording for a legitimate interest; dated request sequences; file contents by applicant role; applicants' declarations about kin; witness statements; postwar residence and death details as leads.
- **S2:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [a thread on a Berlin and Silesian family search](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/2991761/), thread started 2026-02-01. Used for: the Landesarchiv Berlin findbuch search with `wga` plus surname; the layout of a WGA title (signature in B Rep. 025-xx, injured party, address, B Rep. 032 trustee cross-reference, WGA number); several same-name cases; a zero-hit query with a given name; file size and paid digitised copies. The thread's statement that the Einwohnermeldekartei survives only from 1948 was not used, because the Landesarchiv Berlin describes the index differently.
- **S3:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [a thread on a great-grandfather search](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/3014348/), thread started 2026-05-12. Used for: an indexed name that may belong to an heir's claim; legal successors as applicants and the 1995 cut-off a participant gave; a file running to 2014; the suggested relative's-fate category.

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The authored synthesis is the hit-register layout, the separation of roles, the namesake ranking rule and the routing of residences. The forum threads supply the search routes, the field and series names as they appear on screen, the reports of file contents and the experience of making requests. All of these are participant reports. Several statements in S1 come from the same few participants, and S3 quotes S1 for the LAA notice, so the threads are not fully independent. The statute texts, the Bundesarchiv notices, the WGA database closure, the Bayreuth reading-room terms, the Einwohnermeldekartei coverage and the ZLA 1 title were checked at the source: § 14 BArchG and § 234 LAG (gesetze-im-internet.de); the Bundesarchiv notices and Bayreuth site page; Archivportal-D; the Facts & Files WGA project page; and the Landesarchiv Berlin's description of the Einwohnermeldekartei. Processing times and costs are individual experiences, not current terms.
