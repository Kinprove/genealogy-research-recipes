**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The authored synthesis is the hit-register layout, the separation of roles, the namesake ranking rule and the routing of residences. The forum threads supply the search routes for Lastenausgleich and WGA files, the field and series names as they appear on screen, the reports of file contents and the experience of making requests. All of these are participant reports. Several statements in S1 come from the same few participants, and S3 quotes S1 for the LAA notice, so the threads are not fully independent. The dated official references S4–S16 identify the statute, portal, catalogue and archive pages used for the legal, custody and procedure facts. The pilot-announcement date and unbound catalogue date range are not retained as verified facts. The BEG routes and the Berlin custody facts come from the official pages S5–S7, S13 and S14, not from the threads. Processing times and costs are individual experiences, not current terms.
