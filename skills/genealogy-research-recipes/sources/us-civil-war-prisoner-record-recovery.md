## Sources

- **S1:** [Genealogy Just Ask, Civil War POWs and the Genealogist – JG0081](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C6jwgKhEBo0), published 2022-05-28. Supplied transcript excerpts read 2026-11-22. Community presentation. Used for the suggested camp-by-camp timeline and as leads to parole rules and individual series. Direct playback was throttled. The independently checked sources below establish the rules and locators.
- **S2:** [Dix–Hill Cartel, 22 July 1862, NC State University transcription](https://cwnc.omeka.chass.ncsu.edu/items/show/611). Read 2026-11-22. Used for the exchange scale, status of civilians, parole timing, ranks, duties and delivery terms.
- **S3:** [General Orders No. 100, 24 April 1863, Yale Avalon Project transcription](https://avalon.law.yale.edu/19th_century/lieber.asp). Read 2026-11-22. Used for articles 105–110 and the conditions for parole and exchange.
- **S4:** [National Archives, War Department Collection of Confederate Records (RG 109)](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/109.html). Read 2026-11-22. Used for prison, oath, parole and transferred-prisoner records and the M598 finding route.
- **S5:** [National Archives, Confederate Prisoners of War, reference report 918](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/military/civil-war/confederate/confederate-pows.pdf). Read 2026-11-22. Used for the M598 structure, Point Lookout and Rock Island rolls, M918 burial information and the seven rolls of M1781.
- **S6:** [National Archives, Records of the Commissary General of Prisoners (RG 249)](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/249.html). Read 2026-11-22. Used for Federal prisoners, escape and exchange registers, parole camps, Andersonville and Confederate prisoner morning reports.
- **S7:** [National Archives, Records of United States Army Continental Commands (RG 393)](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/393.html). Read 2026-11-22. Used for the installation and parole camp route in section 393.13.
- **S8:** [National Archives, Microfilm Publications and Original Records Digitized by Our Digitization Partners](https://www.archives.gov/digitization/digitized-by-partners). Read 2026-11-22. Used for online M598 partner listings, including Ancestry and FamilySearch.
- **S9:** [FamilySearch, United States, Records of Confederate Prisoners of War, 1861–1865](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1916234). Read 2026-11-22. Used for the collection search, its volume guide and listed types of registers.
- **S10:** [Ancestry Paths, Civil War Confederate Prisoners, 1861 to 1865](https://www.ancestrypaths.com/military-records/civil-war-confederate-prisoners-1861-to-1865/). Read 2026-11-22. Used for its M598 roll-level transcription, checked against National Archives' camp spans. The individual roll images were not inspected.
- **S11:** [National Archives, Military Service in the U.S. Volunteer Infantry, Galvanized Yankees](https://www.archives.gov/files/research/military/civil-war/galvanized-yankees.pdf). Read 2026-11-22. Used for six U.S. Volunteer Infantry regiments, later service and available CMSRs.
- **S12:** [Ohio State University eHistory, Official Records of the Civil War](https://ehistory.osu.edu/books/war-rebellion-official-records-civil-war). Read 2026-11-22. Used for the eight volumes in Series II and the route to the published documents.
- **S13:** [National Archives, The Sultana Disaster, April 1865, M1878 descriptive pamphlet](https://www.fold3.com/pdf/M1878.pdf). Read 2026-11-22. Used for the selected dead and survivor lists drawn from RG 249 entry 98. The pamphlet is available from Fold3; its linked images were not tested.
- **S14:** [U.S. War Department, correspondence printed in Message of the President of the United States: Exchange of prisoners](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/a/aa/Message_of_the_President_of_the_United_States_-_%28with%2C%29_Exchange_of_prisoners_%3B_%28and%2C%29_One_hundred_days%27_volunteers_%3B_%28and%2C%29_Certain_amounts_paid_for_commutation_%28IA_messageofpreside3162linc%29.pdf). Read 2026-11-22. Read the correspondence identifying the 22 July 1862 cartel and General Orders No. 142 of 25 September 1862. The same correspondence distinguishes the cartel's formal status from periods when its operation was suspended.
- **S15:** [National Archives, M1017 descriptive pamphlet](https://www.fold3.com/pdf/M1017.pdf). Read 2026-11-22. Read for the six U.S. Volunteer Infantry regiments and their compiled-service-record jackets. This is a pamphlet hosted by Fold3.
