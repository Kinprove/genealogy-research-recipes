### Notes on the additions of 2026-11-15

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The rules that a land-status label does not change the household, that a run of state-peasant labels is one household, that a passport note names the next society to search, the order of searching an odnodvorets origin, the treasury chamber's journals as the route for a transfer, the reading of an «Р» fond and of a Part 2 entry as acquired status, the use of the sloboda book's revision numbers, the treatment of a foreign subject's «родом из» as testimony, and the split between printed genealogies as leads and the assembly's files as proof are authored synthesis. S21, S22, S25 to S27, S30, S34, S37, S39, S41, S42, S44, S45, S50 and S51 are Dzen posts by two genealogy channels and report their authors' own cases, mostly from Tula guberniya; they show what was found there, not a general rule. S23, S24, S28, S29, S31 to S33, S43, S46 to S49, S54 and S55 are reference works; S35, S36, S38, S40 and S52 are catalogue listings, an index and a researcher's notes; S53 is a scholarly article read only in its abstract.

The following were NOT verified: the passport rules before the regulation of 1894; whether the treasury chamber's journals survive for other provinces; the whole contents of GATO f. Р-1905; the contents of the RGIA files on admission to subjecthood; and whether every sloboda book uses the same column headings.
