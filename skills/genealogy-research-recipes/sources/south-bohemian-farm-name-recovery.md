# Trace a South Bohemian family across farm-name changes — sources

- **S1:** [Svobodný, Adamec, Janovský, nebo Laaf? Jak naši předci měnili příjmení](https://medium.seznam.cz/clanek/blanka-lednicka-svobodny-adamec-janovsky-nebo-laaf-jak-nasi-predci-menili-prijmeni-310345). Published 2026-08-22. Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Community research case by Blanka Lednická. The Bošilec example connects a transfer, an estate list and a marriage across different names. The reported delays and age discrepancies describe individual entries.
- **S2:** [DigiArchiv SOA Třeboň, Třeboň estate land books](https://digi.ceskearchivy.cz/tree.php?doctree=1qatr&id=700012&sa=0). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Official land-register catalogue. Used for the estate and the earlier Bošilec and Lhota volume.
- **S3:** [DigiArchiv SOA Třeboň, Třeboň estate land books, continuation](https://digi.ceskearchivy.cz/tree.php?doctree=1qatr&id=118355&sa=0). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Official land-register catalogue. Used for the later Bošilec volume and its overlapping date coverage.
- **S4:** [DigiArchiv SOA Třeboň, Třeboň estate lists of subjects](https://digi.ceskearchivy.cz/tree.php?doctree=1taw&id=50016&sa=0). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Official estate-register catalogue. Used to locate the Schwarzenberg lists by manor, village and date.
