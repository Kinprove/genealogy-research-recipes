**Authored synthesis.** The following is not taken from any one presenter: the order of the search, the separate-field record, the coverage log, the "local context only" label, the attribution format, and extending the finding-aid caution beyond BYU. The presenters supply the examples and the failure modes. The portals in the former-Soviet memorial-portal section were checked against S8–S10. The aerial-image section combines the historical examples in S11–S13 and S15 with the existing archival image coverage procedure; it does not introduce a new reverse-checking method. The resident-versus-owner distinction preserves separate evidence for the two questions. The Riddarhuset procedure combines S14's named search surfaces with existing coverage and attribution controls; its branch/object comparison and linked card/front/back record are original procedural synthesis. It establishes no person-level cross-service link or genealogical-document workflow. The records-that-carry-a-portrait section collects the record types in S16–S27 into one surface list; the rule that the record attributes its photograph and the portal privacy rule are Kinprove synthesis, the latter matching the burial recipe's rule against collecting grave-holders' contacts. Employee identity files are not listed because no page opened states that they carry photographs.
