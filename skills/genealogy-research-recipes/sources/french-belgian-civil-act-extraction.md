The following are authored synthesis: the field table, the three-way classification of how a death statement is supported, the "kept/superseded" handling of corrections, checking month names against register order, and the rule that a witness attestation establishes only what the act records, with any register search limited to what the act states. The sources supply the reading techniques and the worked examples. Not verified: independent documentation of the *je dis* formula, how the calendar was applied in specific communes, how converters other than napoleon.org behave, and how general the wording patterns the presenter describes really are.

and bounded search result are synthesis. No official Paris inventory, reconstructed-act
image or underlying source was inspected for this addition. The loss date and the
one-third figure come from the Archives de Paris (S6), not from the article. The
article's online year spans, exact annual-table exception, blanket loss/survival
narrative and named family conclusions are not adopted as verified claims.
