### Assize court sources

- **S19:** Jessica Morgan, Family Locket, [Without the Parish: Researching in England Without Baptisms, Marriages, and Burials Part 4- Court Records](https://familylocket.com/without-the-parish-researching-in-england-without-baptisms-marriages-and-burials-part-4-court-records/), published 2024-07-06, read 2026-11-12. Used for: assizes hearing cases too serious for quarter sessions, and the indictments, depositions and gaol books among their records. Its circuit list (including Middlesex and Berkshire moving to the Oxford circuit in 1540) and its treatment of Ancestry's criminal registers and the Old Bailey website as assize records are not used; S20 and S21 replace them.
- **S20:** The National Archives, [Criminal court cases: assize courts 1559-1971](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/criminal-trials-assize-courts-1559-1971/), read 2026-11-12. Used for: records arranged by circuit in ASSI series, found through county appendices; coverage from 1559 for England and 1831 for Wales; six circuits up to 1876; Cheshire joining in 1830 and Durham and Lancaster in 1876, with earlier trials in palatinate court records; the National Library of Wales Crime and Punishment database for Welsh trials of 1543–1830; crown and gaol books as the starting point; depositions surviving mainly in capital cases; records available only in original form at Kew or as copies.
- **S21:** The National Archives, [Criminal court cases: Old Bailey (Central Criminal Court)](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/help-with-your-research/research-guides/criminal-court-cases-old-bailey-the-central-criminal-court/), read 2026-11-12. Used for: the court trying City of London and Middlesex cases before 1834 and, as the Central Criminal Court from 1834, also parts of Essex, Kent and Surrey; its CRIM series; earlier London and Middlesex sessions records at the London Archives.
