## Sources

Third-party sources are cited references, paraphrased rather than reproduced.
The index-audit, locator and series-resolution schemas are original synthesis.

- **S1:** Aimee Cross,
  [Deeds: How to Use Them in Genealogy Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X8NNeexlV8E),
  24 February 2022. Both index directions, recording versus instrument dates and varied index keys.
- **S2:** Family Locket,
  [RLP 49: U.S. Land Records Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KvJ5YmeIHvI),
  17 June 2019. Alternative-entry reconciliation, index-reference logging and a misread page range.
- **S3:** Family Locket,
  [Treasure Hunt at the Love County Courthouse: Uncovering Land Records](https://familylocket.com/treasure-hunt-at-the-love-county-courthouse-uncovering-land-records/),
  11 August 2025. Tract indexes, a certificate in a miscellaneous book and an East/West locator conflict.
- **S4:** BYU Family History Library, John Hendrix,
  [U.S. Land Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gWPPN9DUUSc),
  29 April 2016. Delayed recording, both party indexes and recorded deed-book copies.
- **S5:** BYU Family History Library, James Tanner,
  [When the Baby was Born, the Mother was There and other Research Rules](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gssWfSl8JwY),
  12 October 2016. Abstract and title-company records as a separately held source when county land
  records were destroyed.
- **S6:** Genealogy Gems,
  [AUDIO PODCAST Episode 269 Virginia Genealogy Strategies and 7 Best Websites](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zSx_9MrKP0M),
  17 October 2022. Deed books as clerk copies of returned originals, and re-recording of surviving
  originals after courthouse fires.
- **S7:** Genealogy Gems,
  [Genealogy Gems Podcast Episode 218 How to Find Your Family History](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V0he2PdYk2k),
  14 June 2018. Pennsylvania warrant/survey records followed by county deeds and a later patent,
  plus adjoining owners' deeds to fill gaps left by unrecorded deeds.
- **S8:** BYU Family History Library, Maureen Brady,
  [Family History Research in Illinois and Wisconsin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kFxsAw8HHAs),
  30 January 2026. The presenter describes party-only indexes; verify the actual index's coverage
  before interpreting a negative, and use full-text or document reading for other named people.
- **S9:** BYU Family History Library, James Tanner,
  [Metes & Bounds on Google Earth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gci5qXFBtrQ),
  22 May 2015; with Family History Fanatics,
  [3 MUST Search Genealogy Records to Find US Ancestors Before 1850](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CjjD9mI_dY8),
  23 August 2019. Separate disclaimer deeds and end-of-deed dower releases that name the grantor's
  wife and the date of her examination.
- **S10:** Dana Leeds,
  [My First-Hand Experience and Top 10 Tips for Researching at NARA](https://www.danaleeds.com/10_tips_for_nara/),
  8 December 2023. BLM-linked patent information, land-office/document-number preparation and a
  historical federal-land locator worksheet; historical visit and request-form procedures are not
  a current access contract, and present request forms, appointments and retrieval services were
  not verified.
- **S11:** Dana Leeds,
  [NARA Land Records: Discovering Family History Details That Aren’t Online](https://www.danaleeds.com/nara-land-records-discovering-family-history-details-that-arent-online/),
  14 December 2023. Reported cash-sale, bounty-land and homestead packet components, certificate
  reverses, person roles and distinct event dates. Original files were not independently inspected.
  The recipe does not adopt fixed page counts or page-count ranges, statutory summaries or
  eligibility, the author's family, kinship or service conclusions, or current digitization claims.
- **S12:** Amy, Legacy Tree Genealogists Editor,
  [Women Homesteaders and the Homestead Act: 160 Acres of Independence](https://www.legacytree.com/blog/women-and-the-homestead-act),
  5 May 2026. Patent-to-separate-homestead-file distinction and supplied locator leads;
  no current holdings or request outcome is established by this reference.
- **S15:** Family Locket,
  [RLP 315: Tips for When the Courthouse Burned](https://familylocket.com/rlp-315-tips-for-when-the-courthouse-burned/).
  A reported tax entry recovered under an estate administrator's surname motivates the
  alternate filing-key check. Original tax and land records were not independently inspected;
  the recipe does not adopt the account's death, sale, namesake-identity or legal conclusions.
- **S16:** Nicole Elder Dyer, Family Locket,
  [RLP 329: Using AI in Report Writing](https://familylocket.com/rlp-329-using-ai-in-report-writing/),

  independently inspected; the example supplies no universal tax classes or liability rules.
- **S17:** Family Locket,
  [RLP 50: U.S. Land Records Part 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fEuF00FwZN8),
  24 June 2019. Reported tract-register route to entries that did not produce a patent.
  The speaker describes declined neighboring entries, not recovery of a rejected Royston file.
  Original registers and files were not inspected; universal coverage, survival, locator
  sufficiency, current access and legal or family conclusions are not adopted.
- **S18:** BYU Family History Library, James Tanner,
  [State-land States vs Public Land States for Genealogists - James Tanner](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0fFPtZb5TQw),

  [RLP 196: Texas Land Grants – Republic and Statehood](https://familylocket.com/rlp-196-texas-land-grants-republic-and-statehood/),

- **S20:** Nicole Elder Dyer, Family Locket,
  [RLP 230: Finding a Female’s Father – Research Log and Report](https://familylocket.com/rlp-230-finding-a-females-father-research-log-and-report/),

  stated residence or identity.
- **S21:** Alice Childs, Family Locket,
  [Part 4: Pennsylvania Germans: The Land Acquisition Process in Colonial Pennsylvania](https://familylocket.com/part-4-pennsylvania-germans-the-land-acquisition-process-in-colonial-pennsylvania/),

- **S22:** Roberta Estes, DNAeXplained,
  [Tracking John Dobkins or Dobbins to Maryland – 52 Ancestors #378](https://dna-explained.com/2022/10/16/tracking-john-dobkins-or-dobbins-to-maryland-52-ancestors-378/),
