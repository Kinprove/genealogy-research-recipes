## Sources

Third-party sources are cited references, paraphrased rather than reproduced.
The index-audit, locator and series-resolution schemas are original synthesis.

- **S1:** Aimee Cross,
  [Deeds: How to Use Them in Genealogy Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X8NNeexlV8E),
  24 February 2022. Both index directions, recording versus instrument dates and varied index keys.
- **S2:** Family Locket,
  [RLP 49: U.S. Land Records Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KvJ5YmeIHvI),
  17 June 2019. Alternative-entry reconciliation, index-reference logging and a misread page range.
- **S3:** Family Locket,
  [Treasure Hunt at the Love County Courthouse: Uncovering Land Records](https://familylocket.com/treasure-hunt-at-the-love-county-courthouse-uncovering-land-records/),
  11 August 2025. Tract indexes, a certificate in a miscellaneous book and an East/West locator conflict.
- **S4:** BYU Family History Library, John Hendrix,
  [U.S. Land Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gWPPN9DUUSc),
  29 April 2016. Delayed recording, both party indexes and recorded deed-book copies.
- **S5:** Dana Leeds,
  [10 Tips for NARA](https://www.danaleeds.com/10_tips_for_nara/),
  8 December 2023. Historical federal-land locator worksheet; present request forms,
  appointments and retrieval services were not verified.
- **S6:** Dana Leeds,
  [NARA Land Records: Discovering Family History Details That Aren’t Online](https://www.danaleeds.com/nara-land-records-discovering-family-history-details-that-arent-online/),
  14 December 2023. Reported cash-sale, bounty-warrant and homestead packet components.
  Original files were not independently inspected. The recipe does not adopt page-count
  ranges, statutory eligibility, case kinship/service conclusions or current digitization claims.
- **S7:** Family Locket,
  [RLP 315: Tips for When the Courthouse Burned](https://familylocket.com/rlp-315-tips-for-when-the-courthouse-burned/).
  A reported tax entry recovered under an estate administrator's surname motivates the
  alternate filing-key check. Original tax and land records were not independently inspected;
  the recipe does not adopt the account's death, sale, namesake-identity or legal conclusions.
- **S8:** Nicole Elder Dyer, Family Locket,
  [RLP 329: Using AI in Report Writing](https://familylocket.com/rlp-329-using-ai-in-report-writing/),
  28 October 2024. Reported annual volume/section reorganization and differing filing keys
  motivate the tax-coverage inventory. Original tax images, headings and holdings were not
  independently inspected; the example supplies no universal tax classes or liability rules.

- **S9:** Family Locket,
  [RLP 50: U.S. Land Records Part 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fEuF00FwZN8),
  24 June 2019. Reported tract-register route to entries that did not produce a patent.
  The speaker describes declined neighboring entries, not recovery of a rejected Royston file.
  Original registers and files were not inspected; universal coverage, survival, locator
  sufficiency, current access and legal or family conclusions are not adopted.
