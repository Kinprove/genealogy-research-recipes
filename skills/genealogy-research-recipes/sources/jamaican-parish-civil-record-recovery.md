# Sources for jamaican-parish-civil-record-recovery

- **S1:** [A Genealogist’s Research Guide to the Caribbean](https://familytreemagazine.com/heritage/a-genealogists-research-guide-to-the-caribbean/), web-page, read. Post read 2026-11-22. Family Tree Magazine points readers to civil and denominational routes and suggests the National Library of Jamaica and Mona Library. Its old Registrar General’s Department name is superseded by the current authority’s name.

- **S2:** [FamilySearch, Jamaica, Church of England Parish Register Transcripts, 1664–1880](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1827268), catalogue, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Describes the parish transcripts and their collection scope.

- **S3:** [Jamaican Family Search, Jamaican Parish Registers microfilm list](https://www.jamaicanfamilysearch.com/Samples/fhclist.htm), catalogue, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Identifies the separately catalogued Dissenter marriage transcripts and the volumes mixed into the Anglican image collection.

- **S4:** [Library of Congress, Jamaica Genealogy Resource Guide: Civil Registration](https://guides.loc.gov/jamaica-local-history-genealogy/civil-registration), help-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Distinguishes the civil start dates by event and identifies NIRA as the successor to the Registrar General’s Department.

- **S5:** [Government of Jamaica, National Identification and Registration Authority](https://nira.gov.jm/), web-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Identifies NIRA as responsible for civil registration and lists birth, marriage and death certificate services.

- **S6:** [National Library of Jamaica, Frequently Asked Questions](https://nlj.gov.jm/faq/), help-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Lists newspapers, manuscripts and maps in the national collection.

- **S7:** [University of the West Indies, Mona Library, Special Collections](https://www.mona.uwi.edu/library/special-collections), catalogue, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Describes map and historical newspaper holdings. It does not identify a specific Maroon record series.
