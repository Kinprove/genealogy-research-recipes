### Notes on the additions of 2026-11-15

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** Searching titles by place and estate, searching both levels of the recruitment and military-service boards, reading each household form for whom it names, naming members from the metrical books as an inference, reading a «старожил» mark as a lead, treating scouts' papers as records of the households represented, treating a volost petition as a request, opening every file of a series whatever its title, and reading the brothers' later call-up entries are authored synthesis. S32, S35, S36, S38, S41 to S43, S46, S48, S51 and S52 are Dzen posts by individual genealogists about their own cases, mostly in Tula guberniya; they show what was found there, not a general rule. S33, S34 and S44 are reference works and a guide; S37 is an article on one regional archive; S39, S40, S45 and S47 are a legal news article, a scholarly commentary, a newspaper report and a museum page; S49 and S50 are catalogue listings.

The following were NOT verified: the column numbers of call-up lists in any year; the abbreviations used on census cards outside Nizhny Novgorod; whether scouts' papers survive in a given archive; whether other volost executive committee fonds file their marriage papers the same way; and the detailed fields of the inspection journals beyond the Starodub fond's description.
