### Notes on S32–S52

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** Searching titles by place and estate, searching both levels of the recruitment and military-service boards, reading each household form for whom it names, naming members from the metrical books as an inference, reading a «старожил» mark as a lead, treating scouts' papers as records of the households represented, treating a volost petition as a request, opening every file of a series whatever its title, and reading the brothers' later call-up entries are authored synthesis.
