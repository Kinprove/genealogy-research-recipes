## Sources

- **S1:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, community blog, [Yhteisiä merkkipäiviä vuonna 1814](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2025/02/yhteisia-merkkipaivia-vuonna-1814.html). Published 2025-02-02. Read 2026-11-22. The author compares the 1814 Turku and Porvoo circulars with local newspaper and diary evidence, including a service that preceded its Turku circular and the May Sota-Sanomat enclosure.
- **S2:** National Archives of Finland via Finna, [Turku printed diocesan circulars, B III:4](https://www.finna.fi/Record/narc.VAKKA-202511.KA_VAKKA-153384.KA). Read 2026-11-22. Archive catalogue for circulars 131–159, April 1808 through the end of 1812. The catalogue verifies the volume and number range.
- **S3:** National Archives of Finland via Finna, [Turku printed diocesan circulars, B III:5](https://www.finna.fi/Record/narc.VAKKA-202511.KA_VAKKA-153407.KA). Read 2026-11-22. Archive catalogue for circulars 160–189, February 1813 through 1818.
- **S4:** National Archives of Finland via Finna, [Hornborg's Sammandrag, II Eb:2](https://www.finna.fi/Record/narc.VAKKA-223180.KA_VAKKA-2005563.KA). Read 2026-11-22. Archives catalogue for the 1872 abstract of previously unprinted Porvoo cathedral chapter circulars from 1725 to 1829.
- **S5:** National Library of Finland, [Anders Johan Sjögren: Allmänna Ephemerider. Dagböckerna 1806–1855](https://kansalliskirjasto.finna.fi/Record/doria.10024_177355?lng=en-gb). Read 2026-11-22. Catalogue of the 2020 diary edition. The cited 1814 events are reported through the community post and the linked diary PDF was unavailable.
- **S6:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, community blog, [Yhteisiä merkkipäiviä vuosina 1810-12](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2025/01/yhteisia-merkkipaivia-vuosina-1810-12.html). Published 2025-01-19. Supplied passages read 2026-11-22. The supplied 1810–1812 passages give dates, circular numbers and a reported December-to-January gap in Raahe. The linked post did not open. Independent catalogues establish the series and number ranges.
- **S7:** National Library of Finland, [Åbo allmänna tidning](https://kansalliskirjasto.finna.fi/Record/fikka.5582898?lng=fi). Read 2026-11-22. Newspaper catalogue and the linked run for 1810–1819. Specific issue references in the workflow are attributed to the community posts.
