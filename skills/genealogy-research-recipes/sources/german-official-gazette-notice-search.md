## Sources

- **S1:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Zufallsfunde](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/12130/), thread started 2007-10-14; the expulsion posts (#18, #19 and #23–#25) read 2026-11-13.
  - Expulsion entries from 1894, 1900 and 1901 for Russian-Empire subjects. They were issued by the Regierungspräsidenten in Potsdam and Königsberg and by the Bezirkspräsident in Straßburg, and printed in the Trier *Amtsblatt*.
  - The entry fields: occupation, birth date or age, birthplace, *ortsangehörig* versus citizenship, and the two uses of *ebendaselbst*.
  - The labelled 1894 dates versus the bare 1900–1901 dates, and the birth-surname field.
- **S2:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Ich suche das Buch … Wer hat es? Diskussion/Anfragen](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/2550/), thread started 2006-08-17; the Königsberg *Amtsblatt* extracts (posts #154 and #155) read 2026-11-13.
  - A participant's per-year extracts (1814–1868) from the *Amtsblatt der Königl. Preuß. Regierung zu Königsberg*. They cover clergy, councillors, elected *Ratmänner*, postal transfers, a forester's pension, a seminary exam with home place and age, and women's teaching qualifications.
  - The spelling variants within one run, and the requester's remaining task of placing the people.
- **S3:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [a thread on a Berlin and Breslau family search](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/2991761/), thread started 2026-02-01, read in full (three pages) on 2026-11-13.
  - A participant's dated chronology for one man, from address books, Berlin telephone books and three *Deutscher Reichsanzeiger* lines of 22.06.1938 for him, his wife and their son, with titles and last residences. A later line in the same post, dated 02.10.1940 and naming no source, records the withdrawal of citizenship from a woman and her husband.
  - The house-number conflict with the telephone books, the parenthetical added names, the link to the 1931 Schneidemühl address book, and the requester's three restitution-file hits, one of which the participant called probably the wrong man.
  - One participant's remark that the Berlin registration card file survives only from 1948. It was not used, because the Landesarchiv Berlin's page (S5) describes the index differently.
- **S4:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Jüdische Familienforschung](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/7419/), thread started 2006-02-02.
  - A Palatinate Jewish teacher's career chain, citing a Bavarian newspaper (1834) and the *Amts- und Intelligenzblatt* (Rheinkreis 1837; Pfalz, Speyer, 1850 and 1853).
  - The 1830–1840 teacher list by rabbinate district from the *Israelitische Annalen*, with its conflicting Rockenhausen year.
  - The 1870 Palatinate *Beamtenverzeichnis* on Bavarica.

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The four threads supply the example entries, gazette titles, field types and the participants' search practice. The authored synthesis covers:

- the separate fields
- the date-role rule and the *ebendaselbst* rule
- the limits ("a decision is not a departure", "the gazette's place is not a residence")
- the rule of one row per person per list
- the identity status

All of the gazette content here is participants' transcription or summary. None of it has been checked against the printed volumes.

The sources leave several points open:

- whether the bare 1900–1901 dates are decision dates or publication dates
- whether every Regierung *Amtsblatt* carried the same expulsion lists as Trier
- which years the expulsion series covers
- whether any expulsion was carried out

The examples are limited to Prussia, Alsace-Lorraine and the Bavarian Palatinate, to the years quoted, and to Russian-Empire subjects.

- **S5:** Landesarchiv Berlin, [Historische Einwohnermeldekartei](https://landesarchiv-berlin.de/archiv-nutzen/personen-familienforschung/historische-einwohnermeldekartei/), read 2026-11-13. Used for: addresses recorded officially from 1874, with Berlin residents' data kept on printed cards at police stations; the central police index moved out of the city in 1943 and considered lost; the West Berlin index at the Landesarchiv, rebuilt from cards kept at police stations, incomplete before 1945 and covering residents who were in the city after 1945 and deregistered by 1960; the new index of the whole population begun in 1945, in East Berlin from 1948 and now kept by the LABO; the LABO's responsibility for anyone still registered after 31 December 1960; and the yearly address books printed from the index, complete to 1943 and digitised by the Zentral- und Landesbibliothek Berlin together with telephone books to 1991. No completeness for a particular person is inferred.
