# Trace a wartime civilian inquiry in the Vatican Information Office records — sources

- **S1:** [Luigi Francesco Vittore Pololi](https://www.genealogiafamiliare.it/wp/2026/08/03/luigi-francesco-vittore-pololi/). Published 2026-08-03. Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Community family-history article about an Italian civilian internee and a notification received by his spouse. Used as a case showing how a Vatican reply can add evidence after the detention episode.
- **S2:** [Holy See Press Office, announcement of the Vatican Information Office inventory](https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/it/bollettino/pubblico/2004/06/08/0289/00935.html). Published 2004-06-08. Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Official description of civilian and military searches, index-card fields, boxes, internee lists and the two published inventory volumes.
- **S3:** [Vatican Apostolic Archive, Vatican Information Office fonds](https://www.archivioapostolicovaticano.va/content/aav/en/attivita/ricerca-e-conservazione/progetti/inventariazione/ufficio-informazioni-vaticano--prigionieri-di-guerra--1939-1947-.html). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Official description of the fonds, its dates, and the opening of the records to researchers. It does not advertise a public online search of every card.
