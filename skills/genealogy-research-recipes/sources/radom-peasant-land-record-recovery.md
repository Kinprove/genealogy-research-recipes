# Sources for Recover peasant land and settlement files for Radom Governorate

- **S1:** Daniel Paczkowski, [Pociejowscy - mizerne efekty poszukiwań archiwalnych, jeszcze mizerniejsze dla Stępniów](https://danielpaczkowski.blogspot.com/2023/12/pociejowscy-mizerne-efekty-poszukiwan.html), published 2023-12-16. Community account read 2026-11-22. The author reports inspecting separate Bardo and Szumsko units, reading officials named as witnesses in a Szumsko document and finding a Radostów table among mortgage papers. The titles and document contents are case leads from the account. The archives' fonds entries support the broader route.
- **S2:** Archiwum Państwowe w Radomiu, [Urząd Gubernialny Radomski do Spraw Włościańskich, 58/117/0](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/zespol/-/zespol/28668). State archive fonds description read 2026-11-22. It identifies the custodian, historical office and predecessor commissions, and their work with liquidation tables and complaints.
- **S3:** Archiwum Państwowe w Radomiu, [Peasant-affairs fonds table listings](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/de/web/guest/seria?_Seria_delta=200&_Seria_id_serii=777735&_Seria_nameofjsp=jednostki&_Seria_resetCur=false&p_p_id=Seria&p_p_lifecycle=0&p_p_mode=view&p_p_state=normal&refererPlid=20136). Catalogue series page read 2026-11-22. Unit titles distinguish liquidation tables by village, subdivision, gmina and district.
- **S4:** Archiwum Państwowe w Kielcach, [Hipoteka powiatu kieleckiego, 21/1024/0](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/zespol/-/zespol/112524). State archive fonds description read 2026-11-22. It identifies the custodian and collection of rural mortgage files. It does not independently confirm the item-level Radostów attachment.
