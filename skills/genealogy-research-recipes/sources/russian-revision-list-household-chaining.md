**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The structure is authored synthesis: the holdings register for each revision, the field-by-field transcript, the order of the chain, and the rule that an absence is a bounded gap. The forum threads supply the locators, the worked readings and participants' interpretations. They are attributed to participants. The four-step Southern Trans-Urals route in step 6 is adapted from one participant's account (S18). All but one thread come from a single regional forum (Уральская генеалогия), and the remaining thread is from J-Roots. Several threads share regular participants, so agreement between them is not independent confirmation. The age-bias and boilerplate observations come from particular slobodas. Do not assume them for other places without checking.
