**What is synthesis.** The candidate table, entry log, custodian log and not-searched list are authored synthesis. The sources supply the selection criteria, register-type expectations, index-miss example and renaming example. The Swedish membership-to-parish crosswalk, date-order decision, preservation of alternative readings and independent birth/household comparison are procedural synthesis grounded in S7 and the existing origin-side workflow. The archival guides cover repository scope. The holdings or copying conditions for a particular congregation, series coverage by state and the individual register images were not independently checked.
