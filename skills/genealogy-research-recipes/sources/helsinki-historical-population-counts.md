# Sources for Interpret historical Helsinki population counts and yearbooks

- **S1:** Marjukka Vartiainen, City of Helsinki, [Helsingin historiaa numeroina kolmelta vuosisadalta](https://www.hel.fi/hel2/Tietokeskus/julkaisut/pdf/16_01_11_Tyopapereita_1_Vartiainen.pdf). Population definitions in Working Papers 2016:1 read 2026-11-22. Used for Differences across registers and between national and city definitions of presence.

- **S2:** City of Helsinki, [Helsingin väkiluku iän ja sukupuolen mukaan 1900–1961](https://stat.hel.fi/pxweb/fi/Aluesarjat_Arkisto/Aluesarjat_Arkisto__his__vrm__vaerak/HIS_A03HKI_VR.px/). Aluesarjat table notes read 2026-11-22. Used for The change from census-day presence to resident population and this series’ foreign-national coverage note.

- **S3:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Paljonko Helsingissä oli väkeä?](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/08/paljonko-helsingissa-oli-vakea.html). Population definitions and the 1870 census discussion read 2026-11-22. Used for Whether a person was counted on census day and how subjecthood was tabulated separately from birthplace.

- **S4:** Statistical Office of Finland, [Wäenlasku maaliskuussa 1870 Helsingin, Turun, Wiipurin ja Oulun kaupungissa](https://otos.stat.fi/bitstreams/69f8262c-a34b-4968-9da1-27182a0bb24c/download). Helsinki introductory totals and table 7 read 2026-11-22. Used for Census-day population, temporary visitors, residents away, and separate tabulation of non-Finnish subjects.

- **S5:** Statistics Finland, [Väestölaskennat](https://guides.stat.fi/historiallisentilastotiedonopas/vaestolaskennat). Early urban censuses and archive pointer read 2026-11-22. Used for The urban household census archive and published census reports.

- **S6:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Kirosin Kansallisarkistossa](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2024/01/kirosin-kansallisarkistossa.html). Account of ordering separate census material read 2026-11-22. Used for Locating the enumeration-district list first and independently confirming where the folders can be consulted.

- **S7:** City of Helsinki, [Helsingin kaupungin tilastollinen vuosikirja 1910](https://www.hel.fi/static/tieke/digitoidut_asiakirjat/helsingin_kaupungin_tilastolliset_vuosikirjat/1910.html). Volume landing page read 2026-11-22. Used for The 1910 edition and its PDF link. The linked PDF exceeded the inspection tool’s size limit.

- **S8:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Helsingin tilastollista(kin) historiaa](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/01/helsingin-tilastollistakin-historiaa.html). Yearbook navigation and count comparisons read 2026-11-22. Used for Choosing the edition, population basis and geographic rows before comparing counts.
