## Sources (provenance + dates)

> Original Kinprove procedural synthesis. Third-party passages are **not reproduced**. Artifact
> schemas and the example are original; practitioner procedures are attributed below. H8–H12
> and S18 supply the facts in the Canadian consular index, Doukhobor surname and 1939–1945
> resettlement notes. S19 and H13 supply the court search-notice example and the provincial
> gazettes' search notices.

- **S1:** Ancestry, *Top Tips for Beginning Jewish Family History Research*, 2013-10-15 —
  [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pf-olbDbZlY). Destination research, birthplace versus
  residence, full manifests, naturalization and intermediate residence.
- **S2:** J-Roots participants, *Ellis Island*, thread begun 2010-11-26 —
  [discussion](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=167&t=1551). Header/adjacent pages,
  detention lists and browsing after failed name searches. Historical database coverage and the
  participants' proposed OCR explanation are not adopted as current facts.
- **S3:** Ahnenforschung.net participants, *FN Lavender 1922 aus Polen nach NY, jewish*, thread begun
  2026-02-26 — [discussion](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/2999886/).
  Child-name variant recovery and separately reading origin/destination contact fields. The
  discussed family's identity and locality are not independently adjudicated here.
- **S4:** Aimee Cross, *Finding the RIGHT Immigration Record*, 2025-12-11 —
  [tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xm7HHLneP-E). Family chronology, companions, full
  records and ship/date browsing. Historical coverage dates are checked through H1–H4.
- **S5:** Nicole Elder Dyer, *RLP 313: Immigration Records Part 4 – After 1906*, 2024-07-08 —
  [post](https://familylocket.com/rlp-313-immigration-records-part-4-after-1906/).
  Separate family arrivals, return journeys and intended onward destinations.
- **S6:** James Tanner, *What Can I Do If My Ancestors Came From Poland?*, BYU Library Family History
  Center, 2017-07-07 — [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v-DBVg9Yiwg).
  Destination-first research and dated locality/jurisdiction lookup; broad historical
  generalizations are not required by this procedure.
- **S7:** Suzanne Hoffman, *Mayhem on a Manifest*, BYU Library Family History Center,
  presented 2024-10-10, published 2024-10-11 — [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZgyxRZdHgcw).
  Searching a destination contact to recover a traveler; its other identity inferences are not
  adopted here.
- **S8:** Angela Sinickas, *An Introduction to Lithuanian Genealogy*, BYU Family History Library,
  published 2019-02-26 — [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mXVe1M5AibY). An origin
  named on an immigration record can be the guberniya rather than the same-named city; its
  demographic generalizations are not adopted.
- **S9:** James Tanner, *What Do You Need to Know to Find the Origin of an Immigrant?*, BYU Family
  History Library, published 2022-08-10 — [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wUyse0sA2cU).
  A census country of origin may reflect the departure port the informant named; offered as a
  general warning, not as proof for any particular census.
- **S10:** Genealogy Gems, *Episode 68 Avoid Rabbit Holes and Find More Genealogy!*, published
  2021-08-26 — [episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3y1JkCoCpyU), also *Genealogy Research
  Questions, Plans, and Avoiding Distractions*, published 2020-06-16 —
  [episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VWxN39TRlMk); and Roger Minert, *Germans Immigrants in
  American Church Records*, BYU Family History Library, published 2020-01-23 —
  [presentation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XadMLrXNX9U). A survey of origins across a rare
  surname and searching for relatives in other regions; neither establishes kinship.
- **S11:** Genealogy Gems, *16 Ways to Find Your Ancestor's Village*, published 2022-09-29 —
  [interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RKqz2lXQU7w). Separately filed marriage components
  with differing content, including the NYC licence application with parents' birthplaces (date
  range stated with uncertainty), and big-city names standing in for nearby villages.
- **S12:** GeneaVlogger, *Researching My Paternal Great-Grandfather (VLOG #4)*, published
  2017-07-10 — [vlog](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2YLat4WuFKY); and *Using DNA and Immigration
  Records to Breakdown Brick Walls in American Jewish Genealogy (VLOG #29)*, published 2019-06-02 —
  [vlog](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2h6zplRuD28). Reading a Yiddish signature on a declaration
  as a separate name observation used as a search key; the crowd reading is not adopted as the
  original name.
- **S13:** GeneaVlogger, *Answering Live Genealogy and DNA Test Questions*, published 2025-07-25 —
  [live stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pVfGwY6uBHU); and *Holiday Genealogy & DNA Q&A –
  Ask Me Anything LIVE*, published 2025-12-26 —
  [live stream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VQgE0u9SGSU). Passage-ticket ledger fields and a
  purchaser-passenger link between surname forms; the presenter's uncertainty about the repository
  and column meanings is retained.
- **S14:** J-Roots participants, *Не знаете, где спросить? Спросите здесь*, thread begun 2009-08-24 —
  [discussion](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=75&t=7576). Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used for the pointer to Russian consular records in the USA (viewable on FamilySearch
  film) as a source for undelivered-remittance files naming sender and recipient; coverage is not
  adopted from the thread.
- **S15:** Ahnenforschung.net participants, Ahnenforschung.net topic 3013995, thread begun 2026-04-30 —
  [discussion](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/3013995/).
  Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for comparing census birthplaces and manifest origin
  when a German-speaking family recorded as born in 'Germany' may come from Russian Empire German
  colonies; the family's identity and ancestral origin are not adjudicated here.
- **S16:** Ahnenforschung.net participants, *Auswanderung USA*, thread begun 2021-11-22 —
  [discussion](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/185403/). Rights:
  cited reference, not reproduced. Used for separating same-named destination contacts and
  comparing each one's dated addresses with the manifest contact address; no candidate is adopted
  as the contact.
- **S17:** J-Roots participants, *Нужно мнение коллег*, thread begun 2015-09-22 —
  [discussion](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=4667). Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used for reconstructing an unrecognised place name on a German-written passenger list
  by comparing letterforms on the page and German spellings of Slavic sounds; the proposed reading
  is not adopted as confirmed.
- **S18:** Doukhobor Genealogy, [Ship Passenger Lists](https://doukhobor.org/shiplists/),
  opened 2026-11-02. Used for: the Doukhobor arrival periods, and patronymics or family nicknames
  sometimes recorded instead of official surnames.
- **S19:** Ancestry, *Michael Douglas Discovers the Untold Truth About His History | PBS' Finding
  Your Roots | Ancestry*, published 2026-10-27 — [clip](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uFyZWbkmoAs);
  from PBS, *Finding Your Roots*, season 10, episode 9 —
  [clip page with transcript](https://www.pbs.org/video/michael-douglas-unravels-his-familys-secret-past),
  opened 2026-11-06. A 1906 Mogilev District Court notice seeking a Chausy townsperson and naming
  the article charged, used as a lead to a registration town, court files and siblings' metrical
  entries; the programme's other claims about the charge and the family are not adopted.

- **H2:** U.S. National Archives, [Naturalization Records](https://www.archives.gov/research/immigration/naturalization).
- **H3:** Marian L. Smith, [By Way of Canada](https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/2000/fall/us-canada-immigration-records-1.html),
  *Prologue*, Fall 2000. U.S. records made at Canadian ports, index-to-manifest links and coverage limits.
- **H4:** Library and Archives Canada,
  [Passenger lists 1865 to 1935](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/immigration/passenger-lists-1865-1935.html).

  [A Guide to Interpreting Passenger List Annotations](https://www.jewishgen.org/InfoFiles/Manifests/),
  last revised 2002-09-29.
- **H6:** JewishGen, [The JewishGen Communities Database](https://www.jewishgen.org/Communities/About.htm).
- **H7:** Library and Archives Canada,
  [Form 30A, Ocean arrivals 1919 to 1924](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/immigration/form-30a-ocean-arrivals-1919-1924.html).
  Form coverage, browsing and the exemption for passengers in transit to the United States.
- **H8:** Library and Archives Canada,
  [Immigrants from the Russian Empire, 1898–1922](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/immigration/immigrants-russian-empire.html),
  opened 2026-11-02. Used for: the name index of the Passport and Identity Papers files of the
  Russian consulates in Montréal, Vancouver and Halifax, its search filters, and that only this
  series is digitised.
- **H9:** Bundesarchiv,
  [Personen- und Familienforschung](https://www.bundesarchiv.de/im-archiv-recherchieren/archivgut-recherchieren/personen-und-familienforschung/),
  opened 2026-11-02. Used for: EWZ naturalisation records among its holdings, and the signed
  Benutzungsantrag sent by post or as an emailed scan.
- **H10:** EHRI portal (USHMM description),
  [Einwandererzentralstelle Litzmannstadt (R 69)](https://portal.ehri-project.eu/units/us-005578-irn502308),

- **H11:** NARA History Hub,
  [thread on A3342-EWZ50 application files](https://openhistoryhub.com/t/seeking-information-from-a3342-ewz50-antrage-applications-files/27920),

  [blog post on using the EWZ records](https://www.familysearch.org/blog/en/using-the-ewz-records-for-german-and-eastern-european-research),
  opened 2026-11-02. Used for: the origin regions of the resettled groups, and FamilySearch
  holding only part of the series on film.
- **H13:** RAPSI (Russian legal news agency), Anna Shubina,
  [«100 лет назад. 25 ноября – 1 декабря»](https://rapsinews.ru/judicial_analyst/20131125/269819088.html)

  police offices printed in *Vologodskie gubernskie vedomosti* in November 1913.
- **H14:** Freie und Hansestadt Hamburg, Behörde für Kultur und Medien,
  [Link to your Roots](https://www.hamburg.de/politik-und-verwaltung/behoerden/behoerde-fuer-kultur-und-medien/projekte-und-initiativen/link-to-your-roots-110216),
  opened 2026-11-07. Used for: the Staatsarchiv Hamburg's 555 volumes of emigrant lists for
  1850–1934, recording name, place of origin and destination, searchable online at Ancestry.
- **H15:** Ancestry, [Hamburg Passenger Lists, 1850-1934](https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/1068)
  (collection 1068), read from an
  [Internet Archive capture of 2026-10-21](https://web.archive.org/web/20261021055337/https://www.ancestry.com/search/collections/1068)
  on 2026-11-07 (the live page returned HTTP 403). Used for: the gap of 1915–1919, separate direct
  and indirect lists for 1854–1910, the last place of residence on most lists, and a partial index
  for 1850–1914 and 1920–1934.
- **H16:** David Lukowiecki, MyHeritage Wiki,
  [Israeli Naturalization Records](https://www.myheritage.com/wiki/Naturalization_records_in_Israel),
  2023-01-15, read 2026-11-08. Used for: the Israel State Archives holding more than 60,000
  Mandate-era naturalization applications, all scanned onto its website, many fully indexed and the
  rest only partly; the collection being incomplete; and forms also filled in Hebrew. A secondary
  report by a commercial genealogy company, adopted only as an attributed observation.

  [Archive of pre-state Jewish population offers glimpse of founding generation](https://www.timesofisrael.com/archive-of-pre-state-jewish-population-offers-glimpse-of-founding-generation/amp/),
  2017-12-28, read 2026-11-08. Used for: the span 1937–1947 of about 67,000 requests for
  citizenship in British Mandatory Palestine. A news report, adopted only as an attributed
  observation.
