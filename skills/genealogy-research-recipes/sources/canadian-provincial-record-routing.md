## Sources

- **S1:** Ontario Genealogical Society, [article on Ontario civil registration](https://ogs.on.ca/?p=19335), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: Ontario civil registration from 1 July 1869, and its patchy compliance in the first years.
- **S2:** Archives of Ontario, [Order a copy of a birth, marriage or death registration](https://www.archives.gov.on.ca/order-a-copy-of-a-birth-marriage-or-death-registration/), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: the Archives of Ontario holding registrations from 1869 up to a cut-off per event type, and ServiceOntario holding later years.
- **S3:** Royal BC Museum, [BC Archives Genealogy search](https://genealogy.royalbcmuseum.bc.ca/), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: the index coverage (births 1854–1904, marriages 1871–1950, deaths 1872–2005) and the annual release of data by the Vital Statistics Agency.
- **S4:** Nova Scotia Archives, [Vital Statistics](https://archives.novascotia.ca/vital-statistics/), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: births and deaths registered 1864–1877 and again from October 1908; online coverage as of July 2025 (births 1864–1877 and 1908–1924, delayed births 1830–1924, City of Halifax deaths 1890–1908, marriage bonds 1763–1864 and registrations 1864–1949, deaths 1908–1974); the transfer of 1925 birth registrations to the Archives; Vital Statistics, Service Nova Scotia holding births from 1926, marriages from 1951 and deaths from 1976 as of March 2026.
- **S5:** Library and Archives Canada, [Access First World War service files in Collection Search](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/corporate/updates/2023/access-first-world-war-service-files-in-collection-search.html), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: more than 600,000 CEF service files digitised and integrated into Collection Search.
- **S6:** Library and Archives Canada, [Personnel records of the First World War](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/military-history/first-world-war/fww-personnel.html), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: almost all CEF service files online.
- **S7:** Library and Archives Canada, [Personnel records of the Second World War](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/military-history/second-world-war/personnel-records-second-world-war.html), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: files open only for those who died in service 1939–1947 and downloadable from Collection Search; genealogy packages and about 1,600 complete or partial files; survivors' files closed, requested by ATIP with proof of death or consent.
- **S8:** Library and Archives Canada, [First Nations genealogy](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/indigenous-history/first-nations-genealogy.html), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: treaty annuity paylists in RG 10 and finding aids FA 10-91, 10-109 and 10-116; the band number or ticket; the Privacy Act restriction on lists after 1909.
- **S9:** Archives of Manitoba, [Hudson's Bay Company Archives](https://www.gov.mb.ca/chc/archives/hbca/index.html) and its [glossaries](https://gov.mb.ca/chc/archives/hbca/glossaries.html), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: the HBCA as part of the Archives of Manitoba; servants' contracts; pre-1870 records of almost five hundred posts.
- **S10:** Library and Archives Canada, [Home Children](https://www.canada.ca/en/library-archives/collection/research-help/genealogy-family-history/immigration/home-children.html), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: the 1869 to late-1930s span; the Fairbridge Society bringing some Middlemore children to a British Columbia farm school 1936–1948; the index's sources; the group code for each party.
- **S11:** Barnardo's, [Former Barnardo's children](https://www.barnardos.org.uk/former-barnardos-children), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: Barnardo's holding its child-migrant records and also those of the Marchmont, Annie Macpherson and Liverpool Sheltering Homes; release through the Family History Service with a descendant's consent.
- **S12:** National Archives of Canada, [Tracing Your Ancestors in Canada](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/Collection/SA2-42-1998E.pdf) (revised 1998), printed pages 20 and 29, opened 2026-11-04. Used for: Ontario civil registration from 1 July 1869; Métis scrip applications giving dates and places of birth and the names of parents, children and spouses, filed alphabetically within several RG 15 series.
