**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The following are authored synthesis:

- the run register;
- the fit check;
- the ordered sequence of one-input reruns;
- the label-audit checklist;
- the component status terms (reference-gap artefact / model-dependent / possible proxy / stable within tested scopes / uninterpretable);
- the rules for comparing runs.

The sources supply:

- the worked reruns and their paired distances;
- the posted qpAdm specifications and figures;
- the reported provider category description;
- the locality correction;
- participants' observations about sims, scaling, releases and software behaviour.

All participants are hobbyists posting their own runs. Several of these threads share members, so agreement between them is not independent confirmation.
