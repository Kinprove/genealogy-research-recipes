## Sources

Primary material is paraphrased. The episode/date worksheet and document-recovery procedure are
original synthesis. They apply the existing identity/proof framework without importing legacy
community percentages, legal advice or person-specific examples. The facts in the search-scope,
query, award, decree-window and custodian rules added on 2 November 2026 are checked against the
portal, decree, archive and library pages H16–H44 (H16–H19, H32 and H34 seen only in
search-engine extracts, as marked), and details those pages did not support were dropped.
