## Sources

Original procedural synthesis. The sources are attributed and paraphrased; third-party prose,
screenshots, catalog entries and real family examples are not reproduced.

- **P1:** *Where to Find Genealogy Records for Poland*, published 2024-12-29 —
  [guide](https://whoareyoumadeof.com/blog/where-to-find-genealogy-records-for-poland/).
  Geneteka scan links and the onward archival route.
- **P2:** Genealogy TV, *How to Research Your Polish Ancestors*, published 2019-04-19 —
  [tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i4FsVQFuIw0). Metryki region/parish/year/image browsing.
- **P3:** Legacy Tree Genealogists, *Tracing Your Eastern European Ancestors*, RootsTech 2020,
  uploaded 2020-03-06 — [interview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oOqlg-urSmI).
  Regional archival portals that supplement the central search route.
- **O1:** Szukaj w Archiwach, *Search instructions* → *Genealogy search* and *Search on the
  fonds list page*, checked 2026-10-07 —
  [official instructions](https://www.szukajwarchiwach.gov.pl/en/instrukcje-wyszukiwania).
  Current PRADZIAD, vital-record, fonds and file-list distinctions. Re-read 2026-11-07 from
  Internet Archive captures of the Polish (2026-03-20) and English (2025-04-04) genealogy-search
  pages, because the live site blocks automated access: PRADZIAD is chosen in the document-type
  list of the home-page search, and the vital-records search shows rows with PRADZIAD description
  fields. The pages give no year in which the standalone PRADZIAD database closed.
- **O2:** Polskie Towarzystwo Genealogiczne, *Geneteka* search form, opened 2026-11-02 —
  [start page](https://geneteka.genealodzy.pl/). Used for: the territory list, which includes
  Ukraina, Białoruś and Litwa after the Polish voivodeships.
- **O3:** Polskie Towarzystwo Genealogiczne, *Geneteka* births results page for a very common
  surname in mazowieckie, fetched 2026-11-02 —
  [results page](https://geneteka.genealodzy.pl/index.php?op=gt&lang=pol&bdm=B&w=07mz&rid=B&search_lastname=Kowalski&search_name=&search_lastname2=&search_name2=&from_date=&to_date=).
  Used for: the form labels for parent, couple, exact and 15 km nearby-parish search, and a fetch
  that returned only the header row, with no data rows and no result counter. No browser
  comparison was run, so this is an observed shape, not a proven loading failure.
- **O4:** Polskie Towarzystwo Genealogiczne, *O Genetece*, opened 2026-11-02 —
  [page](https://geneteka.genealodzy.pl/ogenetece.php). Used for: indexed data whose scans and
  photographs are not available.
- **C1:** J-Roots Forum, *Архивы Польши*, first post 2009-12-07 —
  [thread](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=165&t=470). Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used for the Księga ludności / Spis ludności search terms, the house-number
  arrangement of these books, the warning that external indexes of them are incomplete, and for
  giving Polish and Russian surname forms and spelling variants when requesting Congress Poland
  register entries. Checked 2026-11-07: post 1 was read in search-result text after direct access
  failed; it bases the advice to request spelling variants explicitly on the writer's exchanges
  with Radom, Kielce and Warsaw archives;
  it does not establish a policy for every archive. [Post 21](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?t=470&start=20)
  was read through the web reader and identifies the linked `ge298x.xml` inventory as AGAD's eastern-territory metrical books after a
  reader mistook the holding city for the parish location. Used only for that reported inventory
  example; neither the inventory nor its current scans were independently checked.
- **C2:** J-Roots Forum, *Нужно мнение коллег*, first post 2015-09-22 —
  [thread](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=4&t=4667). Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used for the warning that many 20th-century Polish civil-status registers are still
  held by the local Urząd Stanu Cywilnego rather than the state archive.
- **O5:** Poznań Project, [search start page](https://poznan-project.psnc.pl/), opened 2026-11-04.
