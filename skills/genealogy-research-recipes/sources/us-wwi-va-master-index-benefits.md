# Sources for Decode a World War I VA Master Index card and trace its benefit files

- **S1:** Just Genealogy, [Searching for Noel Andrew Byrd - JG0057](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l4auFzsl-BU), community video, published 2022-05-04. Supplied transcript excerpts reviewed 2026-11-22. The video page could not be opened. The presenter recommends using a code key and requesting an XC file by its claim number. The card’s handwritten annotation date was tentative. S3–S9 establish the general rules. S8 gives the accession as `70-A-6324`.

- **S2:** FamilySearch, [United States, Veterans Administration Master Index, 1917–1940](https://www.familysearch.org/search/collection/2968245). Collection description read 2026-11-22. Used for collection scope and the route to card images. Individual case images were not inspected.

- **S3:** National Archives, [VA Master Index Card File Key to Codes and Prefixes](https://www.archives.gov/files/calendar/genealogy-fair/2018/6-csar-handout3.pdf). Code handout read 2026-11-22. Used for `C`, `K`, `T`, `R`, `A`, `UA` and `CT`. The direct archival handout supplies the key because the FamilySearch Wiki page could not be opened.

- **S4:** National Archives, [Frequently Requested Records](https://www.archives.gov/st-louis/frequently-requested). Deceased Veterans Claim Files section read 2026-11-22. Used for `X` as deceased status, closure when benefits ended, the later-closure custody caveat and request identifiers.

- **S5:** National Archives, [World War I Records at the National Archives at St. Louis](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/ww1/st-louis-ww1-series). Deceased Veterans Claims series descriptions read 2026-11-22. Used for archival XC accessions in RG 15 and their differing end dates.

- **S6:** U.S. Congress, [World War Adjusted Compensation Act, 43 Stat. 121](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-43/pdf/STATUTE-43-Pg121.pdf), 19 May 1924. Relevant provisions read 2026-11-22. Used for the qualifying days, separate daily rates and caps, the pay threshold and earliest date, and the original maturity and beneficiary rules.

- **S7:** U.S. Congress, [Adjusted Compensation Payment Act, 1936, 49 Stat. 1099](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/STATUTE-49/pdf/STATUTE-49-Pg1098.pdf), 27 January 1936. Section 1 read 2026-11-22. Used for early settlement on application and surrender.

- **S8:** National Archives, [World War I Bonus Finance Cards, 1924–40, NC1-015-79-12](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-veterans-affairs/rg-0015/nc1-015-79-12_sf115.pdf). Disposition form and cover sheet read 2026-11-22. Used for the accession number and the documented December 1979 destruction.

- **S9:** National Archives, [Nonconverted Lapsed War Risk Term Insurance Folders, 1917–21, NC-174-000213](https://www.archives.gov/files/records-mgmt/rcs/schedules/departments/department-of-veterans-affairs/rg-0015/nc-174-000213_sf115.pdf#page=1). Disposition form and cover sheet read 2026-11-22. Used for the limited `T` series, its disposal authority and the distinction between assumed and documented disposal.
