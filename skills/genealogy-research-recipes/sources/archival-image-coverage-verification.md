# Sources: archival image and coverage verification

Primary pages opened 2026-10-18; cited as references, not reproduced.

- **S1. FamilySearch, [Image Organize: Guiding Principles and Practices to Divide Groups](https://www.familysearch.org/en/fieldops/article/image-organize-guiding-principles-and-practices-to-divide-groups-external)**, sections 2–5 and 9. Supports item boundaries, opening/closing/interior inspection and exceptions involving several books or films. These are provider organisation rules, not proof that a particular scan is complete.
- **S2. FamilySearch, [Document information in Historical Records](https://www.familysearch.org/en/help/helpcenter/article/what-is-the-document-information-in-historical-records)**. Defines digital folder, microfilm, image, page, family and household identifiers; provider and original-reference numbers need not coincide.
- **S3. JewishGen Ukraine Research Division, [FAQ](https://www.jewishgen.org/ukraine/TPL_Base.asp?id=13)**, “How do I find out what new records are being added”. Links monthly and cumulative upload lists and says they change monthly. No individual case's current indexing was investigated for this recipe.
- **S4. JewishGen, [Transcription Rules for JewishGen Databases](https://www.jewishgen.org/databases/%24transcription.html)**, Source Indicator and Data Entry. Requires original-record locators and separates conjecture from source transcription. It does not certify a dataset's completeness.

Saved community demonstration inspected 2026-10-20:

- **S5. Melissa Barker and Constance Knox, Genealogy TV, [How to Preserve Old Photos and Scrapbooks: The Archive Lady](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=11wZOvuGtKs)**. Describes retaining an as-found image and another with a folded item opened, and checking the next microfilm frame for a lifted overlay. The recipe applies this to already available images; it adopts no physical treatment, adhesive-removal or storage instructions. The original objects and frames were not independently inspected.

- **S6. Genealogy TV, [U.S. Census Maps and Other Unindex Records on FamilySearch.org](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1ectqP9Ogys)**, recorded publication 2021-03-12. The saved transcript describes an end-of-roll card at image one, reverse locality order and progressively smaller jumps using readable labels. The recipe retains the conditional navigation step and adds explicit coverage and stop controls. The original frames, present viewer and proposed household locations were not independently inspected; skipped images remain unsearched.

- **S7. Family Locket, [RLP 210: Pennsylvania Germans Part 4 - Probate and Church Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4_fPAYDF-IU)**, recorded publication 2022-07-18. The saved episode describes omitted communicant lists and different marriage entries in typed and handwritten transcriptions of a minister's register. It motivates comparing available derivative coverage when the original is unavailable. Locators, explicit common-origin uncertainty and the proof boundary apply existing controls; the original register, either transcription and the narrated family reconstruction were not independently inspected. The saved capture ends mid-sentence, so saved EOF does not establish a complete original episode. No denominational, custody or historical legal rule is adopted.

The page × target ledger, field-specific adjudication, catalogue-conflict handling and completeness checks are workflow synthesis from local archival-review practice. They introduce no historical cutoff, archive-specific holding assertion or measured accuracy claim. Genealogical identity and source weighting remain with the existing proof recipe.
