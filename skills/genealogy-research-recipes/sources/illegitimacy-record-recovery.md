**Authored content.** The series checklist, status labels, allegation-level recording format and Swedish locator, transfer-request and coverage controls are authored synthesis. S4 and S5 repeat the same story and are not independent. S9 and S10 are separate presentations by the same provider, not independent confirmation of a particular case.

The English double-surname examples remain attributed to S6's UK lecture. The rule to test
local alias usage and retain unresolved maiden/prior-married names prevents those examples
from becoming a general U.S. naming rule. Legitimation and adoption are separate legal-record
routes; neither a later surname nor a foster placement establishes their occurrence.
