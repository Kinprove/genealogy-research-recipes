# Link a historical Stockholm household to its lot and title records — sources

- **S1:** [Piiloutunut lahjoittaja Andreas Bohlin (osa 3/3)](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2025/05/piiloutunut-lahjoittaja-andreas-bohlin_01590413056.html). Published 2025-05-07. Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Kaisa Kyläkoski’s community case from the Anders Bohlin research series. The case compares a residence, plot numbers, purchases and a later sale listed under heirs after a testamentary reversion.
- **S2:** [Stockholmskällan, map of Gamla stan under the 1729 plot numbers](https://stockholmskallan.stockholm.se/post/27670). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Municipal historical-map record explaining the two numbering systems and linking the City Archives comparison tool and a later map.
- **S3:** [Sveriges Riksbank, A real-estate price index for Stockholm, 1726–1875](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/forskning/monetar-statistik/volym-3/riksbanken_iii_ch7.pdf). PDF. Read 2026-11-22. Empirical archival study, especially figure 7.1 and the block names and numbers section. It reproduces a register card and transaction protocol and describes incompletely matched or combined lots.
