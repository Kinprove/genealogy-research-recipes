## Sources

Third-party sources are cited references, paraphrased rather than reproduced.
The linked inventory/queue schema and scope-completion rule are original synthesis.

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library, James Tanner,
  [Probate Part 6: Finding Probate Documents](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oZiYbKNvxJY),
  16 November 2015. Historical courts/custody, delayed proceedings, related jurisdictions and
  executor/administrator deeds as probate leads.
- **S2:** Ancestry,
  [Finding Probate Records That Aren't Indexed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lOmly79G2Tk),
  17 February 2016. Follows an index's will, administration-docket and probate-journal references.
- **S3:** Genealogy TV,
  [Targeted Genealogical Research: Probate Example](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=10DuXkEoTRA),
  12 December 2025. Retrieves additional settlement/appraisal material and identifies a recording
  three years after death.

- **S4:** Dana Leeds,
  [The Horner Estate Dispute: A Family Feud, Fraudulent Deeds, and a Quest for Justice](https://www.danaleeds.com/the-horner-estate-dispute/),
  20 May 2024. Secondary account of a surname/locality case lead, reporter-citation recovery
  of an opinion, and separate will/deed finds. The original opinion, instruments and missing
  embedded material were not inspected for this recipe. Case facts, AI narrative and historical
  service syntax/access are not adopted. Applying the existing queue to instruments actually
  named in a retrieved opinion is original synthesis; the article does not supply their locators.
