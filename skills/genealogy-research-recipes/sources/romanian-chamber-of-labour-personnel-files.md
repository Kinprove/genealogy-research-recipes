# Sources for Find Romanian Chamber of Labour personnel files

- **S1:** [O sursă genealogică mai puțin cunoscută: dosarele personale din fondurile Camerelor de Muncă](https://genealogica.ro/blog/articol/o-sursa-genealogica-mai-putin-cunoscuta-dosarele-personale-din-fondurile-camerelor-de-munca). Andrei Cucuruz’s community article read 2026-11-22. Used for the author’s inventory-first search advice and the examples of credentials, personal data and possible portrait attachments. The two pictured files do not establish a typical set of fields.

- **S2:** [Ghidul fondurilor arhivistice de după 1918 deținute de Arhivele Naționale Harghita](https://www.academia.edu/102885821/Ghidul_fondurilor_arhivistice_de_dup%C4%83_1918_de%C8%9Binute_de_Arhivele_Na%C8%9Bionale_Harghita). Harghita archival guide entries for the two Brașov Chamber offices read 2026-11-22. Used for office-specific inventory numbers, arrangement, document types and series dates. The source is a hosted copy of the archival guide. Individual file availability was not checked.
