# Source notes: Jewish archival-list triage

## Method and scope

The candidate states, title counterchecks, case-key reconciliation and distinction
between geographical selection and verified contents are Kinprove's original
workflow synthesis. They are not a catalogue provider's classification standard.
Keyword stems are recall examples, not a complete historical vocabulary or an
ethnicity classifier. No mandatory batch size, model vendor or publication policy
is part of this general method.

For the subsequent source-image and indexing checks, see the separately sourced
[archival image coverage recipe](../recipes/archival-image-coverage-verification.md)
and its [source notes](archival-image-coverage-verification.md). Those references
support that verification stage, not a claim that any particular fund contains
Jewish lists. No archival case findings are asserted here.
