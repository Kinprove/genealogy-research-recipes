# Sources for Trace Riga’s Swedish–Finnish parish at St James’s

- **S1:** [Riian ruotsalais-suomalaiset papit](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2024_06_09_archive.html). Published 2024-06-14. Kaisa Kyläkoski’s community post in the blog’s weekly archive read 2026-11-22. Used for the St James’s parish and clergy route, the almanac lead and the separate dates for the military and parish offices. The archive contains the full June 14 post.

- **S2:** [Die St. Jacobi-Kirche in Riga von 1710–1790 und Schluss bis 1864](https://dom.lndb.lv/data/obj/file/27213851.pdf). C. A. B.’s St James’s register discussion in Rigascher Almanach für 1865 read 2026-11-22. Used for the last and next baptism entries, interruption to burial entries and the change from Swedish to German recording before the separate Swedish book was established.

- **S3:** [Rīgas Sv. Jēkaba](https://raduraksti.arhivi.lv/collections/1:4:11:2055:2472). Latvian National Archives register inventory read 2026-11-22. Used for the St James’s collection route and dated register listings. The inventory alone does not establish which historical entries survive.

- **S4:** [Ylioppilasmatrikkeli 1640–1852, Jeremias Raumstadius](https://ylioppilasmatrikkeli.fi/henkilo.php?id=626). Kotivuori’s matriculation entry and references read 2026-11-22. Used for appointment versus taking office, the overlapping roles and the source trail to diocesan proceedings and Livonian clergy biographies.
