# Find Finnish archival material in Astia by creator and record type — sources

- **S1:** [Astia ei (minusta) edelleenkään toimi](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/01/astia-ei-minusta-edelleenkaan-toimi.html). Published 2023-01-02. Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Community post by Kaisa Kyläkoski. The January 2023 example came from a Finnish Meteorological Institute search. The post suggests trying catalogue 525, whose contents it leaves open.
- **S2:** [National Archives of Finland, defence administration materials](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/puolustushallinnon-aineistot). Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Official Astia search examples with separate material and actor searches, phrases, date filters and actor restrictions. Used for the documented search procedure.
- **S3:** [Finna, archive structure for Ilmatieteen laitoksen arkisto (1880–1938)](https://www.finna.fi/Collection/narc.VAKKA-167345.KA?hiddenFilters%5B0%5D=hierarchy_top_id%3A%22narc.VAKKA-167345.KA%22&hiddenFilters%5B1%5D=%21id%3A%22narc.VAKKA-167345.KA%22&lng=en-gb&page=7&sid=&type=AllFields). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Finna display of National Archives metadata for the Institute archive. Used for the independent catalogue lead; live Astia cards and the individual weather observations were not inspected.
- **S4:** [National Archives of Finland, government registry office records](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/valtioneuvoston-kirjaajankonttori). Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Official example of using creator cards and a separate archival catalogue. Used for the relationship between creator cards and the units they list.
- **S5:** [Vastasin Kansallisarkiston käyttäjäkyselyyn](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/03/vastasin-kansallisarkiston.html). Published 2023-03-04. Web page. Supplied post excerpts reviewed 2026-11-22. Community post by Kaisa Kyläkoski. The linked post could not be opened. The supplied excerpt identifies an exact Turku Cathedral Chapter match among other search results.
- **S6:** [National Archives of Finland, Turku Cathedral Chapter](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/turun-tuomiokapituli). Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Official fonds description and Astia link. The guide independently identifies the chapter archive and its separate printed catalogue.
