# Sources for Trace a coat of arms to its bearer before testing descent

- **S1:** Just Genealogy, [The History of Genealogy – JG0290](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UUZ6LIOSHao). Supplied transcript excerpts read and video URL attempted 2026-11-22. The presenter points to Crozier and to colonial gravestones. The video request was throttled and the full presentation was not inspected. Crozier supports the Virginia search independently.

- **S2:** William Armstrong Crozier, [Virginia heraldica](https://archive.org/stream/virginiaheraldic00croz/virginiaheraldic00croz_djvu.txt). Digitized book transcription read for sample entries 2026-11-22. Used for the entries that give arms, tombstone descriptions and genealogical notes. The OCR was checked as a finding aid.

- **S3:** Family Tree Magazine, [13 Common Genealogy Questions, Answered](https://familytreemagazine.com/general-genealogy/common-genealogy-questions-answered/). Coat-of-arms question read 2026-11-22. Used for distinguishing crest and arms and for testing claims made on a shared surname.

- **S4:** College of Arms, [Proving a right to arms](https://www.college-of-arms.gov.uk/About/05.htm). Grant, confirmation and pedigree guidance read 2026-11-22. Used for tracing the original bearer and checking pedigree and armorial records within the College of Arms’ jurisdiction.

- **S5:** American Heraldry Society, [Guidelines for Heraldic Practice](https://www.americanheraldry.org/education-resources/guidelines-for-heraldic-practice/). Parts of an achievement and inheritance guidance read 2026-11-22. Used for recording elements of an achievement and distinguishing identification from attribution or entitlement.

- **S6:** Library of Virginia, [Index to Sources on Family Coats of Arms](https://lva-virginia.libguides.com/family-coats-of-arms). Scope and sources list read 2026-11-22. Used to locate Crozier and related armorial references. Its index directs the researcher to the cited works.
