- **S2:** [Clemente Suardi, Emigrazione 1929-1970: norme e archivi](https://www.genealogiafamiliare.it/wp/matrimoni-per-procura/matrimoni-per-procura-emigrazione/). Published 2026-08-27. Community source. A companion research guide distinguishing marriage in Italy by proxy from marriage with both spouses present overseas. Read the guidance on mandate scope, civil banns, the period forms and transcription. Read 2026-11-22.
- **S3:** [Italian Law 27 May 1929 no. 847, implementing the marriage provisions of the Concordat](https://www.comune.bolzano.it/download/csdemog/NORMATIVA/STATO_CIVILE/Legge_27_05_1929_n_847.pdf). Published 1929-06-08. The published law, especially articles 6, 9 and 12–14, describes civil banns, originals and the notice before transcription when banns or dispensation were absent. Read 2026-11-22.
- **S4:** [Holy See, Codex Iuris Canonici (1917), canons 1088–1091](https://www.vatican.va/archive/aas/documents/AAS-09-II-1917-ocr.pdf). Published 1917-06-28. The promulgated text describes the role of the proxy, special mandate, witness or clergy signatures and the priest’s obligations. Read 2026-11-22.
