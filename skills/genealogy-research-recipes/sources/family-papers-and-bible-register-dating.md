**Authored synthesis.** The following is not taken from any one presenter:

- the split into letters, address records and family books
- the separate address and text fields
- the writing-occasion categories
- reading [S7]'s view of many hands as consistency rather than proof
- reading [S11]'s 1909 inscription and shared cover as limits
- the check of whether the register leaves belong to the book, and limiting the before-printing and owner-death tests to leaves that do; the cited pages show register pages removed from Bibles and records copied into new ones, not leaves moved between Bibles
- using the writing medium's first availability as an earliest writing date
- the Return layout

The presenters supply the examples and the failure modes.
