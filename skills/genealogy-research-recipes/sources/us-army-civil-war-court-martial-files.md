- **S3:** [Genealogy Standards for Reasoning From Evidence - Standards 37, 38, and 39 - JG0332](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O4DsiIdgiaE), published 2023-10-07. Reviewed 2026-11-23, video, access unknown. The video URL could not be fetched on 2026-11-23. In the supplied transcript excerpt, the presenter recalls hearing about an alteration note, tentatively associates it with a Lincoln court-martial pardon date, and says he has never looked for the record.

- **S4:** [U.S. National Archives, National Archives Discovers Date Change on Lincoln Record](https://www.archives.gov/press/press-releases/2011/nr11-57.html), published 2011-01-24. Reviewed 2026-11-23, web-page, access read. Read 2026-11-23. The Archives identifies Patrick Murphy’s pardon as NAID 1839980 and records the alteration from April 14, 1864 to April 14, 1865. The release supplies the correction separately from the image it captions and does not establish whether a notice was attached to the physical record.
