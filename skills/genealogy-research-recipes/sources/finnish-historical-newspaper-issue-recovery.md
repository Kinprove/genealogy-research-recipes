- **S1:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Blogin kesälom... sarja alkaa vuodesta 1819](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/06/blogin-kesalom-sarja-alkaa-vuodesta-1819.html). The poem’s locator, title dates and portal scope were checked independently.

- **S2:** University of Turku Library, [Turun Wiikko-Sanomat](https://sites.utu.fi/libuniturku/en/online-exhibitions/250-years-of-finnish-newspapers/turun-wiikko-sanomat/). Title history read 2026-11-22. Used for the first year, publication interruption after the 1827 fire and resumption in 1829.

- **S3:** National Library of Finland, [Kansalliskokoelman sanoma- ja aikakauslehdet](https://www.kansalliskirjasto.fi/fi/kokoelmat/kansalliskokoelman-sanoma-ja-aikakauslehdet). Digitized holdings, access and subject index guidance read 2026-11-22. Used for choosing the access route and the old Finnish newspaper subject index.

- **S4:** National Library of Sweden, [Svenska tidningar](https://tidningar.kb.se/). Search landing page and title list read 2026-11-22. Used for identifying the separate Swedish newspaper search. No individual title or issue was assumed to be present.

The excerpt points out the end of the 1827 run. The blog request was blocked. The University of Turku independently supports the interruption. No sole-title claim is used.

- **S6:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Kotikatuni arkeologinen kohde](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2024/01/kotikatuni-arkeologinen-kohde.html).
