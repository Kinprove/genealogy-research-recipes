## Sources

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library, Roger Minert, [German Census Records: A New Genealogical Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bHzY0uOFiY), published 2023-03-01.
  - *Used for:* census scope and coverage; triennial customs-union counts; content by period and the coexisting list formats; the cataloguing headings beyond "census" and the term *Volkszählung*; archive denials later contradicted; survival-location estimates; regional and state archive holdings, including transfers; the claimed return of records to Poland and France; gazetteer use and lowest-level contact; the fee offer; rarity of indexes.
- **S2:** BYU Family History Library, Roger Minert, [Census Records in Germany 1816-1916](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gMockz7_dBY), published 2021-01-15.
  - *Used for:* miscatalogued house lists; searching at county level or lower; the city/town-then-county order; the low yield of state-archive requests; German-language holdings in Polish archives; the 1875 forms; separating actual pages from statistics.
- **S3:** BYU Family History Library, Roger Minert, [Residential Registration in Germany](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pFiPgYQobzg), published 2021-01-29.
  - *Used for:* the Speyer/Rohrbach worked example; the birthplace, previous-residence and destination columns; the 100-year transfer and privacy restriction; the municipal-site route to the Einwohnermeldeamt or Stadtarchiv; the *Einwohner* Findbuch search; combined census and registration requests; fees; the port-city suggestion.
- **S4:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Erfahrungen mit Archiven](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/114127/), thread started 2009-09-09.
  - *Used for:* a participant's report of GDR-era Kreismeldekartei cards received from a Kreisarchiv and their contents; differing release terms, including a data-protection refusal; the suggestion to ask for the legal basis of a refusal under the state archive law.
- **S5:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Suche nach meinem Urgroßvater](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/3014348/), 2026-05-12.
  - *Used for:* the case of a 1947 deregistration to a named village, followed by "no further entries" replies from local and state archives, and then the person's appearance in a 1950 district address book, as reported by participants.

**What the sources supply and what is authored:** S1–S3 are lectures by one presenter. His survival percentages, fee examples, archive anecdotes and the record-return agreement are his own claims and estimates. S4 and S5 are forum participant reports. The following are authored synthesis, not the presenter's or participants' procedure: the contact ordering as a decision rule, the reply-status register, the place-role fields, the confirmation gate for inferred birthplaces, the rule of treating card entries as leads, the forward-following rule for departure entries, and the German heading variants other than *Volkszählung* and *Einwohner* (standard archival vocabulary matched to the headings he names).

**Not verified:** current fee levels; current FamilySearch Catalog headings and holdings, including the Speyer church films. Some presenter figures and forum reports remain unconfirmed. Not every presenter remark is clear in the recordings.
