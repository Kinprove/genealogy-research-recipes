The following are authored synthesis, not the presenter's or participants' procedure: the contact ordering as a decision rule, the reply-status register, the place-role fields, the confirmation gate for inferred birthplaces, the rule of treating card entries as leads, the forward-following rule for departure entries, and the German heading variants other than *Volkszählung* and *Einwohner* (standard archival vocabulary matched to the headings he names).

**Not verified:** current fee levels; current FamilySearch Catalog headings and holdings, including the Speyer church films. Some presenter figures and forum reports remain unconfirmed. Not every presenter remark is clear in the recordings.
