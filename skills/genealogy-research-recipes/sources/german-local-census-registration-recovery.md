## Sources

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library, Roger Minert, [German Census Records: A New Genealogical Source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9bHzY0uOFiY), published 2023-03-01.
  - *Used for:* census scope and coverage; triennial customs-union counts; content by period and the coexisting list formats; the cataloguing headings beyond "census" and the term *Volkszählung*; archive denials later contradicted; survival-location estimates; regional and state archive holdings, including transfers; the claimed return of records to Poland and France; gazetteer use and lowest-level contact; the fee offer; rarity of indexes.
- **S2:** BYU Family History Library, Roger Minert, [Census Records in Germany 1816-1916](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gMockz7_dBY), published 2021-01-15.
  - *Used for:* miscatalogued house lists; searching at county level or lower; the city/town-then-county order; the low yield of state-archive requests; German-language holdings in Polish archives; the 1875 forms; separating actual pages from statistics.
- **S3:** BYU Family History Library, Roger Minert, [Residential Registration in Germany](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pFiPgYQobzg), published 2021-01-29.
  - *Used for:* the Speyer/Rohrbach worked example; the birthplace, previous-residence and destination columns; the 100-year transfer and privacy restriction; the municipal-site route to the Einwohnermeldeamt or Stadtarchiv; the *Einwohner* Findbuch search; combined census and registration requests; fees; the port-city suggestion.
- **S4:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Erfahrungen mit Archiven](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/114127/), thread started 2009-09-09.
  - *Used for:* a participant's report of GDR-era Kreismeldekartei cards received from a Kreisarchiv and their contents; differing release terms, including a data-protection refusal; the suggestion to ask for the legal basis of a refusal under the state archive law.
- **S5:** Ahnenforschung.net Forum, [Suche nach meinem Urgroßvater](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/3014348/), 2026-05-12.
  - *Used for:* the case of a 1947 deregistration to a named village, followed by "no further entries" replies from local and state archives, and then the person's appearance in a 1950 district address book, as reported by participants.
- **S6:** Bayerischer Landesverein für Familienkunde e.V., [Recherchemöglichkeiten im Bayerischen Staatsarchiv in Landshut](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AzU31Iw2B0k), 2022-01-26; original saved transcript window checked 2026-11-06.
  - *Used for:* the settlement/permission finder-to-personal-file route in the presentation's patrimonial-administration discussion, an origin lead and examples of surviving personal enclosures. The transcript is noisy; no depicted finder, contents table, attachment or original file was inspected. The example establishes neither a universal attachment set or administrative date boundary nor present custody, survival, access or a person's origin.
- **S7:** Bayerischer Landesverein für Familienkunde e.V., [Familienforschung Stadtarchiv Augsburg, Teil 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CvkUR0-sqBQ), 2022-02-20; original saved transcript window checked 2026-11-06.
  - *Used for:* a residential occupation list followed to a trade card and a separate trade-permission file, attributed testimony in that file and a separately described municipal personnel file. No original card, file, depicted extract or employment record was inspected. Independent evidence of municipal employment as the gate to a personnel-file search is authored synthesis; no general licensing rule, adverse character claim, complete career or present holdings/access is established.
- **S8:** Landesarchiv Schleswig-Holstein (ed.), [Kommentierte Beständeübersicht des Landesarchivs Schleswig-Holstein](https://hup.sub.uni-hamburg.de/oa-pub/catalog/download/176/ebook/937?inline=1), Veröffentlichungen des Landesarchivs Schleswig-Holstein 120, Hamburg University Press, 2020, DOI 10.15460/HUP.LASH.120.200, p. 420 ("Abt. 412 Volkszähllisten"); opened 2026-11-07.
  - *Used for:* the census lists in Abt. 412 (1803 for Schleswig and Holstein, 1860 for Holstein, 1864 for Holstein and Lauenburg, 1845 for a few Karrharde parishes); the lists of 1835, 1840, 1845 and 1855 for both duchies and of 1860 for Schleswig held in the Rigsarkivet in Copenhagen, on microfilm in Abt. 415.
- **S9:** Arbeitskreis Volkszahl-Register (AKVZ), [akvz.de](https://www.akvz.de/), opened 2026-11-07.
  - *Used for:* the free database of handwritten censuses up to 1864 for Schleswig, Holstein, Lauenburg and Mecklenburg-Schwerin, named in step 3 and in the index exception under "Limits". The page gives no coverage by year; per-year and per-district completeness was not verified.

**What the sources supply and what is authored:** S1–S3 are lectures by one presenter. His survival percentages, fee examples, archive anecdotes and the record-return agreement are his own claims and estimates. S4 and S5 are forum participant reports. S8 and S9 are an archive's printed holdings guide and an index operator's page, read on 2026-11-07. The following are authored synthesis, not the presenter's or participants' procedure: the contact ordering as a decision rule, the reply-status register, the place-role fields, the confirmation gate for inferred birthplaces, the rule of treating card entries as leads, the forward-following rule for departure entries, and the German heading variants other than *Volkszählung* and *Einwohner* (standard archival vocabulary matched to the headings he names).

**Not verified:** current fee levels; current FamilySearch Catalog headings and holdings, including the Speyer church films. Some presenter figures and forum reports remain unconfirmed. Not every presenter remark is clear in the recordings.

- **S10:** Rigsarkivet (Danish National Archives), [From the Danish Demographic Database to Search the Collections](https://en.rigsarkivet.dk/guide/from-the-danish-demographic-database-to-search-the-collections/) (guide, last updated 2026-05-01), read 2026-11-13.
  - *Used for:* the Danish Demographic Database (ddd.dda.dk) being phased out; all its data moving to Search the Collections, whose interface is in Danish; most datasets expected there by 1 September 2026; its warnings that a dataset may not yet be in Search the Collections and that ddd.dda.dk searches can take a very long time or fail to return results.

- **S11:** FamilySearch Blog, [Using the Mecklenburg Census Records](https://www.familysearch.org/en/blog/using-the-mecklenburg-census-records), published 2012-05-11, read 2026-11-13.
  - *Used for:* the Mecklenburg-Schwerin censuses of 1867, 1890 and 1900 online at FamilySearch in May 2012, with a searchable index for 1890 and 1900 and none then for 1867, which had to be browsed by town. The collection pages answered a bot check on 2026-11-13, so present coverage was not verified.
