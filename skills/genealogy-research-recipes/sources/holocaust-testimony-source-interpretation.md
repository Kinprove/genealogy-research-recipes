## Sources

Primary material is paraphrased. The statement worksheet and conflict-handling procedure are
original synthesis, applying the existing evidence/proof recipe to these source types.

- **H1:** Yad Vashem, [Guide to Genealogical Resources at Yad Vashem](https://wwv.yadvashem.org/yv/en/images/resources_at_yad_vashem_june2011.pdf),
  June 2011, pp. 1–2; opened 18 October 2026. Supports mixed database origins, survivor entries,
  non-family/researcher submissions and incomplete documentation. Historical counts, interface
  instructions, update frequency, contacts and service terms are not adopted as current facts.
- **H2:** Yad Vashem, [Instructions for Submitting Pages of Testimony](https://wwv.yadvashem.org/yv/en/downloads/pdf/daf_ed_en.pdf),
  undated form, first page; opened 18 October 2026. A submitter need not be a relative; the
  instructions permit “Holocaust” when death circumstances are unknown. The recipe interprets
  that allowance narrowly; it does not certify any individual submission's accuracy.
- **H3:** J-Roots Forum, "Отголоски семейных историй. Тема для пробных постов начинающих генеалогов",
  first post 2010-05-16, <https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=20&t=5084>. Rights: cited
  reference, not reproduced. Used for the reported difference between the original evacuation card
  (birthplace, nationality) and the summary certificate issued from it.
- **H4:** J-Roots Forum, thread "Музей Холокоста / Вашингтон", first post 2016-02-12,
  <https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=167&t=4906>. Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used for examples of USHMM copy collections from Kazakh regional archives whose
  finding aids describe evacuation files by original file and folio.
- **H5:** J-Roots Forum, thread "Холокост: обсуждаем документы и публикации", first post 2009-09-17,
  <https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=26&t=138>. Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used as the mixed-list discussion that motivates classifying each component of a
  mixed postwar bundle, and keeping the stated compiler separate from a later forwarding or
  stamping body.
- **H6:** J-Roots Forum, thread "Яд-ваШем - база данных жертв Холокоста", first post 2009-12-02,
  <https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=26&t=1196>. Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used for partial-list examples that motivate seeking other described parts of the
  same original case and preparing a request for an unavailable part.
- **H7:** J-Roots Forum, thread "Эвакуация - поиск данных об эвакуированных", first post 2009-08-29,
  <https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=197&t=1199>. Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used as the evacuation-source discussion that motivates the source-class checks
  (what moved, why the record was created, overlapping volumes) and the household-card crosswalk.
- **H8:** J-Roots Forum, thread "Эвакуация из Городка Витебской области", first post 2021-12-12,
  <https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=197&t=12365>. Rights: cited reference, not
  reproduced. Used as the regional compilation example that motivates keeping the region and
  source-list reference with each row or family counter.
- **H9:** Agency «Узархив» of the Republic of Uzbekistan, [evacuation database](https://evacuation.archive.uz/),
  opened 2026-11-04. Used for the operator, the name search, and what two opened record pages
  showed on that date: a "Holocaust" fund label on evacuees recorded as Russian, a list number with
  a position on the list and no page, and a placeholder birth date.
- **H10:** State Archive of the Republic of Mari El, [databases of the archive](https://gosarhivrme.ru/ru/bazy-dannykh-gosudarstvennogo-arkhiva-respubliki-mariy-el),
  opened 2026-11-04. Used for the published lists of citizens evacuated to the Mari ASSR in
  1941–1945 at evacuation.gosarhivrme.ru.
