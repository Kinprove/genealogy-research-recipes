## Sources

Primary material is paraphrased. The statement worksheet and conflict-handling procedure are
original synthesis, applying the existing evidence/proof recipe to these source types.

- **H1:** Yad Vashem, [Guide to Genealogical Resources at Yad Vashem](https://wwv.yadvashem.org/yv/en/images/resources_at_yad_vashem_june2011.pdf),
  June 2011, pp. 1–2; opened 18 October 2026. Supports mixed database origins, survivor entries,
  non-family/researcher submissions and incomplete documentation. Historical counts, interface
  instructions, update frequency, contacts and service terms are not adopted as current facts.
- **H2:** Yad Vashem, [Instructions for Submitting Pages of Testimony](https://wwv.yadvashem.org/yv/en/downloads/pdf/daf_ed_en.pdf),

  instructions permit “Holocaust” when death circumstances are unknown. The recipe interprets
  that allowance narrowly; it does not certify any individual submission's accuracy.
