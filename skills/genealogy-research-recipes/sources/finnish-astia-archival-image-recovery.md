# Sources for Recover a Finnish archival image through Astia and Digihakemisto

- **S1:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Astiassa on puu](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/01/astiassa-on-puu.html). Community post and its attributed reports read 2026-11-22. The author used the alphabetical tree for the Finnish Meteorological Institute. The report about hidden results comes from researchers quoted in the post.

- **S2:** [National Archives of Finland, Selaa aineistoja](https://astia.narc.fi/uusiastia/arkistot.html). Public alphabetical listing description and purpose declaration read 2026-11-22. The purpose panel describes conditional visibility of personal data. A signed-in search was outside the check.

- **S3:** [National Archives of Finland, Mummonmökin historiaa (hakuohje)](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/mummonmokin-historiaa-hakuohje). Property research guide sections on court records, inventories and poll-tax rolls read 2026-11-22. Used for archive, series, year and image-view navigation, the digital-material filter, and court-specific estate inventory examples.

- **S4:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Astia herättää tunteita](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2022/04/astia-herattaa-tunteita.html). Community post on the 2022 Astia change read 2026-11-22. The author quotes researchers on failed links, a filtered court-book search and old numeric IDs, and describes retained Digihakemisto indexes. No current recurrence is claimed.

- **S5:** [Migration Institute of Finland, Sukututkija siirtolaisten jäljillä](https://www.doria.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/192818/sukututkija-siirtolaisten-jaljilla-osat-1-4_2024-1.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1). Digihakemisto and Astia sections read 2026-11-22. The guide distinguishes archive-level search from unit-level images and tables of contents and shows the Henkikirjat, court and SAY entry points. The live Digihakemisto interface returned HTTP 403 during this check.

- **S6:** [Tampere Region Genealogical Society, Ajankohtaista](https://tamsuku.fi/ajankohtaista.html). Announcement of Digihakemisto text search dated 16 August 2023 read 2026-11-22. The announcement identifies page descriptions as the searchable layer and gives people and places as example terms.

- **S7:** [National Archives of Finland, Henkikirjat](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/henkikirjat). Finding sections for Swedish-period and later poll-tax rolls read 2026-11-22. Used for the provincial series and the parish index 120:1, which excludes towns.

- **S8:** [National Archives of Finland, Puolustushallinnon aineistot](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/puolustushallinnon-aineistot). Astia search guidance and war-diary example read 2026-11-22. Used for card metadata, truncation, creator filtering and looking up a diary by unit.

- **S9:** [National Archives of Finland, Astia, Kansallisarkiston asiointipalvelu](https://www.suomi.fi/palvelut/verkkoasiointi/astia-kansallisarkiston-asiointipalvelu-kansallisarkisto/f3072c49-85be-4404-8925-516682975f57). Service description and identification requirement read 2026-11-22. Used with Astia’s purpose notice. Neither source promises access to a particular restricted item.

- **S10:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Astia ei (minusta) edelleenkään toimi](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/01/astia-ei-minusta-edelleenkaan-toimi.html). Community post on the Pori register descriptions read 2026-11-22. The author started in Digihakemisto and uses the official descriptions to distinguish the burial listing from a mixed register.

- **S11:** [National Archives of Finland, Pori parish register I C1:3](https://www.finna.fi/Record/narc.VAKKA-184885.KA_VAKKA-331659.KA). Finna item title and full contents note read 2026-11-22. The volume is titled for births and baptisms and the contents note also lists marriages and burials for the town and rural parishes. No individual entry was inspected.

- **S12:** [National Archives of Finland, Pori burial register I F1:4](https://www.finna.fi/Record/narc.VAKKA-184885.KA_VAKKA-331733.KA). Finna item title and parent series read 2026-11-22. Used for the similarly dated Pori town burial listing.

- **S13:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Astia, kuvailutieto, käytettävyys ja pysyvät linkit](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2024/04/astia-kuvailutieto-kaytettavyys-ja.html). Community post on poll-tax roll word searches read 2026-11-22. The text was read in the blog's weekly archive for 14 April 2024 after the direct post returned an error. The reported near-complete set of links was specific to the author's search.
