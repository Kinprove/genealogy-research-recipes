**Authored synthesis.** The following is workflow design, not taken from any one presenter: the stable item references and parent-page links, the surface states (captured, missing, uninspected, inaccessible), the file roles, the progress-marker contents, the join check, the found-elsewhere versus not-located distinction, the handover record and the hand-off check.
