Used: nitrate film sold from August 1889 and made in various formats until the early 1950s; Kodak as the only maker that published end dates; its flammability; separating nitrate negatives at once; a "nitrate" marking not proving the base; and unmarked amateur roll film.

Used: acetate replacing nitrate from the 1920s; nitrate as a fire risk, kept separate from other records; copied film carrying the original's edge printing; nitrate duplicated or digitized only by a knowledgeable company or a person specially trained to handle it; a vinegar odour as a typical sign of acetate deterioration; and isolating deteriorating acetate film.
- **S19:** Image Permanence Institute, [User's Guide for A-D Strips](https://s3.cad.rit.edu/ipi-assets/publications/adstrips_instructions.pdf), seventh edition, 2022, opened 2026-11-06. Used: clean hands or clean nitrile gloves; handling by the edges without touching the image; no paper clips, rubber bands, other fasteners, self-adhesive tape, sticky notes or glue; marking the housing in preference to the photograph; and, where a mark is needed, soft graphite pencil on the back near the edge.
- **S21:** U.S. National Park Service, [Conserve O Gram 14/4: Caring for Photographs: General Guidelines](https://www.nps.gov/subjects/museums/upload/14-04_508.pdf), June 1997, opened 2026-11-06. Used: do not laminate original photographs.

Used: a signed deed of gift transferring legal ownership of the donated physical or digital materials; intellectual property rights that may also be transferred by the deed, or a separate licence for digital content; and a donor's inability to transfer rights in works created by others.
- **S23:** U.S. Copyright Office, [Copyright Law of the United States, Chapter 2: Copyright Ownership and Transfer](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap2.html), opened 2026-11-06. **Authored synthesis.** The following is workflow design, not taken from any one presenter: the stable item references and parent-page links, the surface states (captured, missing, uninspected, inaccessible), the file roles, the progress-marker contents, the join check, the found-elsewhere versus not-located distinction, the handover record and the hand-off check. None of these is a native export header or a platform schema. The display-derivative rule applies the recipe's own derivative control. The presenters supply the examples and the failure modes. The capture and handling limits come from the conservation and archive pages cited as S13–S21; grouping them by object type, and keeping suspected nitrate out of routine capture altogether, are Kinprove synthesis from those pages.
