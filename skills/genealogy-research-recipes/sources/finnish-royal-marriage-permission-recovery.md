# Sources for Trace a royal marriage permission in Swedish-era Finland

- **S1:** [Turun tuomiokapituli](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/turun-tuomiokapituli). Finnish National Archives guide to the Turku Cathedral Chapter read 2026-11-22. Used for the surviving royal letters and their archive route.

- **S2:** [Serkuksia kuninkaan luvalla naimisiin 1760-luvulla](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/05/serkuksia-kuninkaan-luvalla-naimisiin.html). Published 2023-05-09. Kaisa Kyläkoski’s community post read 2026-11-22. Used for the E I 9 letter route, the difference between the authorized and recorded spouse, and the Keuruu baptism preceding both authorization and marriage. The linked parish entries were not independently inspected.

- **S3:** [Kuninkaan luvalla alaikäisenä naimisiin](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/03/kuninkaan-luvalla-alaikaisena-naimisiin.html). Published 2023-03-08. Kaisa Kyläkoski’s community post read 2026-11-22. Used for the E I 8 letter route, testing minority against dated records and identifying a parish from a village. The numerical age rule is outside the recipe’s scope.

- **S4:** [Preussin ottamien sotavankien vaimot](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/08/preussin-ottamien-sotavankien-vaimot.html). Published 2023-08-20. Kaisa Kyläkoski’s community post read 2026-11-22. Used for the royal letters in E I 9 and the distinction between a missing soldier and death, and between permission and the parish wedding.

- **S5:** [Att söka kungligt tillstånd för giftermål](https://septentrio.uit.no/index.php/1700/issue/download/260/20). Bonnie Clementsson’s article in Sjuttonhundratal read 2026-11-22. Used for the Justitierevisionen proceedings and for the role of the underlying applications and kinship statements.

- **S6:** [Evankelis-luterilaiset seurakunnat](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/evankelis-luterilaiset-seurakunnat). Finnish National Archives guide to Lutheran parish records read 2026-11-22. Used for HisKi as a finder and for verifying its entries in the original registers.

- **S7:** [Serkuksia kuninkaan luvalla naimisiin 1758](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023_11_26_archive.html). Published 2023-11-29. Kaisa Kyläkoski’s community post in the blog’s weekly archive read 2026-11-22. Used for the E I 8 cousin-dispensation examples and the case of a November permission followed by a wedding the next March.
