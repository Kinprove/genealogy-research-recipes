# Sources for Trace county record series through WPA inventories

- **S1:** Just Genealogy, [What Did They Mean By That? And More New Genealogy Books at Heritage Books - JG0300](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dx4B6IzOgbs), community video, published 2023-05-07. Supplied transcript excerpts reviewed 2026-11-22. The video page could not be opened. The presenter suggests trying FamilySearch, using inventory entries to establish former existence and starting with the clerk to find where the series went. S5 supplies the author’s correct name.

- **S2:** National Archives, [Records of the Work Projects Administration, RG 69](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/069.html). Historical Records Survey section read 2026-11-22. Used for the purpose and records of the survey, including field supervisors’ reports and project applications.

- **S3:** FamilySearch, [How do I use the FamilySearch Catalog to learn about the digitized historical records on FamilySearch?](https://articles.familysearch.org/hc/en-us/articles/57917254058395-How-do-I-use-the-FamilySearch-Catalog-to-learn-about-the-digitized-historical-records-on-FamilySearch). Catalog instructions read 2026-11-22. Used for place and title search and access indicators. The route does not establish coverage of an individual county.

- **S4:** Geneanet, [Inventory of the county archives of Ohio, no. 48, Lucas County (Toledo)](https://en.geneanet.org/library/doc/5592838/inventory-of-the-county-archives-of-ohio-no-48-lucas-county-toledo). Bibliographic description read 2026-11-22. Used for the original volume number, title and year. Text of the volume was not inspected.

- **S5:** Heritage Books, [Additions and Corrections to the W.P.A. Inventory of Lucas County, Ohio: Toledo](https://heritagebooks.com/products/101-b3041). Publisher description read 2026-11-22. Used for the title, author Jana Sloan Broglin and scope of the expanded edition. Text of the edition was not inspected.

- **S6:** Library of Congress, [Ohio: Local History & Genealogy Resource Guide, Courthouse Records](https://guides.loc.gov/ohio-local-history-genealogy/courthouse-records). Courthouse records guide read 2026-11-22. Used for routing county records by their record type and checking county offices, state and regional repositories for holdings and copies.
