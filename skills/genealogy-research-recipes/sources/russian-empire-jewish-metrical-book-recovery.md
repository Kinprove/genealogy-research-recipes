Used for: registration at the nearest synagogue with rare exceptions; the Rechitsa 1905–1915 loss report and the archive replies; the correction about a candidate town in another guberniya; separate replies from the national and zonal archives; metrical extracts in personal files; and surviving uezd and volost papers.
- **S5:** J-Roots Forum, [Не знаете, где спросить? Спросите здесь](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=75&t=7576), thread started 2009-08-24. Used for: Seda having no surviving books while other Telshi-uezd towns survive, read as possibly having no crown rabbinate, with registration not necessarily in Telshi.

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The following are authored synthesis: the jurisdiction table, the per-rabbinate survival check, the substitute register, the rule of ranking rather than excluding, the overlap search for ZAGS transition years, and the order of steps. The forum threads supply the worked examples, most archive locators and the participants' descriptions of practice. All five are threads on one forum, and several key statements come from a small number of regular participants, so agreement across threads is not independent confirmation. The notebook description is a participant's quotation of another thread citing the Svod zakonov, so it is second-hand.
