**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The series checklist, the status register, the post-1874 police row, the per-member label rule and the non-confessant rule are authored synthesis. The fonds and series in the table are what forum participants transcribed or listed for Ekaterinburg and Shadrinsk. They are worked examples, not a general inventory. S1–S4 come largely from the same few participants on one regional forum, so they are not independent of each other. Not verified: that comparable series exist or survive elsewhere, leaf numbers beyond the transcriptions, and every claim in step 8.
