# Sources for Reconstruct a Danish household from church books and probate

- **S1:** [Rigsarkivet, Kirkebøger – Kom godt i gang!](https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/vejledning/kirkeboger-borgerlige-vielser-m-m/kirkeboeger-kom-godt-i-gang/). Reviewed 2026-11-18, web-page, access read. Read 2026-11-18. Rigsarkivet's church-book guide. Used the holdings range; the royal letters of 1645 and 1646; the 1812 rescript, preprinted forms from about 1814 and the two copies kept by the priest and the parish clerk; the period routes before about 1814, about 1814–1960 and from 1961; the optional pre-1814 entries; the event order and the males-first rule; the moves lists in some parishes; the 100-year access period, blurred CPR numbers and blurred birth-list remarks from 1892; DigDag for finding the parish; the pointer to a separate guide for recognised faith communities.

- **S2:** [Rigsarkivet, Kirkebøger fra 1800-tallet – Dét finder du i dem](https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Kirkeboeger-fra-1800-tallet-Det-finder-du-i-dem.pdf). Reviewed 2026-11-18, pdf, access read. Read 2026-11-18. Rigsarkivet's leaflet on nineteenth-century church books. Used the special forms from about 1812; the two sets written by the priest and by the schoolteacher or parish clerk, stored apart and meant to agree; the moves lists until 1854 in towns and 1875 in the countryside, mostly servants and poor people; the contents of baptism, confirmation, marriage and burial entries.

- **S3:** [Rigsarkivet, Borgerlige vielser – Kom godt i gang!](https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/vejledning/kirkeboger-borgerlige-vielser-m-m/borgerlige-vielser-kom-godt-i-gang/). Reviewed 2026-11-18, web-page, access read. Read 2026-11-18. Rigsarkivet's civil-marriage guide. Used who could marry civilly from 1851 and from 1923; the authorities and series for 1851–1919, 1919–1922, 1923–1970 and from 1971; the Copenhagen and South Jutland exceptions; the note at the back of the church book, up to 1922 in the bride's parish; town magistrate archives.

- **S4:** [Rigsarkivet, Sønderjyske kirkebøger og personregistrering – Kom godt i gang!](https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/vejledning/kirkeboger-borgerlige-vielser-m-m/soenderjyske-kirkeboeger-og-personregistrering-kom-godt-i-gang/). Reviewed 2026-11-18, web-page, access read. Read 2026-11-18. Rigsarkivet's guide to South Jutland church books and civil registers. Used the oldest books, fixed forms from 1763 and two copies from 1775; Prussian civil registration from 1874 and the church books as secondary sources for 1874–1925; the return of marriages to the priests from 1926; the registration districts; the period routes to 1973.

- **S5:** [Rigsarkivet, Skiftearkivalier – Kom godt i gang!](https://www.rigsarkivet.dk/vejledning/skifter-og-testamenter/skiftearkivalier-kom-godt-i-gang/).
