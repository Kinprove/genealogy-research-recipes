# Source notes: ELIB Ukrainian archive reference books

## Provider examples checked 2026-10-18

- **S1:** Institute of History of Ukraine, [item 0014010](https://resource.history.org.ua/item/0014010).
  The card identifies the Chernihiv regional archive guide, volume 1 (2001), and
  links its full text. It supports the specific identifier, bibliographic fields
  and catalogue-to-PDF route, not a claim of complete catalogue coverage.
- **S2:** [Full-text PDF linked by that card](https://resource.history.org.ua/cgi-bin/eiu/history.exe?C21COM=2&I21DBN=ELIB&P21DBN=ELIB&Image_file_name=book/0014010.pdf&IMAGE_FILE_DOWNLOAD=0).
  The PDF was accessible; text extraction from its first pages was garbled.
  The recipe's visual/OCR fallback is a response to that observed extraction
  limitation, not a claim that all ELIB books have the same extraction problem.

## Original workflow synthesis

Bounded neighbouring-ID discovery, inspecting volume/part and fund scope, keeping
printed versus PDF pagination, and checking current holdings independently from
a historical guide are Kinprove's research procedures. S3–S5 supply the
bibliographic facts for the consolidated-catalogue step. The provider does not
promise that adjacent IDs form a collection or that search misses prove absence.
Links cite the guide and metadata; no guide prose or images are reproduced.
