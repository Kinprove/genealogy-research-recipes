# Sources: corpus-seeded name search

Primary pages opened 2026-10-18; cited as references, not reproduced.

- **S1. Unicode Consortium, [UAX #15: Unicode Normalization Forms](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr15/)**, sections 1.1–1.3. Distinguishes canonical/compatibility equivalence and warns that compatibility transformations can remove meaningful distinctions. It does not prescribe name-search masks or removal of Hebrew points.
- **S2. Unicode Consortium, [The Unicode Standard, chapter 9, Hebrew](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode17.0.0/core-spec/chapter-9/)**, sections 9.1.1–9.1.2. Describes separately encoded final letters, Yiddish digraphs and presentation forms. It does not instruct researchers to equate different spellings. A local Python `unicodedata` check also confirmed that NFKD leaves the three Yiddish digraph characters and five final letters intact.
- **S3. JewishGen, [Transcription Rules for JewishGen Databases](https://www.jewishgen.org/databases/%24transcription.html)**, name/town fields and section V. Supports the distinction between displayed text and hidden fields used for typed-field indexing.
- **S4. JewishGen, [Revision Lists for Belarus](https://www.jewishgen.org/databases/Belarus/BelarusRevisionLists.htm)**, given-name search instructions. Documents a phonetic-search miss involving a diminutive; the collection-specific example does not measure every engine's recall.
- **S5. James Tanner, BYU Family History Library**: "What if you don't speak your ancestors' language? Foreign Language Resources" (2022-12-15, [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1CzAygZqg2Q)); "Finding Your Ancestors in Iceland" (2021-04-29, [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4jhIdvmyFUw)); "Discovering your Ancestors in Mexico" (2021-06-08, [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=siMS_6gGKYs)). Used for the observation that extra native letters change alphabetical filing and may be stripped in transcribed databases. No per-index collation is verified.
- **S6. Genealogy Gems, [Episode 102 - The 2nd Season of the Generations Project with the Executive Producer Kendall Wilcox](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ClvFJDrlrUw)** (2016-12-23). Used for browsing adjacent surname cards in an image-only death index, with the found card treated as an unconfirmed candidate.
- **S7. Maureen Brady, BYU Family History Library, [Quhat's in a Nayme](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aL2BGQ7raRw)** (2022-03-15); **Laurie Castillo, BYU Family History Library, [Why Can't I Find My Family? Digging Deeper on Surnames](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDW8dEp5mN0)** (2022-09-14); **Denyse Allen, [Ancestor Name Variations Got You Stuck? This AI Method Works](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XJeAPcgLHpc)** (2026-04-26). Used for recording the origin, record type and place of each variant, and for noticing spelling changes that coincide with a new recorder.
- **S8. Larry Jensen, BYU Family History Library, [Understanding German Cultural Background – Larry Jensen (25 January 2024)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g8pltErjQpU)**, published 2024-01-26. Used for the point that court, military or district offices in another dialect area may record a parish family's name in that area's form.
- **S9. Roger Minert, BYU Family History Library, [Your First Confrontation with an Old Document in German(y) – Dr. Roger Minert (27 June 2024)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cc8_6tNN8C4)**, published 2024-07-01. Used for Latinized church given names and French-administration renderings in Alsace-Lorraine.
- **S10. Angela Sinickas, BYU Family History Library, [Lutherans in Lithuania - Angela Sinickas (7 Nov 2019)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xoY83Sx3ANk)**, published 2019-11-18. Used for different pastors recording the same person under German and Lithuanian forms.
- **S11. Ahnenforschung.net Forum, "Humoris causa"** (first post 2006-07-14, [thread](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/928/)). Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for indexed entry-formula words and inherited 'unknown' placeholders mistaken for given names, and for a browser-translated index list whose surnames became common words, detected because they broke the sort order. Re-read 2026-11-07 in the retained thread text, with the [farm-name chains, post 18](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/928-humoris-causa/) and [Latinized university name, post 690](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/928-humoris-causa/?pageNo=35) also read online. Also used for:
  - farm names carried through successive marriages, including stacked `gnt.` names;
  - the discussion identifying `Magni Ketterlini` as genitive forms of `Magnus Ketterlinus` in a child's entry;
  - a Helmstedt matriculation with a Latinized name and separate origin adjectives.
  These are participant reports, not independently inspected register or matriculation images; they establish neither a general conversion rule nor the prevalence of the naming practices.
- **S12. Уральская генеалогия, [Зауральское Приисетье - земля Шадринская. Фамильный указатель](https://rodgersforum.borda.ru/?1-7-0-00000258-000-0-0-1776484154=)** (first post undated). Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for the compiler's caution that index headings formed from given names may be patronymics rather than surnames.
- **S13. Уральская генеалогия, "Гражданская война 1918-1922 годов. Участники гражданской войны"** (first post undated, [thread](https://rodgersforum.borda.ru/?1-23-0-00000012-000-0-0-1714980847=)) and **"Словарь родоведа. Уточнение терминов и выражений"** (first post undated, [thread](https://rodgersforum.borda.ru/?1-0-0-00000141-000-0-0-1701453218=)). Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for -ских/-ские and -ов/-ев surname-ending alternation as search lanes within one family; the pre-reform-letter explanation was not verified.
- **S14. J-Roots Forum, "Евреи Шяуляя, 19 век"** (first post 2018-05-01, [thread](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=164&t=6963)). Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for reported transcription errors that swapped given name and patronymic or replaced a given name with a dissimilar one.
- **S15. Ahnenforschung.net Forum, "Familienbuch Hajos"** (first post 2010-03-06, [thread](https://forum.ahnenforschung.net/forum/thread/17915/)). Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for removing the feminine -in suffix and checking a misread surname against a family book's variants and list of wives.
- **S16. J-Roots Forum, "ФОРУМ: функция Поиск... Вопросы и ответы"** (first post 2012-01-23, [thread](https://forum.j-roots.info/viewtopic.php?f=181&t=9163)). Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for a site search that silently ignored an over-long term while returning hits for the other query word, and for retrying with a shorter wildcard stem.
- **S17. Совет Народных Комиссаров, [Декрет о введении новой орфографии](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Декрет_о_введении_новой_орфографии)** (10 October 1918; Wikisource text), opened 2026-11-02. Used for: replacing ѣ, ѳ and і with е, ф and и, dropping word-final ъ while keeping it as a separator inside words, and the decree's silence on ижица.
- **S18. Народный комиссариат просвещения, [Декрет о введении нового правописания](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Декрет_о_введении_нового_правописания)** (23 December 1917; Wikisource text), opened 2026-11-02. Used for: the earlier decree setting the same letter rules.
- **S19. Википедия, [Ижица](https://ru.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ижица)**, opened 2026-11-02. Used for: ижица being rare by the reform, unnamed in the reform decrees, falling out of use gradually and still appearing in some parish-register names.
- **S20. Віртуальны музей савецкіх рэпрэсій у Беларусі, search for a common surname in [Russian spelling](https://represii.net/repressed?search=Иванов) and in [Belarusian spelling](https://represii.net/repressed?search=Іваноў)**, opened 2026-11-02. Used for: 0 hits in Russian spelling against 194 in Belarusian spelling on that date. A dated observation; counts change.
- **S21. Жніво, [Belarus genealogical databases: search help and terms](https://db.zhnivo.com/by/belarus-genealogical-databases/)**, opened 2026-11-02. Used for: entering the surname in Russian, `|` alternatives and the `*` wildcard, the coverage statement, the warning about machine-read names, and the ban on automated bulk or partial download without the project's permission.
- **S22. Древо Жизни Форум, "О толковании записей в метрических церковных книгах"** (first post 2024-05-20, [thread](https://genery.com/ru/forum/viewtopic.php?t=4868)). Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for retrieving an entry whose target name may be misread by searching for an independently known co-listed participant, then reading the target in the full entry.
- **S23. Tesseract OCR, [src/api/baseapi.cpp](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract/main/src/api/baseapi.cpp)** (`GetTSVText`), opened 2026-11-04. Used for: TSV rows joined by tabs and ended by a newline, with the word text written without quoting or escaping.
- **S24. Python documentation, [csv — CSV File Reading and Writing](https://docs.python.org/3/library/csv.html)**, opened 2026-11-04. Used for: `"` as the default quote character, `QUOTE_MINIMAL` as the default quoting, quoted records spanning several lines, and `QUOTE_NONE` making the reader do no quote processing. A quote opens a quoted field only at the start of a field.
- **S25. pandas documentation, [pandas.read_csv](https://pandas.pydata.org/docs/reference/api/pandas.read_csv.html)**, opened 2026-11-04. Used for: quoted items that can contain the delimiter, `QUOTE_MINIMAL` (0) as the default, and 3 as `csv.QUOTE_NONE`. Neither documentation page describes the row-swallowing failure itself; it follows from the documented behaviour.

Corpus attestation, positive-control queries, surface-form review and separately labelled search lanes are workflow synthesis. The decrees, the encyclopedia entry and the two sites supply the facts for the pre-reform spelling lane and per-database language calibration; S23–S25 supply the facts for the TSV parsing check. No percentage improvement, universal consonant swap or claimed complete name inventory is asserted. Dictionary membership, search counts and successful normalization provide no person-identity or kinship proof.
