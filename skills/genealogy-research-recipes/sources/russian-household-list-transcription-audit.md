**What is authored and what the sources supply.**

The order of the audit, counting people before comparing fields, confirming a reference sample, putting two disputed glyphs side by side, keeping a stroke rule per scribe, anchoring an age on the running number, adding a name level for a relationship chain, the cross-year table for each item, logging finds for other sheets, the classes of the audit log, and the arithmetic on a wide crop are authored synthesis. The sources supply the record formulas and examples, the palaeography advice, the name and kin-term meanings, the editing rules and the model documentation.

S1 to S5, S7 to S9, S13, S19 to S24 and S28 to S32 are guides, reference works and documentation. S6, S10 and S27 are forum posts and are single participants' reports. S11, S14 to S18, S25 and S33 are published transcriptions or translations of individual lists, and they show what one list says, not a general rule. S26 is Kinprove's own private audit record and is not public; the recipe cites it only beside a public source.

Every public entry was opened on 2026-11-10 and checked by matching the passage cited.

The following were NOT verified: any public report of Максим and Михаил confused with each other; any public source for an age written above or below the line of its name; any public source for twins marked with a bracket, or for a margin note on a household split into a separate house; and how widely the fixed-interval ages shown in S11 and S27 occur outside those lists.
