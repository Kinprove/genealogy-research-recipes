## Sources

Based on recorded talks (reviewed 2026-10-20). Selected claims were checked on 2026-10-21 against archive, government and publisher pages. Sources S9–S19 were added on 2026-11-16 and read that day; S9 was read only in transcript excerpts.

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library / James Tanner, [Revolutionary War Records: Patriots to Hessians](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=md30J3MFJk0), published 2017-09-27. Used for: fixing residence and enlistment place; Continental vs militia classification; local militias.
- **S2:** Genealogy Gems / Michael Strauss (Military Minutes), [Genealogist's Complete Guide to Military Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNEdtlxMlpU), published 2026-05-23. Used for: CMSRs at NARA for the volunteer army, excluding regular-army enlistments; the Revolutionary records rebuilt after the 1800 fire.
- **S3:** Denyse Allen with Aaron McWilliams, [All About PA Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6FWARk1ELGk), published 2024-07-25. Used for: militia fines and levies in tax records; militia-loan certificates and cards; Power Library / Comptroller General records; donation-land eligibility and the ranger-company exception.
- **S4:** BYU Family History Library / Michael Strauss, [The Road to Independence: Revolutionary War Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nJsQ9ftU9iM), published 2025-02-08. Used for: Pennsylvania drafted-militia class/company/battalion numbering on active and inactive rosters; the old-war period; state bounty land, Bockstruck, the Massachusetts choice and the location of Virginia warrants; Loyalist newspapers and claim databases.
- **S5:** BYU Family History Library / Marilyn Thomsen, [Military Research: What and When](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C2_9AVcodcg), published 2024-08-20. Used for: the Georgia county-history militia roster and its enumeration notes; the militia-district map for same-named men.
- **S6:** BYU Family History Library / Marilyn Thomsen, [State Military Record Resources](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8i254jbCQDQ), published 2024-09-03. Used for: pay vouchers (amount, date and place claimed) to prove service and separate same-named men.
- **S7:** BYU Family History Library / Alyssa Martinez, [O Say, Can You See: Introducing U.S. Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRt2jTgEqVw), published 2024-06-12. Used for: comparing where units were recruited with census residence; the NARA vs state-archive custodian split.
- **S8:** BYU Family History Library / James Tanner, [Finding Your Ancestors and Relatives with Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cWym50l1PYU), published 2020-11-17. Used for: narrowing a common-name Civil War soldier by birth date, residence and locally raised units.

**Synthesis and limits:** The following are authored synthesis: the classification step, the per-series eligibility table, the evidence-kind labels, the roster-status rule and the candidate-separation table. The presenters supply the examples, the eligibility statements and the record descriptions, each attributed above. The instruction to check pension and state records after an empty or incomplete CMSR search is a routing rule handed to the pension recipe (§4). It is not a measured rate of CMSR omissions.

- **S9:** Just Genealogy (community source), [Why You Can’t Find Your War Of 1812 Soldier In Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fpGPSDcIXww), published 2024-02-03; transcript excerpts read 2026-11-16 (the video page showed only the title and channel). Used for: War of 1812 CMSRs not generally online; following a Register of Enlistments entry back to its rolls; company and descriptive books; state adjutant-general and Society/Daughters of 1812 publications; Virginia men missing from the printed militia volumes; Butler's Virginia regulars. Not used, because no source opened on 2026-11-16 confirmed them: the talk's description of the Virginia volumes as a "land office" publication, its statement that they exclude federalized troops (with a Loudoun County example), its reading of "MR" and "PR" in the Register, its Thomas Balch Library payrolls, and a Tennessee compilation credited to "Eric Johnson" (the title found is Allen's, S19).
- **S10:** National Archives, [Compiled Military Service Records](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/army/compiled-military-service-records), page last reviewed 2020-06-03; read 2026-11-16. Used for: War of 1812 carded service records (NAID 300392) not yet digitized in full; their indexes online at Ancestry and Fold3; select files free in the National Archives Catalog.
- **S11:** Stuart L. Butler, [Genealogical Records of the War of 1812](https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/1991/winter/war-of-1812.html), *Prologue* 23, no. 4 (Winter 1991); article read 2026-11-16. Used for: the fifteen 1798–1815 Register volumes, what an entry can show, and the records the entries were built from; states' published rosters. Historical statements as of 1991.
- **S12:** National Archives, [Indian Scouts, Companies, and Codetalkers](https://www.archives.gov/research/native-americans/military/scouts-enlisted-more.html), page last reviewed 2022-05-02; read on 2026-11-16. Used for: the Registers of Enlistments (M233) online at Ancestry, FamilySearch and Fold3. Limitation: the page is about enlisted Indian scouts; its availability statement concerns the registers as a series.
- **S13:** National Archives, [Guide to Federal Records: Records of United States Army Commands, 1784–1821 (Record Group 98)](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/098.html); guide page read 2026-11-16. Used for: company and order books of 1812–15 regular regiments. Limitation: the web guide is based on the 1995 printed *Guide to Federal Records*.
- **S14:** Library of Virginia, [Virginia War of 1812 Records](https://lva-virginia.libguides.com/war-of-1812), research guide last updated 2026-05-28; read 2026-11-16 at that version. Used for: the two printed militia volumes as the official record; restricted access to the originals; APA 253a rolls not in the printed volumes; federal service and benefit records at the National Archives; Butler's *Virginia Soldiers in the United States Army, 1800–1815* among its published indexes.
- **S15:** Internet Archive, [Report of the Adjutant General of the State of Kentucky. Soldiers of the War of 1812](https://archive.org/details/reportofadjutant01kent) (Frankfort: E. P. Johnson, public printer, 1891); Kentucky item page read 2026-11-16 (catalogue data and scan, not the volume's text). Used for: bibliographic details; the scan.
- **S16:** Kentucky Genealogical Society, [Kentucky's Hidden Heritage: Exploring War of 1812 Veterans' Documents](https://kygs.org/kentucky-war-1812-records), undated; society page read 2026-11-16. Used for: regulars and Virginia units absent from the 1891 report; destruction of the original state-level records after publication. A society's secondary account.
- **S17:** Internet Archive, [Records of Officers and Men of New Jersey in Wars, 1791–1815](https://archive.org/details/recordsofoffice00newj) (Trenton: State Gazette Publishing Co., 1909; New Jersey Adjutant-General's Office); New Jersey item page read 2026-11-16 (catalogue data and contents list). Used for: bibliographic details; War of 1812 coverage; the scan.
- **S18:** Internet Archive, [Roster of Ohio Soldiers in the War of 1812](https://archive.org/details/rosterofohiosold00ohio) (Columbus: Edward T. Miller Co., 1916; Ohio Adjutant General's Office); Ohio item page read 2026-11-16 (catalogue data and scan). Used for: bibliographic details; the subject heading "Ohio -- Militia"; the scan. Limitation: the item page does not state the roster's scope.
- **S19:** Wikipedia, [Penelope Johnson Allen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penelope_Johnson_Allen); article read 2026-11-16. Used for: the 1947 *Tennessee Soldiers in the War of 1812: Regiments of Col. Allcorn and Col. Allison* and its co-credit to the National and Tennessee Societies of the U.S. Daughters of 1812. Limitation: a reference-list entry; the book itself was not seen.

The War of 1812 additions (2026-11-16) are also authored synthesis where they tell the reader what to do: label an online War of 1812 service-record hit as an index entry, treat a Register entry as a derived abstract and follow it to its rolls, search the RG 98 books when the Register is thin, and count a state-roster miss only inside that roster's stated coverage. The coverage and custody statements themselves are attributed to S9–S19 as listed. Reading the Tennessee title as limited to two regiments is an inference from the title.
