## Sources

Based on recorded talks (reviewed 2026-10-20). Selected claims were checked on 2026-10-21 against archive, government and publisher pages.

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library / James Tanner, [Revolutionary War Records: Patriots to Hessians](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=md30J3MFJk0), published 2017-09-27. Used for: fixing residence and enlistment place; Continental vs militia classification; local militias.
- **S2:** Genealogy Gems / Michael Strauss (Military Minutes), [Genealogist's Complete Guide to Military Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNEdtlxMlpU), published 2026-05-23. Used for: CMSRs at NARA for the volunteer army, excluding regular-army enlistments; the Revolutionary records rebuilt after the 1800 fire.
- **S3:** Denyse Allen with Aaron McWilliams, [All About PA Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6FWARk1ELGk), published 2024-07-25. Used for: militia fines and levies in tax records; militia-loan certificates and cards; Power Library / Comptroller General records; donation-land eligibility and the ranger-company exception.
- **S4:** BYU Family History Library / Michael Strauss, [The Road to Independence: Revolutionary War Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nJsQ9ftU9iM), published 2025-02-08. Used for: Pennsylvania drafted-militia class/company/battalion numbering on active and inactive rosters; the old-war period; state bounty land, Bockstruck, the Massachusetts choice and the location of Virginia warrants; Loyalist newspapers and claim databases.
- **S5:** BYU Family History Library / Marilyn Thomsen, [Military Research: What and When](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C2_9AVcodcg), published 2024-08-20. Used for: the Georgia county-history militia roster and its enumeration notes; the militia-district map for same-named men.
- **S6:** BYU Family History Library / Marilyn Thomsen, [State Military Record Resources](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8i254jbCQDQ), published 2024-09-03. Used for: pay vouchers (amount, date and place claimed) to prove service and separate same-named men.
- **S7:** BYU Family History Library / Alyssa Martinez, [O Say, Can You See: Introducing U.S. Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRt2jTgEqVw), published 2024-06-12. Used for: comparing where units were recruited with census residence; the NARA vs state-archive custodian split.
- **S8:** BYU Family History Library / James Tanner, [Finding Your Ancestors and Relatives with Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cWym50l1PYU), published 2020-11-17. Used for: narrowing a common-name Civil War soldier by birth date, residence and locally raised units.

**Synthesis and limits:** The following are authored synthesis: the classification step, the per-series eligibility table, the evidence-kind labels, the roster-status rule and the candidate-separation table. The presenters supply the examples, the eligibility statements and the record descriptions, each attributed above. The instruction to check pension and state records after an empty or incomplete CMSR search is a routing rule handed to the pension recipe (§4). It is not a measured rate of CMSR omissions.
