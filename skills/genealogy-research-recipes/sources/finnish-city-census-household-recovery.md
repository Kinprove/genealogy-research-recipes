# Sources for Trace a household in Finland's historical city censuses

- **S1:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Kun helsinkiläisiä yritettiin laskea](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2024/10/kun-helsinkilaisia-yritettiin-laskea.html). Community post and its excerpts from the Helsinki account read 2026-11-22. The author directs readers to the contemporary report. The report supplies the published interpretation, enumeration observations and language.

- **S2:** [Statistical Office of Finland, Wäenlasku maaliskuussa 1870 Helsingin, Turun, Wiipurin ja Oulun kaupungeissa](https://www.doria.fi/bitstream/handle/10024/67272/vael3_1870.pdf). Contents and Helsinki enumeration account read 2026-11-22. Used for the announcements, suspected departures, evasion, supplementary count, self-reported livelihoods and former-status terms.

- **S3:** [Statistics Finland, Historiallisen tilastotiedon opas: Väestölaskennat](https://guides.stat.fi/historiallisentilastotiedonopas/vaestolaskennat). City census coverage table and unpublished materials section read 2026-11-22. Used for city and year coverage, published totals versus dwelling forms, and the archive's name and custodian.

- **S4:** [National Archives of Finland, Tilastollisen päätoimiston kaupunkien väestönlaskennat -arkisto (1870–1930)](https://www.finna.fi/Collection/narc.VAKKA-166293.KA). Finna fonds description and city subcollections read 2026-11-22. Establishes the catalogue hierarchy. It does not establish that a particular historic dwelling form is digitized or survives.

- **S5:** [National Archives of Finland, Ou:2 Kiinteistökortit ja niihin liittyvät huoneistokortit (1870)](https://www.finna.fi/Record/narc.VAKKA-166293.KA_VAKKA-1519193.KA). Finna unit description for Oulu in 1870 read 2026-11-22. Used as a catalogue locator for property and apartment cards. Images and their contents were outside the check.
