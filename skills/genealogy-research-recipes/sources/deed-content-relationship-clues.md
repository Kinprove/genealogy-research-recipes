- **S5:** BYU Family History Library / James Tanner, [Researching In-depth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_-q0vqAdMiw), 2015-03-18. Used for: a county-wide same-surname land sweep from about 20 years before birth to build a candidate pool, and the limit that land records seldom give children.

Used for: life estates reserved by deed, survivorship tenancy, warranty, special warranty and quitclaim definitions, the disclaimer deed in Arizona's community-property setting, the nine modern community-property states, and the remark that evidence of a life-estate transfer is usually required.
- **S8:** GeneaVlogger, [Colonial Jewish Records and Using DNA to Prove Jewish Ancestry](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z3VhHOdkM6I), 2022-05-13. Used for: "of Halifax" and "of the same place" left unresolved between residence and birthplace.
- **S9:** Denyse Allen, [What Deeds Tell You That the Census Can't](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rB2JmV3Gung), 2026-08-15. Used for: an AI-assisted parcel comparison by citation, dimensions and neighbours, a part-of relationship, a same-date same-grantor false lead, the Wilmer/Wimmer separation and a 13th/30th date discrepancy.
- **S10:** Robyn, Reclaiming Kin, [The Value of Court Records](https://reclaimingkin.com/the-value-of-court-records/), published 2024-10-24. Used for: a reported deed-of-trust purpose recital linking a pledge, a named surety and a court bond, including an expressly stated relationship. Role separation and the bond/case retrieval cross-check are procedural synthesis. The original images and archival locators were not independently inspected. The author's conclusive-parentage claim, general legal rules and current ordering or custody arrangements are not adopted; personal names and offense details are not reproduced.

The ordering into steps, the per-instrument clue register and the requirement to attach a corroboration need and a namesake alternative to every clue are authored synthesis. The sources supply the individual techniques and examples.

The following were not verified:

- any jurisdiction's historical community-property, dower or life-estate law;
- what a dower release contained in a given place and period;
- historical recording requirements and delays;
- the general warranty-deed definition, beyond S7's statement;
- the presenters' case outcomes;
- any going-rate data.

Motives stated by presenters (tax avoidance, probate avoidance) are not adopted.
