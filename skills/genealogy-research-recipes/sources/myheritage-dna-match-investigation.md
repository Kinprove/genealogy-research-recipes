## Sources

Sources are cited and paraphrased, not reproduced. Community tutorials supply practical working
patterns; vendor pages supply checkable platform behavior. Vendor sources checked 2026-10-03;
the additional Q&A was reviewed 2026-10-21.

- [Reviewing your DNA matches on MyHeritage](https://education.myheritage.com/article/reviewing-your-dna-matches-on-myheritage/) — match-review features and shared-match investigation.
- [Eight mistakes with MyHeritage DNA and how to fix them](https://education.myheritage.com/article/8-mistakes-you-might-be-making-with-myheritage-dna-and-how-to-fix-them/) — labels, notes and avoiding repeated investigation.
- [DNA results and filtering your DNA matches](https://education.myheritage.com/article/dna-results-and-filtering-your-dna-matches/) — targeted shortlist, relationship-category definitions and filter workflow.
- [How to use chromosome browsers for genealogy](https://education.myheritage.com/article/how-to-use-chromosome-browsers-for-genealogy/) — comparison selection and segment evidence.
- [The Theory of Family Relativity for DNA matches](https://education.myheritage.com/article/the-theory-of-family-relativity-for-dna-matches/) — theory paths, underlying links and review states.
- [How can I confirm or reject a theory?](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12879497-how-can-i-confirm-or-reject-a-theory-of-family-relativity) — recorded assessment and separate manual tree updates.
- [How do I use labels for DNA matches?](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12889244-how-do-i-use-the-labels-for-dna-matches-on-myheritage) — multiple labels, bulk edits and displayed dots.
- [Where can I see DNA matches on my family site?](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/13130410-where-can-i-see-dna-matches-on-my-family-site) — search clearing, notes and access states.
- [How do I assign a DNA kit to someone else?](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12851690-how-do-i-assign-a-dna-kit-to-someone-else) — kit assignment, assignee permission, permanent theory loss and Health-kit exception.
- [What are the advanced MyHeritage DNA features?](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12852419-what-are-the-advanced-myheritage-dna-features) — feature-access prerequisites.
- [How can I find triangulated DNA segments?](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12851488-how-can-i-find-triangulated-dna-segments) — indicator, hover count and automatic three-person loading.
- [Now that I have my DNA results, what can I do with them?](https://education.myheritage.com/article/now-that-i-have-my-dna-results-what-can-i-do-with-them/) — seven-match browser capacity and follow-up research.
- [Chromosome browser upgrade](https://blog.myheritage.com/2018/03/major-upgrade-to-the-myheritage-chromosome-browser-for-better-exploration-of-dna-matches/) — triangulation of the selected group.
- [Contacting DNA matches is free](https://blog.myheritage.com/2018/01/contacting-dna-matches-is-now-free/) — contact from DNA-match cards and kit managers.
- [Why can't I upload my DNA data file?](https://www.myheritage.com/help/en/articles/12852724-why-can-t-i-upload-my-dna-data-file) (page dated 2026-07-21, checked 2026-11-06) — current upload restriction ("DNA uploads are no longer supported"), relevant to excluding obsolete setup advice and to treating any uploaded kit as an earlier one; the page says nothing about kits uploaded before the change.
- [Using My Heritage DNA for Segment Analysis — James Baker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DzNZhaIdQJo) (BYU Family History Library, 2021-08-06) — sorting an unfiltered segment export, checking overlapping matches against an anchor's shared matches for the other parental side, and a segment lost to a working cM cutoff.
- [MyHeritage's New Whole Genome Sequence Test | 700k vs 3 billion DNA markers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=arP83dD_7a0) (GeneaVlogger, 2025-10-18) — vendor FAQ read aloud: no reprocessing of array kits, mixed processing during the transition, and processing type stated in the raw-file header.
- [My Heritage Cousin Finder](https://genarchivist.net/showthread.php?tid=1549) (GenArchivist Forum, first post 2025-03-31) — Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for treating Cousin Finder entries as tree assertions and reading a missing "DNA Match" marker as no results at MyHeritage, not as untested.
- MyHeritage, [Live DNA Q&A With the Experts!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Nsir-VBEhWg),
  published 2024-09-18 — the Q&A motivates preserving the basis of a question before
  first contact with a close match. Workflow 8's minimal private packet is Kinprove synthesis;
  the source's “screenshot everything” advice and causal account of withdrawal are not adopted.
  No actual withdrawn kit, original video or current hide/delete mechanism was verified.
- MyHeritage, [Ask The Expert - Exploring Your Family Tree Timeline on MyHeritage](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0TUlka43n2I),
  published 2022-04-26 — the recipe calls this a 2022 Q&A and uses its question about replacing
  the linked tree, which workflow 1's "Before replacing the whole linked tree" preflight addresses.
  The old/new kit-profile crosswalk and post-change link check are Kinprove synthesis. The recipe
  takes no guarantee from this source that replacing a tree preserves links or deleted theories.
- [Part Two: MyHeritage DNA Theories of Family Relativity](https://www.legacytree.com/blog/myheritage-dna-theories-of-family-relativity)
  (Legacy Tree Genealogists, 2023-06-02) — Rights: cited reference, not reproduced. Used for a
  proposed theory path not necessarily being the source of the shared DNA, and for shared matches
  and AutoClusters as checks. Extending the caution from a vendor-proposed path to a fully
  documented one, and the `unresolved` default, are Kinprove synthesis.
