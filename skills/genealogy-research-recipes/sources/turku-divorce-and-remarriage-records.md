# Sources: Recover Turku divorce and remarriage records

- **S1:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Sokean miehen kolme avioliitoa](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/11/sokean-miehen-kolme-avioliitoa.html). Published 2023-11-14. Community account and its 1758 royal-letter citation read 2026-11-22. The author transcribes the marriage-status wording and describes a distinct permission to remarry. The individual case is a retrieval example.

- **S2:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Maria Elisabet Langin elämästä](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/04/maria-elisabet-langin-elamasta.html). Published 2023-04-03. Community account and citations for two letters in 1762 read 2026-11-22. The author provisionally interprets the reply as a refusal. Use the two dates to retrieve the documents, and read the reply before assigning an outcome.

- **S3:** [National Archives of Finland, Turun tuomiokapituli](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/turun-tuomiokapituli). Archival guide sections on marriage matters and surviving series read 2026-11-22. Describes the divorce certificate and civil proceeding, and the series that survived the 1827 fire.

- **S4:** [National Archives of Finland, Turun arkkihiippakunnan tuomiokapitulin arkisto](https://finna.fi/Collection/narc.VAKKA-202511.KA). Collection context and royal-correspondence series read 2026-11-22. Catalogue evidence for the archival structure. Individual letters were not inspected.

- **S5:** [National Archives of Finland, Tuomiokapitulit](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/tuomiokapitulit). Archival guide section on divorce letters and court proceedings read 2026-11-22. Identifies both chapter and court records and explains that a secular decision often preceded the chapter proceeding.
