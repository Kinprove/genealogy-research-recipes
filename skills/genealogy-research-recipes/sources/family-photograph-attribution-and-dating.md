**Authored synthesis.** The following is not taken from any one source: the section order; the bound vocabulary (earliest or latest; exposure versus print; studio working period versus sitting; introduction versus abolition); the rule that a date bound derived from a naming cannot test that same naming; and the catalog-wide rule that the agent never compares faces or assigns identity by appearance. The sources supply the examples and failure modes. The facts in the identity-document photo bound and the 1943 shoulder-board rule were checked on 2026-11-02 against S28–S31. Also Kinprove synthesis: naming the object class first; reading the end of a process's range as "probably before"; the copy-print exception; and the rule that a postcard back, a stamp box or a paper mark dates the card or paper, not the exposure. The process, postcard, paper and copyright facts were checked on 2026-11-06 against S38–S51.
