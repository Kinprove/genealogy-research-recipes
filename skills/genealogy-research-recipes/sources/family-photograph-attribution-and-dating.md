**Authored synthesis.** The following is not taken from any one source: the section order; the bound vocabulary (earliest or latest; exposure versus print; studio working period versus sitting; introduction versus abolition); the rule that a date bound derived from a naming cannot test that same naming; and the catalog-wide rule that the agent never compares faces or assigns identity by appearance. The sources supply the examples and failure modes.

- the order numbers, dates and unit attributions cited in [S21]
- the studio periods, directory entries and range disagreements in [S17], [S19] and [S20]
- the format date ranges the presenters give in [S13], [S15] and [S22]; the recipe gives no format date table
- the current behaviour of any image-search, photo-sharing or tree platform
- the identifications in the presenters' and forum examples
