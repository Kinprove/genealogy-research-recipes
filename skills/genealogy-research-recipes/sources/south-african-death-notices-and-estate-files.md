# Recover a South African death notice inside an estate file — sources

- **S1:** [How to Explore South Africa Genealogy Records Online](https://blog.myheritage.com/2025/01/how-to-explore-south-africa-genealogy-records-online/). Published 2025-01-14. Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Community guide on the MyHeritage blog. Used for the suggestion to look for probate and estate material in multiple repositories. The published collection pages and archival handout establish the narrower retrieval routes.
- **S2:** [FamilySearch, South Africa: National Archives](https://cms-b-assets.familysearch.org/1e/ae/c891fb74ba60f24290966019d393/south-africa-national-archives-handout.pdf). PDF. Read 2026-11-22. Daniel R. Jones’s 2014 handout, used for the archival reference fields and depot-based sourcing. The old interface and ordering instructions do not establish current access.
- **S3:** [FamilySearch, Cape Province, South Africa, Probate Records, 1834–1989](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/2517051). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Collection page and coverage description. Used for packet numbering, index fields and content; individual packet images were not inspected.
- **S4:** [FamilySearch, South Africa, Orange Free State, Estate Files, 1951–2006](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/1407787). Catalogue. Read 2026-11-22. Collection page and coverage description. Used for the Bloemfontein records, the image arrangement and the death notice contents; individual packet images were not inspected.
- **S5:** [A Guide to Tracing Your Family in South Africa: Finding Your Roots](https://blog.myheritage.com/2025/01/a-guide-to-tracing-your-family-in-south-africa-finding-your-roots-2/). Published 2025-01-20. Web page. Read 2026-11-22. Community guide on the MyHeritage blog. Used for the suggestion to use the national archives for estate research. Its broader civil, immigration and military coverage claims are outside this procedure.
