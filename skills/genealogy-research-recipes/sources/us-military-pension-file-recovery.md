## Sources

Third-party sources are cited references, paraphrased rather than reproduced.
The linked routing, request-receipt and image/timeline schemas are original synthesis.

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library, Julia A. Anderson,
  [U.S. Military Pension Files (1776–1916): A Gold Mine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OcAeLJxT9XU),
  15 July 2024. Service clues, index versus file, requests and chronological reading.
- **S2:** BlackProGen LIVE!,
  [Episode 65: Civil War Pensions and the Grand Army of the Republic](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nSfAqzYaiWM),
  15 August 2018. Application/certificate locators, unsuccessful applications and reading the
  continuing claim. Historical transfer assertions are not adopted as current custody rules.
- **S3:** Ancestry, Loretta Evans,
  [U.S. Military Pension Files](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RRzzQVNQU2I),
  20 April 2016. Separate service, pension and bounty-land records; unsuccessful applications.
- **S4:** BYU Family History Library, Marilyn Thomsen,
  [State Military Record Resources](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8i254jbCQDQ),
  3 September 2024; Julie Wilcox,
  [Discover Family History Through Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HG6sY_S-Neg),
  14 September 2023; James Tanner,
  [Looking for Your Ancestors' Family Bibles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x2ERq3jWhdI),
  20 September 2023. Used for searching collateral relatives' pension files for Bible pages and
  for affidavits that state relationships.
- **S5:** BYU Family History Library, Rachel Derenthal,
  [Wrong Until Proven Right: Think Like a Genealogist](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cLMzNfMNHic),
  21 August 2025; GeneaVlogger,
  [Watching Reactions to my Videos](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PIFPwcrZ7Ac),
  22 March 2024. Used for checking pensions for other conflicts a person lived through.
- **S6:** BYU Family History Library, Laura Lefler,
  [How to Learn About My Civil War Ancestors Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tD9lgibwBRE),
  19 November 2024; GeneaVlogger,
  [From Guerrilla Fighter to U.S. Citizen](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jXxHTUPOy6Y),
  16 December 2025; with S1. Used for routing Confederate pension searches to the state of
  residence at application.
- **S7:** Family History Fanatics,
  [Creating the Foundation: Initial Genealogy Research Using Spreadsheets - Civil War Part 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P9g7JwVmnIM),
  21 October 2024; [Diving into the Data - Civil War Part 3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cpulm6BuAJ8),
  28 October 2024;
  [What I Learned Researching 1,000 Civil War Soldiers - Civil War Genealogy Pt 10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8h90dEdVBYc),
  22 February 2025. Differing pension-index versions; unit-arranged browsing versus error-prone
  field search; pension applicants absent from a roster; widow on card not always the wartime wife.
- **S8:** Family History Fanatics,
  [EASILY Research Civil War Pension Files Using @findmypast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wo-M7iIWj78),
  5 April 2021. The presenter interprets the illustrated series' state field as filing residence; verify the series' field definition before applying that interpretation.
- **S9:** BYU Family History Library, Alyssa Martinez,
  [O Say, Can You See: Introducing U.S. Military Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRt2jTgEqVw),
  12 June 2024. A veteran's and widow's papers tended to be combined under the widow's certificate
  number.
- **S10:** BYU Family History Library, Marilyn Thomsen,
  [Military Research: What and When](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C2_9AVcodcg),
  20 August 2024, with S4. A Georgia pension-roll entry as a pointer; a remarried widow's later
  applications; reading claims against the rules at each filing; drop reports as death-date
  sources.
- **S11:** Lisa Lisson,
  [Finding An Ancestor's Date of Death](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bc92E8Oq4zU),
  13 March 2020. A successor widow's application as evidence of a previous wife's death;
  payment-stop notations.
- **S12:** BYU Family History Library, Michael Strauss,
  [The Road to Independence: Revolutionary War Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nJsQ9ftU9iM),
  8 February 2025. Treasury final pension payment cards and ledgers as a separate series that can
  give dates of death.
- **S13:** Genealogy Gems, Michael Strauss segments,
  [Episode 213](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M72-nL_Yb2I), 10 January 2018, and
  [Episode 209](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CPSlx8VfKps), 11 October 2017. A pension file may
  leave out regular-army or earlier Confederate service. S7 also covers pension applicants absent
  from the roster.
- **H1:** National Archives,
  [Requesting Copies of Older Military Service Records](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records/pre-ww-1-records),
  page reviewed 16 January 2026; checked 7 October 2026. Request forms, federal scope and
  complete-file versus Pension Documents Packet products.
- **H2:** National Archives,
  [Genealogy Research in Military Records](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/genealogy),
  checked 7 October 2026. Record-type, service category and custodian routing.
