## Sources

Third-party sources are cited references, paraphrased rather than reproduced.
The linked routing, request-receipt and image/timeline schemas are original synthesis.

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library, Julia A. Anderson,
  [U.S. Military Pension Files (1776–1916): A Gold Mine](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OcAeLJxT9XU),
  15 July 2024. Service clues, index versus file, requests and chronological reading.
- **S2:** BlackProGen LIVE!,

  continuing claim. Historical transfer assertions are not adopted as current custody rules.

  20 April 2016. Separate service, pension and bounty-land records; unsuccessful applications.

  [Looking for Your Ancestors' Family Bibles](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x2ERq3jWhdI),
