**What is authored synthesis and what is not verified.** The date-to-register routing, the entry classification, the gap ledger and the event-register output are authored synthesis. The presentations supply the examples and some period claims, each attributed to its presenter where it has not been confirmed. The calendar, census-image, executry and valuation-roll facts rest on NRS, ScotlandsPeople, statute and scholarly sources (S12–S22); the death-at-sea facts rest on S24–S26; the image limits, charges, fee consultation and death-index field rest on S27–S33; the rules built on them are authored synthesis.

The following were not verified:

- the meaning of decimal-suffix parish numbers;
- the Free Church proportion and geography at the Disruption;
- the 1819 and 1853–55 batch entries and post-1855 quoad sacra delayed entries;
- the current ScotlandsPeople charge for RCE pages.

The Covenanter and Cromwellian-era gaps and "clandestine" marriage entries are not covered by the cited presentations and are not asserted here. Irregular marriages are a known reason a marriage is missing from both the OPR and the statutory registers; research them separately.
