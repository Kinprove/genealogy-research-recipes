- **S15:** Jonny Perl, [Shared DNA at a glance: the new DNA matrix tool](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/shared-dna-at-a-glance-the-new-dna-matrix-tool/), 2025-05-21. Tester-pair amounts and separately entered relationships; current import formats and entitlements unverified. Read 2026-11-12 for the plain CSV export (testers and shared amounts), the JSON export and manual entry of relationships for pasted GEDmatch and FamilyTreeDNA data.
- **S16:** Jonny Perl, [Recent improvements to the matrix tool](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/matrix-tool-improvements/), 2025-06-25. Descriptive views, an input-error example, symmetric pairs and historical edit/save distinctions.
- **S17:** Jonny Perl, [New in the matrix tool: enhanced charts, sorting, and more…](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/new-in-the-matrix-tool-enhanced-charts-sorting-and-more/), 2025-08-28. Per-tester display modes and a sibling clone that copies relationships while leaving cM blank; numerical examples do not supply acceptance thresholds.
- **S18:** Jonny Perl, [My first experiments with AncestryDNA Custom Clusters](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/my-initial-experiments-with-ancestrydna-custom-clusters/), 2025-11-26. The stated copy operation transfers names without shared cM; narrated ancestral conclusions and cluster thresholds are not adopted.
- **S19:** Jonny Perl, [New: Showcase your roots with Y & mtDNA in your chromosome map](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/new-showcase-your-roots-with-y-mtdna-in-your-chromosome-map/), 2024-08-30. Separate map fields for a haplogroup, kit link and lineage ancestor; entering an ancestor does not require a reported Y/mt test result.
- **S20:** Jonny Perl, [Bringing Y, mt and mitoYDNA into DNA Painter trees](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/bringing-y-mt-and-mitoydna-into-dna-painter-trees/), 2020-03-18. Tree haplogroup annotations, propagation and kit links. The propagation-rule list was not available when consulted; no rule or current field contract is reconstructed.
- **S21:** Jonny Perl, [DNA Painter Dimensions: a new way to showcase your ancestral line](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/dna-painter-dimensions-a-new-way-to-showcase-your-ancestral-line/), 2021-04-13. Custom/generated categories and manual correction of inferred values. Automatic category lists and sharing examples are missing; ancestral propagation of religion or other personal facts is not adopted.
- **S22:** Jonny Perl, [Introducing Places, a new geographical map of your Ancestral Tree](https://blog.dnapainter.com/blog/introducing-places-new-map-ancestral-tree/), 2025-03-01. Birthplace-text geocoding, displayed scope and correction routes; original-text/correction logging is synthesis. Missing options, current interface behavior and historical defaults remain unverified; a plotted pedigree is not a genetic journey.
- **H1–H4:** Current public [Common Segment Generator](https://dnapainter.com/tools/csg), [Inferred Segments Generator](https://dnapainter.com/tools/isg), [Distinct Segment Generator](https://dnapainter.com/tools/dsg) and [Coverage Estimator](https://dnapainter.com/tools/coverage) pages, accessed 2026-10-04.

- **S23:** Blaine Bettinger, [Enhancements to the Shared cM Project at DNAPainter.com](https://thegeneticgenealogist.com/2022/08/27/enhancements-to-the-shared-cm-project-at-dnapainter-com/), 2022-08-27. Relationship-specific histogram bins and approximate cumulative percentages; these are not probabilities of competing relationships. Read 2026-11-12 for bins labelled by their upper limit (137 cM in the bin marked 140, covering 131–140 cM).
- **S24:** Blaine Bettinger, [Leveraging the Power of Siblings and Cousins to Narrow Relationship Possibilities](https://thegeneticgenealogist.com/2022/11/12/leveraging-the-power-of-siblings-and-cousins-to-narrow-relationship-possibilities/), 2022-11-12. Two eligible measured amounts, same-relationship and additional-path conditions, and the first-input percentage display. Missing requirements images and current interface behavior are not reconstructed. Input provenance, retaining all amounts and not treating unavailable comparisons as zero extend this recipe's existing controls; pre-TIMBER preference and cross-company equivalence are not adopted.
- **S25:** Legacy Tree Genealogists, [5 Ways to Use the DNA Coverage Estimator](https://www.legacytree.com/blog/genealogy-tools-dna-painter-coverage), 2022-10-17. Active coverage from accessible tests, next-tester output read as a collaboration or transfer priority, division of a unique-segment union by coverage, and the floor's single-relationship condition. Its ranking of that quotient above the individual pairwise amounts is not adopted. Computing the percentage for the same testers, the modeled-estimate label and the error-magnification note are synthesis; which databases currently accept uploads is not verified.
- **S26:** Legacy Tree Genealogists, [Using DNA For Genealogy Research: tools for evaluating total shared centimorgans](https://www.legacytree.com/blog/using-dna-genealogy-tools-centimorgans), 2024-05-21. AncestryDNA-derived, Timber-weighted bases of the Shared cM tool's probabilities, and group probabilities for genetically equivalent relationships contrasted with per-relationship Shared cM Project data. Which dataset a current tool version uses is not established; the cross-company mismatch note is synthesis.
- **H5:** Current public [Shared cM Project tool](https://dnapainter.com/tools/sharedcmv4) page, accessed 2026-10-23. Its probability heading credits "stats from The DNA Geek". Rechecked 2026-11-12: the range is shown as low to high at the 99th percentile, and for relationships more distant than half second cousin the averages count only pairs that shared DNA.
- **S27:** Jonny Perl, [Can AncestryDNA's new origins segments help identify ancestral lines?](https://dnapainter.com/blog/can-ancestrydnas-new-origins-segments-help-identify-ancestral-lines/), 2026-10-30. Bookmarklet and ACPS routes to Ancestry population-segment coordinates, batch cM sizes, and reading painted regions as clues against mapped matches and a parent's own report. The post's browser list and grandparent-split feature are not adopted.
- **S28:** Jonny Perl, [New: AncestryDNA Chromosome Painter Segments](https://dnapainter.com/blog/new-ancestrydna-chromosome-painter-segments/), 2022-07-27. ACPS estimates segment positions from the page data and build 37 chromosome lengths.
