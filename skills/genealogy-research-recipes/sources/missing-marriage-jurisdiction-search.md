## Sources

Based on recorded talks (reviewed 2026-10-20). No statute, Census Bureau report, map, bond or register was inspected.

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library / James Tanner, [What Makes Vital Records So Vital](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBUxLGm1_uY), 2017-08-24. Gretna Green concept; the bride's location having more influence on where a marriage happened; Nevada's laxer residency and blood-test rules and Mexico as period destinations.
- **S4:** BYU Family History Library / James Tanner, [Using First Level Genealogical Sources](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KTHR-RiI3c4), 2016-10-20. Lists of Gretna Green places in the FamilySearch Research Wiki; examples in New England and the South.
- **S5:** BYU Family History Library / Laura Lefler, [Southern Research Part II: Proving Relationships with Vital Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PXKxUZZFbnw), 2023-01-23. Couples usually marrying where the bride lived; Aiken, South Carolina marriage explained by military training at Camp Gordon.
- **S6:** Genealogy Gems / J. Mark Lowe, [Marriage Records Case Study with J. Mark Lowe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FZiyunMO5IA), 2022-03-31; also reissued as [Episode 277](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2GL2yiNDBvE), 2023-06-16. Warren County, Kentucky search across adjacent and rail-line counties using a 1924 map; Mitchellville, Sumner County bond; bondsman who was also the officiant; recurring family destinations.
- **S7:** Genealogy Gems, [Gretna Green and Marriage Records](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P6NmNtTOdOw), 2022-03-24. Census Bureau marriage-and-divorce reports; 1909 Kentucky and Tennessee consent ages; 1922 Brooke County table; Cornell LII as a law summary.
- **S8:** Genealogy Gems, [Episode 274 – Marriage Records and Gretna Green](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SciDaHPME1c), 2023-03-12. Audio reissue of the same report walkthrough.
- **S9:** Lisa Lisson, [Why You Can't Find an Ancestor's Marriage Record](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aOqT1i33QIE), 2022-02-04. Courthouse proximity and spring floods; justice of the peace who never returned his register; topographic maps; circuit-rider journals via ArchiveGrid and PERSI.
- **S10:** Lisa Lisson, [Why You Could Be Missing Your Ancestor's MARRIAGE Record](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bBhVuIw2dw4), 2022-08-23. Named marriage-mill destinations; FamilySearch Wiki article; no guarantee of a hit; possibility the couple never married.
- **S11:** Lisa Lisson, [Why You Are Not Finding Your Ancestor's Marriage Record](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CgcnOlNO9bA), 2020-06-09. Surry County, North Carolina river and seasonal-wedding reasoning; crossing into Virginia; FamilySearch Wiki chart; church records and newspapers after county loss.
- **S12:** Denyse Allen, [3 Key PA Vital Records and Where to Find Them](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fA8OLwUUaLU), 2024-07-25. Three-county Pennsylvania example; date window; index versus original.
- **S13:** Denyse Allen, [3 Key Pennsylvania Vital Records and How to Get Them](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jzm44GVzN_s), 2022-06-30. Earlier version of S12.
- **S14:** Lisa Lisson, [Answering Reader's Common Questions!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jYj1VD1naSA), 2023-02-10. Marriage found in a sister's home county; wartime haste; FamilySearch Wiki charts.
- **S15:** BYU Family History Library / Laurie Castillo, [Jurisdiction = When + Where](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S_X6RH7iugM), 2017-03-03. Conway/Perry County, Arkansas river example; local Gretna Greens; travel to a preferred church; jurisdiction tied to date as well as place.
- **S16:** Lisa Lisson, [LIVE Genealogy Chat – Answering Readers' Questions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2vfiVf3pnEo), 2021-05-07. Searching the FamilySearch Wiki for Gretna Greens; adding a date range to web searches for local Gretna Greens because destinations changed.
