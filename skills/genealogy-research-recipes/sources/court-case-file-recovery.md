Utah describes court retention followed generally by transfer after fifty years; Maryland explicitly says holdings vary with each county's transfers. Recent clerk/prothonotary and transferred federal-file routes are authored synthesis with the linked federal-court recipe; no particular file's custody was established.

The following are authored synthesis: the pointer-to-file crosswalk, the stage inventory with explicit gaps, the bounded-negative logging rule and the case register. The sources supply the court-procedure explanations, the retrieval tips and the examples.

### Chancery and case-law search sources

- **S16:** Roberta Estes, DNAeXplained, [Barney Campbell's Descendants in the 1872 Chancery Court Suit – 52 Ancestors #414](https://dna-explained.com/2023/10/22/barney-campbells-descendants-in-the-1872-chancery-court-suit-52-ancestors-414/), published 2023-10-22, read 2026-11-12. Used for: chancery bills as statements of what is alleged; chancery as a court separate from circuit and criminal courts in most places, generally serving a region; the Claiborne County, Tennessee chancery notes absent from the regular county court notes, with the chancery bills, pleadings and minutes recorded at Tazewell.
- **S17:** County Technical Assistance Service, University of Tennessee, [Hardeman County court system historical notes](https://www.ctas.tennessee.edu/private-acts/court-system-historical-notes-34), read 2026-11-12. Used for: the 1837–38 act making Fayette, Shelby, Hardeman and McNairy counties one chancery district meeting at Somerville, and the 1869–70 act dividing Tennessee into twelve chancery districts.
- **S18:** Library of Virginia, [Chancery Records Index Project Information](https://www.lva.virginia.gov/collections/cri/info), read 2026-11-12. Used for: the courts that heard Virginia chancery causes and their dates, including county courts until 1870; the bill of complaint, the defendant's answer and the decrees as the judge's decisions.
- **S19:** Free Law Project, [Advanced Search and Query Techniques](https://wiki.free.law/c/courtlistener/help/search/advanced-search-and-query-techniques), read 2026-11-12. Used for: CourtListener's proximity search (a quoted phrase followed by a tilde and a word distance) and fuzzy search (a tilde after a single term, values 1 or 2). The page does not document a parenthesised group inside a proximity search.
- **S20:** Dana Leeds, [The Horner Estate Dispute: A Family Feud, Fraudulent Deeds, and a Quest for Justice](https://www.danaleeds.com/the-horner-estate-dispute/), published 2024-05-19, read 2026-11-12. Used for: a CourtListener proximity search pairing a Perry County, Tennessee surname with its county and state that returned the Missouri case Jackson v. Grissom, 196 Mo. 624 (1906). The post's parenthesised query form is not the documented syntax (S19).
