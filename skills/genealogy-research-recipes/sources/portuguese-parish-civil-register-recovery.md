**What was not verified:**

- the text of Article 15 of Decree-Law 324/2007 itself. The transfer thresholds come from tombo.pt, and the FamilySearch Wiki cites the same page, so they are one source.
- whether Civil Online or a civil registry office asks for proof of relationship in practice
- the Madeira index years, which come from a 2022 handout; the archive's own site was not opened
- the Azores portal's current address (the Legacy Tree link returned an access error)
- which fonds holds the marriage-process series in each diocese
- whether the second set of Adaúfe books at Viana do Castelo is a duplicate copy
- that the Elvas municipal civil books are the 1878 non-Catholic registers
- which Elvas books are at the Elvas municipal archive rather than at Portalegre (S6 and S20–S21 disagree on the diocese's books)
- the exact day the 1911 Code took effect (an archive guide is reported to give 1 April 1911; it was not readable here), which is why the recipe asks for both registers in 1911
- the red condition rating and its effect, which come only from a FamilySearch video
- the presenters' accounts of surname switching, baptism dates in emigrant records and U.S. lay societies
- national census survival, beyond the FamilySearch Wiki's "believed to have been destroyed"
