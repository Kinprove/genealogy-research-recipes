Used for: Savoy's return to Sardinian law in 1816 and union with France in 1860; mayors resuming civil registration on 1 January 1861; for 1816–1860, Catholic registers with duplicates or copies sent to the lay courts and bishoprics, and extracts, then certified copies, of the 1828–1860 acts handed to the mairies after 1861; parish registers handed to the priests under the Sardinian decree of 22 December 1815 not returning to the mairies; a very large part of the Chambéry diocese's registers deposited in the Savoie departmental archives in 1972.
- **S58:** Archives de Paris, [État civil de Paris](https://archives.paris.fr/archives-numerisees/etat-civil-de-paris), read 2026-11-13. Used for: the 12th arrondissement's birth acts of 1 January 1870 to 25 May 1871 destroyed, to be sought among the reconstructed acts.
- **S59:** Archives de Paris, [Recensements de la population](https://archives.paris.fr/recherches/presentation-des-fonds/sources-genealogiques/recensements-de-la-population), read 2026-11-13. Used for: nominative lists in Paris only for 1926, 1931, 1936 and 1946; their arrangement by arrondissement, quartier and street, with streets not always alphabetical; birth date and place and occupation for each family member; the lists online except those of 1946, seen only in the reading room; census records of 1856–1872 for the 5th and 10th arrondissements among the arrondissement mairies' records.
- **S60:** Archives départementales de Maine-et-Loire, [Les archives des notaires](https://archives.maine-et-loire.fr/fileadmin/rechercher_consulter/conseils_recherche/FICHE_LES_ARCHIVES_DES_NOTAIRES_2021.pdf) (research aid, PDF; text layer read), read 2026-11-13. Used for: finding a place's notaries and their répertoires when the notary is unknown; the contrôle des actes from 1693 (series C) replaced in December 1790 by the enregistrement, whose registers list the acts of the notaries of each office's area in date order (sub-series 3 Q); the administration's tables of sellers, buyers, marriage contracts and wills; an example entry giving the parties, the act date, the registration date and the notary; and tables that give only the registration date.
- **S61:** Neogenea, [coverage page](https://neogenea.fr/couverture.html), read 2026-11-13. Used for: the départements and recruitment bureaux indexed, with the classes covered in each (81 départements and classes 1878–1926 overall, class 1926 only for the Jura, many départements only 1887–1921), and 5,778,129 of 14,213,648 register images reread by hand.
