**What is synthesis.** These parts were written for this recipe: the order of steps, the warning against linking by patronymic, the rule for labelling gaps, and the packet layout. S6 to S19 supply the archive, custody and coverage facts for the poll-tax, probate, emigration and Finnish sections; S26 and S27 supply the census-database coverage and the Stockholm roteman register.
