# Sources for Trace a historical Helsinki household across parish, tax and address records

- **S1:** National Archives of Finland, [Seurakuntien mikrokorttiluettelo](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/seurakuntien-mikrokorttiluettelo). Helsinki parish entries read 2026-11-22. Used for The Swedish-Finnish parish’s early volume gap and separate runs of communion books.

- **S2:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Vielä Burtzin perijöistä](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/02/viela-burtzin-perijoista.html). Case and register locators read 2026-11-22. Used for The early communion-book gap, a married daughter found under her later designation, recurring poll-tax adjacency and a marriage place in the communion book. The study gives KA 8448:14, KA 8452:7, KA 8463:7, KA 8485:6 and KA 8503:7 for the roll comparisons, and pp. 20, 229 and 435 for the communion-book leads.

- **S3:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Ananaksia Helsingissä kasvattanut mies](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2025/08/ananaksia-helsingissa-kasvattanut-mies.html). Parish citations at the end of the post read 2026-11-22. Used for Separate volumes for married and unmarried workers.

- **S4:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Muotiompelija Regina Bandly](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/10/muotiompelija-regina-bandly.html). Case and archive pointers read 2026-11-22. Used for A municipal permission and Aminoff entries found through the spouse’s name. The author’s 1848 HisKi cutoff remains unverified.

- **S5:** City of Helsinki, [Helsingin kirkkojen historiaa](https://historia.hel.fi/fi/ilmiot/aatteet-ja-katsomukset/helsingin-kirkkojen-historiaa). Section on Helsinki Lutheran parishes read 2026-11-22. Used for Formation of the separate German Lutheran congregation in 1858.

- **S6:** Genealogical Society of Finland, [Helsingin saksalainen seurakunta](https://seurakunnat.genealogia.fi/post/helsingin-saksalainen-seurakunta). Parish founding and archive description read 2026-11-22. Used for The beginning of the congregation’s historical registers in 1858. The asserted HisKi indexing cutoff was not verified.

- **S7:** University of Helsinki repository, [Sotilaita ja siviilejä – Viaporin väestö vuonna 1806](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/1346db40-6181-49cf-a309-7e56c3795da2/content). Comparison of 1806 population sources read 2026-11-22. Used for The differing Helsinki–Viapori coverage of parish and poll-tax records.

- **S8:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Tykistöläiset puutarhatyöntekijöinä](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2026/02/tykistolaiset-puutarhatyontekijoina.html). Case and record references read 2026-11-22. Used for Children’s registers as workplace leads, military details in baptism entries, and a line-level occupational label that differs from its page grouping. The study compares the 1789–1804 children’s register, the Viapori artillery-parish baptism, dated Helsinki baptisms and burials, the 1806 roll and named communion-book pages.

- **S9:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Problemaattinen Edvard Schoultz](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2025/09/problemaattinen-edvard-schoultz.html). Case and seamen’s house citations read 2026-11-22. Used for Alphabetical sailor rolls, a possible burgher admission year and a roll entry later than the reported death. The post also cites Helsinki’s unmarried workers’ communion book I Aa:16, 1844–1860, pp. 141 and 145, and Rippikirjat I, 1856–1869, p. 982, for communion marks and occupations.

- **S10:** National Archives of Finland, [Henkikirjat](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/henkikirjat). Sections on series and arrangement read 2026-11-22. Used for County accounts through 1808 and provincial series in Astia for later years.

- **S11:** Helsinki City Archives, [Henkilö- ja sukututkimusaineistot Helsingin kaupunginarkistossa](https://kaupunginarkisto.hel.fi/aineistoesittelyja-fi/henkilo-ja-sukututkimusaineistot/). Sections on population and estate records read 2026-11-22. Henkikirjat section on arrangement and geographic scope read 2026-11-22. Supports the municipal roll lookup by district, block and lot from 1844, the headings and recorded roles, and the villas and islands section.

- **S12:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Venäläiset 1800-luvun henkikirjoissa, esimerkkinä Matrosoff](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/08/venalaiset-1800-luvun-henkikirjoissa.html). Case and archive references read 2026-11-22. Used for The magistrate index as a route to trading records, and a person named indirectly on a servant’s tax-roll line.

- **S13:** Helsinki City Archives, [Helsingin poliisilaitoksen osoitetoimiston osoiterekisteri](https://kaupunginarkisto.hel.fi/aineistoesittelyja-fi/osoiterekisteri/). Sections on contents and use read 2026-11-22. Used for Phonetic filing, cross-checking spouses, moves, and the limits of the reporting system.

- **S14:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Maria Andersson, helsinkiläinen vanhus](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2026/11/maria-andersson-helsinkilainen-vanhus.html). Historical address-card comparison read 2026-11-22. Used for The annexation boundary, card versus poll-tax mismatch, temporary addresses, a pasted clipping and the host surname as a locator. The post’s shared album was not used. The post supplies the example card and roll locators.

- **S15:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Romukauppias Lindström](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2026/09/romukauppias-lindstrom.html). Published 2026-09-13. Reviewed read web-page. Community post and cited roll references read 2026-11-22. The author describes using the property card for a buyer’s initials and recorded title, then checking the 1866 roll at the same address for the buyer and competing surname matches.

- **S16:** National Archives of Finland, [Helsingin kaupungin kiinteistökortisto](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/helsingin-kaupungin-kiinteistokortisto). Reviewed read help-page. Property-card guide and Sinetti instructions read 2026-11-22. Supports the catalogue search, district, block and lot structure, address and transaction fields, and the separate municipal poll-tax records for inhabitants.

- **S17:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Kansallismuseon naapurustosta (4/7): Leipuri ja kauppias](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2025/06/kansallismuseon-naapurustosta-47.html). Published 2025-06-15. Reviewed read web-page. Community post and source references read 2026-11-22. The author compares Fjälldal’s villa names and roles in newspapers and tax rolls (U82:1913 and U105:1121–1135), and the bankruptcy advertisement in Finland of 1 October 1891 against directory listings. The author also checked Andstén’s probate inventory (HRA 11488).

- **S18:** Kaisa Kyläkoski, [Taulukoinnissa hukatut tiedot](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2026/01/taulukoinnissa-hukatut-tiedot.html). Published 2026-01-12. Reviewed read web-page. Community post read in the blog’s weekly archive 2026-11-22. Reports the declaration sequence in KA 8529 and examples of information omitted from the roll. Pages 151, 158, 178, 237 and 252 are the post’s example locators. The workflow uses the sequence as a lead to be tested against each document.

- **S19:** National Archives of Finland, [Läänintilit](https://portti.kansallisarkisto.fi/arkistojen-portti/laanintilit). Reviewed read help-page. County accounts guide read 2026-11-22. Supports routing Swedish-period poll-tax rolls and supporting tax schedules to the county accounts.

- **S20:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Henkikirjojen ajoitus eli tarkista, äläkä arvaa](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2023/11/henkikirjojen-ajoitus-eli-tarkista.html). Published 2023-11-15. Reviewed read web-page. Community article and Outi Koste’s dated comment read 2026-11-22. Kyläkoski suggests checking closing dates and notices. Koste reports a different block-reporting and volume-closing date for 1884.

- **S21:** [Kaisa Kyläkoski, Kuinka tarkasti muuton voi ajoittaa?](https://sukututkijanloppuvuosi.blogspot.com/2024/01/kuinka-tarkasti-muuton-voi-ajoittaa.html). Published 2024-01-26.
