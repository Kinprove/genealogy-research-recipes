# Sources for italian-second-world-war-captivity-records

- **S1:** [Costantino Lacasella — prigioniero in Sudafrica e India](https://www.genealogiafamiliare.it/wp/2026/08/04/costantino-lacasella-prigioniero-in-sudafrica-e-india/), web-page, read. Post read 2026-11-22. Genealogia Familiare compares an Inter Arma Caritas card and a Commonwealth custody file. The records share a prisoner number. The latter supplies an embarkation after the transfer recorded on the card.

- **S2:** [Erminio Renzo Rossi — prigioniero italiano in Australia](https://www.genealogiafamiliare.it/wp/?p=1467), web-page, read. Post read 2026-11-22. The Rossi account reproduces Australian and Vatican documents, including a correction of destination, another card, the Report on Prisoner of War and a Hay camp property declaration.

- **S3:** [Ricerca di prigionieri e internati italiani della Seconda Guerra Mondiale](https://www.genealogiafamiliare.it/wp/ricerca-di-prigionieri-e-internati-italiani-della-seconda-guerra-mondiale/), web-page, read. Post read 2026-11-22. The author describes using the Vatican index and Arolsen records and links a South Africa case to its Commonwealth file.

- **S4:** [Vatican Apostolic Archive, Vatican Information Office for Prisoners of War, 1939–1947](https://www.archivioapostolicovaticano.va/content/aav/it/attivita/ricerca-e-conservazione/progetti/inventariazione/ufficio-informazioni-vaticano--prigionieri-di-guerra--1939-1947-.html), catalogue, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Describes the archive and card index recording inquiries and outcomes.

- **S5:** [Australian War Memorial, Inter arma caritas](https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/LIB100005382), catalogue, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Catalogues the DVD reproduction of the wartime card index.

- **S6:** [National Archives of Australia, Chinese–Australian Journeys](https://www.naa.gov.au/sites/default/files/2023-06/research-guide-chinese-australian-journeys_0.pdf), pdf, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. The wartime internment series entries describe MP1103/1 and MP1103/2 and identify the transfer and repatriation detail available in the former.

- **S7:** [Arolsen Archives, Inquiry](https://arolsen-archives.org/en/archive/inquiry/), help-page, read. Relevant material read 2026-11-22. Explains document scope for Nazi-era imprisonment, including Italian military internees.
