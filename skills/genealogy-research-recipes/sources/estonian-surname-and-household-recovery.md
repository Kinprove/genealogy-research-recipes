- **S2:** [Fred Puss, Kockeste’st Koiksoniks](https://keeljakirjandus.ee/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/F.PUSS_.pdf). The author compares historical records and distinguishes proposed name origins from documented family continuity. The article is a locator for cited register examples.

The archive explains parallel bynames, registrations and the distinction between parish and manor evidence.
