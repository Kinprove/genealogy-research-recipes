**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The order of steps, the output tables, the coverage-gap rule, the treatment of an age mismatch as a ranking signal, and the requirement to log the reference date of every age are authored synthesis. Most of the general statements come from one participant's reference article (S6), so agreement with it in other threads is not independent confirmation. Several threads share the same contributors.
