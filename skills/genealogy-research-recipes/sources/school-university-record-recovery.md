**What is authored and what the sources supply.** Some parts are authored synthesis:

- the layer order;
- the custody register;
- the role classification as a separate step;
- the rule that a mismatch ranks a candidate lower without excluding them;
- the rule that an absence applies only to the list and years searched.

Forum participants supplied the fond numbers, case contents, start years, coverage limits and archive replies. A single participant often supplied a given detail, and several threads share regular contributors. Agreement between threads is therefore not independent confirmation. Some points remain open:

- The ЦИАМ 459.14.61 signature was the indexer's guess.
- Fees, email routes and website addresses for the Kyiv, Odessa, Tartu and Tomsk archives may have changed.
