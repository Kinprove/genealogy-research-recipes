# Sources for Distinguish Estonian farm bynames from inherited family names

- **S1:** Fred Puss, Institute of the Estonian Language, [Keeleminutid. Eestis leidub poole tuhande aasta vanuseid perekonnanimesid](https://eki.ee/uudis/keeleminutid-eestis-leidub-poole-tuhande-aasta-vanuseid-perekonnanimesid/). Byname, mobility and surname discussion read 2026-11-22. Used for the historical distinction between the terms and the comparison of names across moves and surname assignment.

- **S2:** National Archives of Estonia, [VAU – Genealogy](https://www.ra.ee/vau/index.php/en/page/article/index?menuId=16). Name usage and assignment sections read 2026-11-22. Used for examples of multiple naming elements and the Estonia and Livonia series. Individual family entries were not inspected.

- **S3:** Fred Puss, [16th-century bynames in Estonian family names](https://www.keeljakirjandus.ee/en/archives/38102). Article abstract and archival references read 2026-11-22. Used for the comparison of names in early lists with assigned surnames. The study and the Institute article share an author and are one line of evidence for the author’s findings.
