**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The search-log structure, the
reconciled/unreconciled split, the rule to report the registers searched instead of declaring an
event unrecorded, the notary-grouped lead log and the treatment of non-Catholic settlers are
authored synthesis. The presenter supplies the naming customs, register layout, Montréal recording
practice and notarial arrangement as teaching observations. The Cahokia and Michilimackinac
register dates, the Notre-Dame de Montréal correction and the judicial tutelle series come from outside authorities.

The duplicate-register custody, its 1678 start and the 1994 transfer come from BAnQ, the Directeur
de l'état civil, FamilySearch and the Revue d'histoire de l'Amérique française (S3 to S6); the rule
to log which copy was read is authored. The Parchemin description comes from its publisher (S7);
the order of the notarial routes is authored.

**Not verified:** that entries from Green Bay, Cahokia or other western posts appear in the
Notre-Dame de Montréal register; and which copy, parish or court, the Drouin films show for a
given parish.
