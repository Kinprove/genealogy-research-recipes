## Sources

- **S1:** BYU Family History Library — Maureen Brady, [The Québecois – French-Canadian Research](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j_VNScjY6gk),
  published 2024-06-24 (session dated 20 June 2024). Used for: the Marie/Jean-Baptiste prefix,
  dit-name forms and order, St/Saint searching, reconciliation through baptism, marriage and
  burial, the combined B/M/S register, M-numbering and *défunte* cue, register start dates, the
  presenter's Montréal route for early settlements, Généalogie Québec and PRDH as listed
  resources, and notarial records arranged by notary with an Ancestry index as the lead.
- **S2:** BYU Family History Library — Maureen Brady, [Family History Research in Illinois and Wisconsin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kFxsAw8HHAs),
  published 2026-01-30 (session dated 28 January 2026). Used for: the presenter's account of
  circuit priests from Montréal serving sparsely settled posts, after-the-fact recording of
  baptisms and marriages in clusters, and burials expected at the time of death.
- **S3:** Bibliothèque et Archives nationales du Québec (BAnQ), [Reproduction of vital records (baptism, marriage, burial)](https://www.banq.qc.ca/vital-records),
  opened 2026-11-04. Used for: the two copies of each record (parish and government), the yearly
  deposit of the civil copy with the local court clerk, other authorized officials keeping
  registers, and BAnQ custody of records more than 100 years old.
- **S4:** Directeur de l'état civil du Québec, [20th anniversary](https://etatcivil.gouv.qc.ca/en/20years.html),
  opened 2026-11-04. Used for: civil status kept before 1994 by ministers of religion and
  prothonotaries, and the registrar becoming the sole officer of civil status on 1 January 1994,
  when the Civil Code of Québec came into force.
- **S5:** FamilySearch, [Canada, Quebec, Catholic Parish Registers, 1621-1979](https://familysearch.org/search/collection/1321742),
  opened 2026-11-04. Used for: the originals credited to BAnQ, and a surviving copy where its
  duplicate was destroyed.

**What is authored and what the sources supply.** The search-log structure, the
reconciled/unreconciled split, the rule to report the registers searched instead of declaring an
event unrecorded, the notary-grouped lead log and the treatment of non-Catholic settlers are
authored synthesis. The presenter supplies the naming customs, register layout, Montréal recording
practice and notarial arrangement as teaching observations. The Cahokia and Michilimackinac
register dates, the Notre-Dame de Montréal correction and the judicial tutelle series come from outside authorities.

The duplicate-register custody and the 1994 transfer come from BAnQ, the Directeur de l'état civil
and FamilySearch (S3 to S5); the rule to log which copy was read is authored.

**Not verified:** that entries from Green Bay, Cahokia or other western posts appear in the
Notre-Dame de Montréal register; and which copy, parish or court, the Drouin films show for a
given parish.
