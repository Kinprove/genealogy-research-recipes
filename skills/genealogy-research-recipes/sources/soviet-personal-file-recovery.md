**What is authored and what the sources supply.** These parts are authored synthesis: the inventory of subject, spouse and descendants; the one-row-per-locator register; the title-only versus inspected marking; the ranking rule for estimate mismatches; and the self-report treatment. Every custodian route, locator, access condition and file description in S1–S13 comes from forum participants' posts and applies only to the archive, region and years they name. The exceptions are the fond descriptions and the retention rule checked on 2026-10-21 against archive catalogues and reference pages: spbarchives.ru, the UrFU idun.urfu.ru records, the RGASPI catalogue, narb.by, a published journal article on the Samara university fond, the EHRI portal and a summary of Federal Law 125-FZ, Art. 22.1. The facts in the household-book, maternal-award, passport, ZAGS and ДРАЦС, probate-register and archive-access rules were checked on 2026-11-02 against the archive pages, legal texts and official portals in S14–S32. The request wording and the kinship-chain rule are method guidance, not observed archive behaviour.

Several of the S2 and S9 routes come from a few experienced participants, and the Ural list extracts come mostly from one contributor's database excerpts. Agreement among them is therefore not independent confirmation. Comintern files (RGASPI f. 495) are not covered by the cited sources.
